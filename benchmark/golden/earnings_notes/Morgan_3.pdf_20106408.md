# 4Q23 Pre-Announcements: Major Deterioration

4Q23 alerts have set a new record low since 2020 (net -4.2% for MSCI China since 2018 vs. net -14.2% for A-shares). Deterioration is broad-based across sectors and company size, with relative resilience skewed towards larger caps. Names to own/avoid are listed based on earnings visibility.

As of February 5, 2024, 2,798 A-share companies (\~54% of all A-share listings, \~42% by market cap) had released 4Q23 pre-announcements, with a major deterioration compared to previous quarters:

• 4Q23 A-shares pre-announcements aggregated a net -14.2% (by number of companies), a significant decrease vs 2Q23 (net -4.2%).

• Companies across large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap have all seen significant deterioration and recorded the worst pre-announcement results since 1Q20 ( Exhibit 4 ). By number of companies, 22 out of 24 industry groups saw net negative alerts: All industry groups except Autos had more negative than positive alerts ( Exhibit 5 ).

Similar deterioration was observed for MSCI China universe (based on 258 alerts, representing 34% by number of companies and 11% by market cap).

• MSCI China posted a net negative of 4.2% in pre-announcements by number of companies, the worst since we launched the earnings analysis in 2Q18: We saw a continuous drop in the percentage of net positive alerts from 4Q21 onwards and the trend entered negative territory in 4Q23 (Exhibit 2).

• Only 3 out of 11 sectors had net positive alerts. Consumer Discretionary and Healthcare have the highest net positive alerts delivery. Materials and Real Estate have the most net negative alerts ( Exhibit 10 ).

2023 consensus earnings continued to be revised down between Nov 30, 2023 and Feb 5, 2024, a major headwind in 1Q24 as we have been highlighting: We note 336 MSCI China constituents with downward revisions to 2023 consensus EPS estimates, and 102 constituents with upward revisions larger than 1% during the period, suggesting, net net, 32% of the index universe with downward revisions by number of companies. By index weight, companies with downward revisions outweighed those with upward revisions by 10ppt.

# Earnings Estimate Revisions (November 30, 2023-February 5, 2024): Broad-Based Reductions In 2023 Consensus Forecasts Across Industry Groups

As of February 5, 2023, 438 companies, representing 44% of MSCI China by index weight, have had consensus earnings estimates raised or lowered by over 1% during the assessment period (November 30, 2023 through February 5, 2024). We have analyzed analysts' earnings estimate revision trajectories by industry group during the period in terms of both number of companies and index weight. We run the analysis based on consensus data.

## Key observations based on consensus data:

1. Overall, net 32% of MSCI China by number of companies saw their consensus 2023 EPS revised down during the assessment period, a worsening from 27% in our 3Q23 analysis: Consensus 2023 EPS of 102 MSCI China constituents were raised by more than 1% since end-November 2023, while those of 336 constituents were lowered during the same period.

2. If measured by index weight, the percentage with downward revisions outweighed that with upward revisions by 10ppt during November 30, 2023, to February 5, 2024: In all, 17% of MSCI China by index weight saw their 2023 consensus earnings rise during the period vs. 27% with a decrease.

3. Sectors with net increases: Commercial & Professional Services is the only industry group that showed net rises by both number of companies and index weight in 2023 consensus estimates. Automobiles, Consumer Discretionary, Tech Hardware, Media & Entertainment, Banks, and Software had positive revisions by index weight ( Exhibit 13 & Exhibit 14 ).

4. Sectors with net decreases: In terms of number of companies, the majority of industry groups (21 out of the total 24) showed net declines in 2023 consensus (i.e., number of downwards revisions outweighs number of upwards revisions in % terms), led by Insurance, Transportation and Healthcare. If measured by index weight, 16 out of 24 industry groups had net negative revisions, led by Insurance, Transportation and Real Estate Management & Development ( Exhibit 13 & Exhibit 14 ).

# 1Q24 Pre-announcements: Similar Pattern to 1Q/3Q with Limited Indicative Power

1Q24 A-share pre-announcements are net positive, a rebound from 4Q23, but their indicative power may be limited due to small sample size.

As of April 26, 2024, \~200 A-share companies (\~4% of all A-share listings) have released 1Q24 pre-announcements vs. \~300 in 1Q23 and 3Q23, due to a disclosure rule change that became effective in 1Q22 and makes Q1 and Q3 preannouncements voluntary for A-shares.

Net 2.2% of A-shares (by number of companies) posted positive preannouncements, a notable improvement vs. 4Q23 (-14.5%), while largely the same vs. 3Q23 and 1Q23 (2.7% and 2.1%, respectively). However, the indicative power for 1Q23 earnings delivery may be limited due to its voluntary nature. In 3Q23, A-share pre-announcements were net 2.7% positive ( Exhibit 1 ), but the actual earnings delivery greatly disappointed.

Hong Kong-listed companies are only required to report semi-annually, and US-listed Chinese companies are not subject to pre-announcement requirements. We provide MSCI China constituents' 1Q24 pre-announcements in the Appendix .

The 2024 consensus earnings estimate revision trend has shown early signs of stabilization, with a skew towards large-cap: Measured by index weight, a net 1.5% of MSCI China saw 2024 consensus earnings estimates revised up during February 28 - April 26, 2024. If measured by number of companies, a net 46% of MSCI China constituents saw 2024 consensus earnings estimates revised down during the period. This implies that positive revisions are concentrated in large-caps while small- and mid-caps are still experiencing downward revisions. By sector, Consumer Services saw net upward revisions by both number of companies and index weight during Feb 28 - April 26.

We continue to remain cautious on the MSCI China/China A earnings estimate revision breadth trend before we see any meaningful turning point in macro data and corporate guidance.

# Earnings Estimate Revisions (February 28, 2024- April 26, 2024): Continued Broad-based Reductions in 2024 Consensus Forecasts across Industry Groups

As of April 26, 2024, 521 companies, representing 74% of MSCI China by number of companies, have had consensus earnings estimates raised or lowered >1% during the assessment period (February 28, 2024 - April 26, 2024). We have analyzed analysts' earnings estimate revision trajectories by industry group during the period in terms of both number of companies and index weight. We run the analysis based on consensus data.

## Key observations based on consensus data:

1. If measured by index weight, a net 1.5% of MSCI China saw 2024 consensus earnings estimates revised up during February 28 - April 26, 2024: 43% of MSCI China by index weight saw their 2024 consensus earnings estimates rise during the period vs 41% with a decrease ( Exhibit 2 ).

2. If measured by number of companies, a net 46% of MSCI China constituents saw 2024 consensus earnings estimates revised down during February 28 - April 26, 14ppt down from the 32% net negative in our 4Q23 analysis: Consensus 2024 EPS estimates of 120 MSCI China constituents were raised by more than 1% during the period, while those of 401 constituents were lowered ( Exhibit 3 ).

3. Upward 2024 consensus earnings estimate revision during the period is therefore skewed towards large-caps.

4. Sectors with net increases: Consumer Services showed net rises in 2024 consensus estimates, measured by both number of companies and index weight. Media & Entertainment, Telecommunications, Consumer Durables & Apparel, and Materials led positive revisions by index weight.

5. Sectors with net decreases: Measured by number of companies, the majority of industry groups (20 out of a total of 25) showed net declines in 2024 consensus estimates (i.e., number of downward estimate revisions outweighs number of upward revisions in percentage terms), led by Consumer Retailing, Real Estate, Utilities and Financial Services. Measured by index weight, 17 out of 25 industry groups had net negative revisions, led by Consumer Retailing, Real Estate, Financial Services and Insurance.

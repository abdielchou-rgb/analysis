Summary

Investment Highlights:

2023 net profit attributable to shareholders reached RMB 558 million, surpassing the profit alert. Revenue grew by 7.67% YoY to RMB 3.91 billion, with net profit attributable to shareholders increasing by 14.21% YoY, exceeding the profit alert by RMB 4 million. Recurring NPAtS was RMB 533 million, up 12.74% YoY, outperforming the profit alert by RMB 12 million. GPM rose to 40.92%, and NPAtS margin increased to 14.26%, with recurring NPAtS margin at 13.65%. Expense ratios for sales, management, R&D, and finance were 9.08%, 10.42%, 4.05%, and 0.42% respectively, totaling a 1.32pct increase YoY.

Revenue growth hit a six-quarter high in Q4. Q4 revenue reached RMB 1 billion, up 23.77% YoY, marking the highest growth since Q2 2022. Core categories, zippers and buttons, saw restorative growth, with sales volumes of 500 million meters and 8.9 billion pieces, up 5.67% and 4.63% YoY respectively. H2 saw significant improvements, with zipper revenue up 17.3% and button revenue up 14.3%. Capacity utilization reached 66.81%, with Bangladesh at 50.60%, driving GPM improvements.

The Company’s competitiveness continues to strengthen, likely increasing market share. Q4 retail growth in domestic apparel outpaced the Company’s revenue growth, indicating potential for continued market share gains.

The Company remains committed to transformation and smart manufacturing, focusing on global expansion and digitalization. Despite challenging dynamics, it has maintained stable operations and is accelerating its Vietnam industrial park and enhancing the Bangladesh park’s efficiency.

Earnings Forecast and Valuation: We project revenues of RMB 4.37. 4.96, and 5.81 billion for 2024-2026, with net profits of RMB 611, 679, and 803 million respectively. Assigning a 25X PE for 2024, the target price is RMB 13.05, maintaining an ‘Outperform’ rating.

Risk Warning: Risks include order loss and capacity expansion falling short of expectations.

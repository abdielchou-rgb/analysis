# China Quantitative Strategy | Asia Pacific A-shares Alpha Guide – 1Q24

Morgan Stanley's "A-shares Alpha Guide" provides a comprehensive quarterly update of: 1) stock ideas in QuantChina Long-Only Model Stock Screen; 2) quant signals and market dynamics that we are monitoring; and 3) our latest takeaways and recommendations on the market.

## QUANTWISE[]

Market overview: The China A-shares market continued to trend lower in 4Q23, as the CSI300 and CSI500 returned -6.2% and -4.6%, respectively, in local currency. Value (e.g. Materials, Industrials) outperformed Growth (e.g., Tech Hardware, Media). Concurrently, Low Beta factor outperformed the most, but Quality, Momentum, and Large cap underperformed.

QuantChina stock screen performance and change highlights: Our screen outperformed CSI300 by +1.9% in 4Q23, rounding up outperformance in 2023 at +3.1% in total. The outperformance in 4Q23 was driven by our exposure toward Low Beta Value stocks, while our short price momentum exposure also played out. Heading into 1Q24, our screen ( Exhibit 8 ) continued to suggest exploring alpha opportunities in Industrials and Info Tech sectors ( Exhibit 4 ).

Our takeaways on A-shares in 1Q24: Our China equity strategy team remained cautious on China equity overall amid risks lingering on further earnings deterioration and a lack of near-term policy action, in their view. Still they prefer Ashares over offshore equity in the near term, given more favorable setup of upcoming catalysts.

From the Quant strategy team, we suggest two trading strategies in 1Q24:

1) Keep Shorting Price Momentum factor: The current deflation backdrop in China is likely to keep A-shares being led by Value and Reversal styles, as investors tend to take a short holding period, and prefer cheap valuation ideas to trade on reversal basis. We believe Momentum factor will keep underperforming in A-shares before any market dynamics shift. Meanwhile, the recent hedges unwinding in the onshore derivatives market also provides a near-term tailwind to favor trading strategies with short exposure on Price Momentum ( Exhibit 12 ).

2) Prefer Low over High Quality stocks: We note that Quality factor could not provide defensiveness amid the recent market drawdown. According to WIND data, onshore investors' redemptions of China domestic mutual funds were on the rise throughout 2023, and accelerated into the 4th quarter. From a quant perspective, it is a crowded trade unwinding situation, in which High Quality names or large cap industry leaders might face higher selling pressure ( Exhibit 21 ).

## A-Shares Investment Landscape Overview

Onshore mutual fund redemptions accelerated in 4Q23, resulting in some degree of crowded trades unwinding in A-shares. i.e. best Quality stocks that are commonly owned by mutual funds faced a higher selling pressure due to investors' redemptions.

Such a market backdrop lowered the alpha efficacy of the Quality factor during the quarter even if market sentiment overall was still defensively biased. From a quant perspective, we believe this backdrop will continue, and we suggest investors avoid Quality stocks in A-shares in 1Q24.

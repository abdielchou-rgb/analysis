# 4Q23 Interim Earnings Cut for MSCI China – Still Net Miss but Better than 3Q So Far

## Key Takeaways

We see divergent earnings results within MSCI China constituents so far. Interim review is based on results for 53% of MSCI China (by index weight).

MSCI China 4Q23 earnings results so far have still fallen short of consensus but are largely better than 3Q.

■ Only Consumer Discretionary and Energy companies posted net beats; remaining sectors have delivered mixed results.

Consensus earnings estimates revision breadth is still negative and its downward revision momentum of consensus earnings estimates continues to accelerate.

For MSCI China, reported earnings so far reflect an overall miss vs. consensus forecasts by number of companies (-8%), but post a beat if measured by weighted surprise (+17%): The divergence between these two measures shows the earnings strength skewed toward large caps, and is driven by strong results by Internet players such as PDD. Based on 53% of index weight reported so far, the degree of net miss by number of companies has improved quite significantly vs. 3Q23 (-30%). The net beat by weighted surprise increased meaningfully vs. 3Q23 (+6%).

In revenue terms, reported revenue has delivered in line both by number of companies (+0%) and by weighted surprise (+1%): 4Q23 revenue surprise so far is largely better than 3Q23. The results measured by number of companies are a significant improvement from the net miss reading in 3Q23 (-30%, Exhibit 7 ).

Sector observations: Energy posted a net beat with consensus by numbers of companies. Meanwhile, Consumer Discretionary has recorded a net beat by both number of companies and by weighted earnings. Select Internet players are one of the bright spots from an earnings surprise perspective so far, led by Meituan and PDD. Communication Services is the only sector that has delivered in-line results for both earnings and revenue. Healthcare, IT and Materials have posted net misses by number of companies. No sector has recorded a net miss on both counts.

# MSCI China Earnings Surprises – 4Q23 Interim Review: Still Net Miss but Better than 3Q

Note: As of March 25, 2024, 114 companies in MSCI China have reported 4Q23 results. Our analysis of MSCI China earnings surprises is based on constituent companies that have reasonable quality data for consensus estimates, comprising 100 companies in MSCI China, representing 13% of the index by number of companies and 53% by index weight.

## Key observations

1) ) At the aggregate index level, reported earnings have fallen short of consensus forecasts by number of companies (-8%) but have beaten consensus if measured by weighted surprise (+17%): See Exhibit 4 . Based on 53% of index weight reported so far, the degree of net miss by number of companies decreased vs. 3Q23 (-30%). The net beat by weighted surprise increased meaningfully vs. 3Q23 (+6%).

2) Sectors with earnings beats: We only count sectors with no less than 30% of sectorlevel index weight reporting. Energy has beaten consensus earnings estimates by number of companies. Consumer Discretionary has recorded a net beat by both number of companies and by weighted earnings.

• Based on 80% of sector-level index weight reported, Consumer Discretionary posted net betas by both number of companies and weighted surprise. Key contributors are e-commerce companies, including Meituan and Pinduoduo (PDD's strong 4Q23 results suggest sustainable GMV growth and take rate improvement at its domestic business, as well as strong momentum for Temu growth).

• Energy has beaten consensus earnings by weighted surprise, based on close to 50% of sector index weight reported so far. The results posted beat if measured by number of companies. while the results are in line if measured by weighted surprise. Sinopec is the main contributor. Sinopec lifted its payout ratio by 7ppt to 71% for 2023, despite a lower bottom line dragged by one-off items.

3) Sectors with earnings in line: We only count sectors with no less than 30% of sectorlevel index weight reporting only, Communication Services posted in-line results both by number of companies and weighted earnings surprise.

• Based on 98% of Communications Services sector index weight reported, the sector is in line with consensus earnings estimates by number of companies and weighted surprise. The biggest constituents in the sector, Tencent and Baidu, posted in line results.

4) Sectors with earnings misses: We count only sectors with no less than 30% of sectorlevel index weight reporting. Healthcare, IT and Materials have posted net misses by number of companies. No sector has recorded a net miss on both counts.

• Based on 33% of sector-level index weight reported so far, Healthcare has missed consensus earnings estimates by number of companies. The results have been in line if measured by weighted surprise.

• IT has posted a miss by number of companies, based on 43% of sector index weight reported so far. Thundersoft contributed the most to the miss, mainly owing to the increase in capitalized R&D. The results posted a beat if measured by weighted surprise. The main contributors are Hua Hong Semi (it remains a threat to other mature node foundry players) and Advanced Micro-Fabrication Equipment Inc .

• Based on 36% of sector index weight reported so far, Materials has missed consensus by number of companies. Key drags are Sinopec and Ninxia Baofeng. The results are in line if measured by weighted surprise.

Note: Companies with reported earnings and Bloomberg consensus available for analysis in Consumer Staples, Financials, Industrials, Real Estate and Utilities account for less than 30% of sector total by index weight as of March 25, 2023. Their earnings results are not discussed in the earnings beat/miss section above.

Note: As of March 25, 2024, 114 companies in MSCI China have reported 4Q23 results. Our analysis of MSCI China revenue surprises is based on non-financial constituent companies that have reasonable quality data for consensus estimates, comprising 78 companies in MSCI China, representing 12% of the index by number of companies and 51% by index weight.

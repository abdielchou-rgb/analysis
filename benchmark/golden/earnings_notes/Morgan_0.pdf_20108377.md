# 1Q24 Earnings Final Cut for MSCI China – Still Net Miss but Better than 4Q23

## Key Takeaways

A 11th consecutive quarter of earnings miss (by # of companies) but scale of misses has dropped vs. 4Q23.

一 Communication Services and Consumer Discretionary beat consensus broadly while other sectors delivered mixed results.

Consensus earnings estimate revision breadth continued its accelerated downward momentum, which has been ongoing since end February.

For MSCI China, reported earnings have missed consensus by number of companies (-19.9%), but have beaten consensus if measured by weighted surprise (+6.7%): Earnings delivery divergence is still wide this quarter due to vast majority of Internet companies posting decisive beats, such as PDD, whereas the rest are still struggling to meet market expectations. Based on 75% of index weight reported so far, the degree of net miss by number of companies has improved vs. 4Q23 (-30.2%). The net beat by weighted surprise stays on par vs. 4Q23 (+7.3%).

In revenue terms, reported revenue missed consensus by number of companies (-9.4%), also suggesting a smaller miss vs. 4Q23 (-18.0%): If measured by weighted surprise, reported revenue was in line with consensus (+1.3%) in 1Q24, similar with 4Q23 ( Exhibit 7 ).

Sector observations: Communication Services and Consumer Discretionary have recorded a net beat by both number of companies and by weighted earnings, mainly driven by the Internet players. Consumer Staples, Health Care and Industrials had divergent intra-sector earnings delivery where they beat consensus earnings by one measure. Energy, Financials, IT and Materials have recorded a net miss on both counts.

We have raised our earnings growth forecast for 2024 to reflect better-thanexpected macro stabilization trend as well as MS China economics team's recently upgraded China 2024 GDP growth forecast, and a less cautious view on USDCNY toward year-end. Earnings growth, however, will remain subpar vs. historical level under persistent deflation pressure and will not recover to double-digit (13% for MSCI China) until 2026. Therefore, we expect the downward revisions for consensus estimates to continue, and whether or not the speed of that could slow remains key to watch in the coming months. # MSCI China Earnings Surprises 1Q24 Final Cut: Narrower net miss Vs. 4Q23 By Number Of Companies

Note: As of May 23, 2024, 652 companies out of 704 in MSCI China had reported 1Q24 results. Our analysis of MSCI China earnings surprises is based on constituent companies that have reasonable quality data for consensus estimates, comprising 402 companies in MSCI China, representing 57% of the index by number of companies and 75% by index weight.

## Key observations

1) ) At the aggregate index level, reported earnings fell short of consensus forecasts by number of companies (-19.9%), but delivered a beat if measured by weighted surprise (+6.7%): See Exhibit 4 . The divergence between these two measures is driven by strong results from Communication Services and Consumer Discretionary but weakness largely elsewhere. Based on 75% of index weight reported so far, the degree of net miss by number of companies narrowed vs. 4Q23 (-30.2%). The net beat by weighted surprise stays on par vs. 4Q23 (+7.3%)..

2) Sectors with earnings beats: We only count sectors with no less than 30% of sectorlevel index weight reporting. Consumer Staples, Health Care and Industrials have beaten consensus earnings estimates either by number of companies or weighted surprise. Communication Services and Consumer Discretionary have recorded a net beat by both number of companies and by weighted earnings.

• Based on 97% of sector-level index weight reported, Communication Services posted net betas by both number of companies and weighted surprise. Key contributors are Tencent (solid games growth turnaround, robust ads growth, sustainable margin expansion), Tencent Music Entertainment (driven by strong music and ads growth, self-developed content, and leveraging minimum guarantee part of content cost) and IQIYI (driven by higher-than-expected Content Distribution and Others revenues).

• Consumer Discretionary also beat consensus earnings estimates by both number of companies and weighted surprise. Key contributors are PDD, TCOM (strong volume share gain across all segments, and cost savings/sustainable margin expansion), JD (thanks to improving operating efficiency) and GWM (on the back of good exports and Tank sales).

• Consumer Staples has beaten consensus earnings by number of companies, based on close to 46% of sector index weight reported so far. The results are in line if measured by weighted surprise. Chongqing Brew, Tsingtao Brewery, and Yonghui Superstores post slight beats,

• Health Care has beaten consensus earnings by weighted surprise, but in line if measured by number of companies. Key contributors are BGNE, BYS , and ZLAB (driven in part by healthy commercial uptake of Vyvgart).

• Industrials delivered mixed results as it beat consensus earnings by weighted surprise, but it saw a net miss against consensus earnings if measured by number of companies. Key contributors are CATL (driven by improving mix and cost

control), JD Logistics, Sunwoda Electrs and ZTO (Better service quality to lead to mix upgrades and higher pricing premiums).

3) Sectors with earnings misses: We count only sectors with no less than 30% of sectorlevel index weight reporting. Energy, Financials, IT and Materials have recorded a net miss on both counts.

• Based on 91% of sector-level index weight reported, Energy posted a double miss both by number of companies and weighted surprise. Key drags are COSL (due to a lower-than-expected utilization rate of rigs), and Yankuang (drop in domestic spot coal price, and the premium for spot coal over contract has been narrowed).

• Financials has missed consensus earnings estimates by number of companies and by weighted surprise. More banks reported modest yoy profit declines on continued revenue and PPOP pressure. NIM shows signs of stabilization and even rebounded at some banks, while asset quality is stable at most banks, which should support future revenue and profits as well as share prices. 1Q24 results continued to show weaker trend across multiple business lines at leading brokers. For Insurance, Earnings declined due to a high base, but ROE was still healthy. Key drag includes China Life Insurance (Both insurance service results and investment results saw some decline).

• IT posted a double miss both by number of companies and weighted surprise based on 72% of sector index weight reported so far. LONGi (mainly owing to inventory impairment) and Tongwei (impacted by temporarily elevated tariffs in Sichuan and lower cell capacity utilization during the PERC-TOPCon transition) contributed the most to the miss.

• Based on 73% of sector index weight reported so far, Materials has missed by both number of companies and weighted surprise. Key drags are CNBM (the price decline of major products including cement, concrete, and glass fiber, although the decline in production costs), Ganfeng Lithium (given the large high-cost inventory at end-2023 and lower investment income from associates including Yiliping Salt Lake), and CMOC (mainly due to lower copper sales volume).

Note: Companies with reported earnings and Bloomberg consensus available for analysis in Real Estate and Utilities account for less than 30% of sector total by index weight as of May 23, 2024. Their earnings results are not discussed in the earnings beat/miss section above.

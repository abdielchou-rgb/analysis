# APxJ/EM 1Q24 Results - Narrow beats vs broad misses; Consensus downgrades continue

EM and APxJ equities are delivering weighted NP beats of 7.4%/9.3%, but breadth measures remain negative at -4ppt/-5ppt. China encapsulated this trend, with some strong results lifting 1Q to an aggregate +6.5% beat, while a net 20ppt of companies missed. Consensus EPS continue to fall in EM/APxJ.

Moderate beats for EM and APxJ earnings: At \~60% of market cap reported, weighted net income has come in ahead of consensus estimates, with MSCI EM and MSCI APxJ at +7.4% and +9.3%, respectively. However, breadth measures were skewed toward misses, with net beat ratios of -4ppt for MSCI EM and -5ppt for MSCI APxJ, mainly driven by China's net miss ratio (-23ppt). Revenues were in-line, at 0.7% for MSCI EM and 1.0% for MSCI APxJ vs. consensus. These results come amidst a still sharply falling trend for consensus estimates for the 2024 and 2025 fiscal years.

APxJ showing strongest results, with LatAm and EEMEA in-line: APxJ's market cap weighted beat was driven mainly by Korea (+21%) and Taiwan (+9%). We see slightly below-consensus results in Indonesia (-2%) and Philippines (-3%). EEMEA companies have posted an aggregate small miss of -3%, driven by Saudi Arabia (-7%) and Kuwait (-4%). Results in LatAm (-0.1%) have slightly trailed consensus, mainly dragged down by Brazil (-6%).

Major earnings beats in Consumer Discretionary, Industrials and Communication Services vs. misses for Energy and Consumer Staples: Across EM sectors, sizable beats came from Consumer Discretionary (+20%), driven by Consumer Services (+74%) and Autos & Components (+33%). Oil & Gas Exploration & Production was a key source of misses, while Staples Retailing and Food and Beverage also disappointed.

Japan results saw buybacks accelerate: In aggregate, F3/24 4Q (Jan-Mar) results for Japan showed sales +6.5% YoY, delivering a 19.7% YoY increase in RP (Recurring Profit), and 9.1% YoY in NP (Net Profit). FY23 share repurchase programs reached a total value of ¥10.0trn, a record high. This momentum has continued into FY24. For more details see our Japan Strategy team's latest earnings season report.

## Japan F3/24 4Q Results Review (Preliminary)

## Key takeaways:

• Cumulative F3/24 4Q results came in at +5.5% sales, +18.1% Recurring Profit (RP), and +18.1% Net Profit (NP) across all industries.

• Within RP growth of 18.1% YoY, transport equipment and electricity/gas contributions were high, at 9.2%pt and 6.2%pt, respectively.

• F3/25 guidance calls for +2.1% sales, and profit declines of -6.5% for RP, and -8.1% for NP.

• However, companies historically tend to set weak guidance, so this comes as no surprise especially in light of the consensus.

• FY23 share repurchase programs reached a total value of ¥10.0trn, a record high. This momentum has continued into FY24.

# APxJ/EM 4Q23 Results - Solid Innings from Australia, India, Korea and Mexico

EM and APxJ earnings surprises have picked up in 4Q23 on a weighted net income basis, although breadth remains negative in APxJ and LatAM. Australia and Mexico saw beats on value and breadth, while surprise breadth was negative in Brazil and China.

Moderate beats vs. lower bar for EM and APxJ earnings: At \~50% of market cap reported, weighted net income has come in ahead of lowered consensus estimates, with MSCI EM and MSCI APxJ at +7.9% and +8.0%, respectively. However, breadth measures were skewed toward misses, with net beat ratios of -10ppt for both MSCI EM and MSCI APxJ, mainly driven by Korea's net miss ratio (-32ppt). Revenues were in-line, at 0.1% for MSCI EM and 0.4% for MSCI APxJ vs. consensus. These results come amidst a still sharply falling trend for consensus estimates for the 2023 and 2024 fiscal years.

APxJ showing strongest results, with LatAm and EEMEA in-line: APxJ's market cap weighted beat was driven mainly by Korea (31%) and India (10%). We see belowconsensus results in Taiwan (-4%) and Thailand (-8%). EEMEA companies have posted an aggregate slight beat of 2.2%, driven by UAE (19%), Hungary (13%), and Poland (7%). LatAm (-2%) results have trailed consensus, mainly dragged by Brazil (- 4%) and Peru (-9%).

Major earnings misses in Materials and Industrials vs. beats for Information Technology, Real Estate and Health Care: Across EM sectors, sizable misses came from Materials (-15%), driven by Chemicals (-27%), Construction Materials (-24%) and Paper & Forest Products (-14%). Among Information Technology, Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment was the key source of beats.

Japan buybacks accelerate: At the all-industry level, F3/24 1-3Q (Apr-Dec) results were increases (YoY) of 5.3% in sales, 19.7% in RP (Recurring Profit), and 24.2% in NP (Net Profit). Beats/misses were balanced, but the total value of buyback programs set in F3/24 was an all-time high ¥9.4trn. For more details see our Japan Strategy team's latest earnings season report.

## Key takeaways

• At the all-industry level, F3/24 1-3Q (Apr-Dec) results were increases (YoY) of 5.3% in sales, 19.7% in RP (Recurring Profit), and 24.2% in NP (Net Profit).

• While announcements confirmed healthy corporate earnings, large contributions from utilities (electricity/gas) and transport equipment industries stood out.

• The ratios of above/below consensus results were roughly the same at 50%. Multiple cases of missing consensus by firms in industries with high exposure to China.

• We forecast increases (YoY) of 4.0% in sales and 17.2% in RP in F3/24 and 2.7% in sales and 7.0% in RP in F3/25 (consensus estimates).

• Total value of buyback programs set in F3/24 was an all-time high ¥9.4trn. Despite record-high stock prices, firms are actively conducting shareholder return.

For details, please refer to our Japan Strategy team's latest earnings season report.

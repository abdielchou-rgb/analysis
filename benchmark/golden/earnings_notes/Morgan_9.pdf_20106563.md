# 3FQ24 Earnings – Final Cut: Another Good Quarter

Earnings growth trend remained intact in 3FQ24 as margins expanded for the 4th consecutive quarter. Revenue and Earnings growth beat our analysts' expectations albeit the breadth in earnings beat was at a 5-year low. Broad Market earnings outperformed the narrow market.

Morgan Stanley coverage: For F3Q24, companies under Morgan Stanley's coverage reported revenue, EBITDA, PBT and net profit growth of 5%, 13%, 25% and 23%, respectively, vs. our analysts' expectations of 2%, 12%, 18% and 20%. EBITDA margin expanded for the 4th quarter running. Ex-Energy, revenue and net profit growth stood at 9% and 22%, respectively, in-line with our analysts' expectations. The earnings beat ratio fell to 5-year lows, at 42%, while relative stock outperformance post-earnings announcements was up QoQ, at 46% ( Exhibit14 ).

Marginal beat on earnings: Sensex reported revenue growth of 8% YoY, in-line with our analysts' expectations while Nifty reported revenue growth of 6% YoY, a beat of 2ppt. Net profit growth for Sensex and Nifty stood at 15% and 13%, beating our analysts' expectations by 2ppt and 1ppt, respectively. Broad market revenue and net profit growth were 5% and 26% YoY, respectively, with margin expansion of 195bps ( Exhibit 30 ).

Consumer Discretionary led the performance: Earnings growth was positive for nine out of ten sectors covered. Materials (ex-chemicals) and Consumer Discretionary reported the best earnings growth, while Technology reported a decline in earnings. Revenue growth was positive for nine sectors with Consumer Discretionary and Industrials reporting the fastest growth while Materials was the only sector to report negative growth. Consumer Discretionary and Energy had the biggest earnings beats vs. our analysts' expectations. EBITDA margin beat our analysts' expectations in seven sectors.

Strong breadth continues: Share of top companies in net profit remained at cyclelow levels for a tenth consecutive quarter ( Exhibit 3 ). Around 60% of our coverage universe reported double-digit profit growth vs 63% in the previous quarter ( Exhibit22 ).

Earnings expectations: Morgan Stanley's analysts and consensus both forecast F2024 Sensex earnings growth of 20%, respectively, which is 2ppt lower vs our topdown estimate of 22% ( Exhibit 36 ). The 3MMA consensus earnings estimate revision breadth fell for FY1 but rose FY2, standing at -1.4% and 0.5% vs. -0.4% and 0.0%, respectively, as of the end of Dec-23 ( Exhibit7 ). Consumer Discretionary and Materials have been subject to the most positive and negative revisions in F2024 earnings growth estimates, respectively ( Exhibit37 ).

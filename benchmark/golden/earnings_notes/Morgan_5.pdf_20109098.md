This quarterly report updates our proprietary "Enhanced Dividend Screen" and "Dividend At Risk Screen," reflecting the most- and least-preferred dividend stocks in APxJ per both Morgan Stanley quant and industry analysts' opinions.

## QUANTWISE 圖

2Q24 dividend strategy recap: MSCI APxJ High Dividend Index +6.01% is largely flat with MSCI APxJ +6.35% in 2Q24. Our Enhanced Dividend Model realized marketneutral L/S alpha of +8.31% during the quarter ( Exhibit 2 ). Market is currently pricing a peak rate environment while volatility in UST bond yields and Asia/EM market continued to favor Quality and Dividend stocks to outperform. The cautious investors' sentiment also drives allocation toward Quality Dividend stocks.

Our key message to dividend investors in 3Q24 – tailwind on dividend stocks remains: Market has been pricing peak rate environment. Our Morgan Stanley market implied terminal rate (MSTERM) headed down from 5.35% in mid-June to 5.3% at the latest check. With more disinflation evidence, our house view is that UST bond yields will moderate from current levels toward year-end. We still prefer Dividend stocks given cautious risk sentiment in Asia/EM and see support in valuations for quality dividend stocks due to their defensiveness. Investor appetite on corporate reform and shareholder return theme in Asia/EM also remain high, which are likely to benefit dividend stocks.

Top Quantamental ideas for 3Q24: Our selection process includes two steps: 1) We leverage our quantitative model to identify Dividend stocks with balance sheet resilience and business stability over the long term. 2) We then collaborate with our bottom-up industry and stock analysts to finalize ideas where both quantitative and fundamental analyses align. ## Methodology: A Two-page Overview

What we try to achieve: The investment universe is defined as index constituents in MSCI Asia Pacific ex Japan, while our performance evaluation is benchmarked to MSCI AC Asia Pacific ex Japan High Dividend Index. The goal is to deliver relative return with:

1. Similar or higher dividend yield than benchmark;

2. Lower dividend shortfall risk, and preserving long-term capital gains;

3. Low turnover of ideas.

What we have done differently: We differentiate this analysis by taking the following three approaches to enhance the search of favored dividend stocks:

1. The importance of dividend policy is emphasized by a low payout ratio in parallel with stable dividend growth over the past six years.

2. Short-term liquidity is measured by FCF generating ability and low accruals on balance sheet.

3. Earnings stability is evaluated on both historical and forward-looking bases.

Use fundamental factors to screen dividend stocks: The selection of factors was based on fundamental considerations with quantitative insights to strengthen the signal.

We divide the screening factors into three batches:

• Mandatory Factor – High Historical Dividend Yield

• Dividend Policy-related Factors – High 6Y Dividend Growth, Low Variation of 6Y Dividend Growth, High FCF Yield, Low Payout Ratio

• Business Resilience Factors – Low Leverage, Low Accruals, High Earning Stability, Positive 3M Earnings Revisions

Quantamental Screening process: This is comprised of two major steps – Quantitative screening and Fundamental Insights override.

For the Quantitative screening, per our usual practice of keeping screening rules simple and transparent, equal-weighting is applied among descriptors and parameters. Exhibit 6 summarizes the finalized composition of our screening rules for high Dividend stocks in Asia Pacific ex Japan.

For Fundamental Insights override, we conduct internal research to seek forward-looking opinions from our sector analysts for the risk of dividend cuts, payout ratio outlook, overall valuation attractiveness, and the strategist's view on the market exposure. Stocks with negative feedback in any one of these parameters are excluded from the final screen. Exhibit 7 shows the complete process.

More details can be referred to in the launch report of our dividend strategy - Asia Quantitative Strategy: All About Dividend Investing (17 May 2020)

Sector allocation of our methodology versus the conventional approach: Conventional dividend-seeking investors in Asia tend to explore opportunities in Financials, Utilities and Real Estate, and these are our top three sectors in the final screen too.

From an active weighting perspective, our screen differentiates from the MSCI High Dividend Index by:

• Overweighting Real Estate: However, our choices within the sector are those property developers with quality-growth profiles rather than simply buying REITs, because the high payout ratios for REITs exposes them to larger cyclical risk and thus more uncertainty on the dividend stream.

• Underweighting Cyclicals: High dividend stocks in cyclical sectors are subject to higher risk of dividend cuts during downturns, which is not preferred per our selection principles. Dividend certainty and balance sheet wellbeing are our top criteria.

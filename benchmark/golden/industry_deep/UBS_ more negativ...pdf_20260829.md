# China Industrials

# Industrial insights (June) — more negative signs vs. May

## More negative signals observed in June

We present key industrial data in our monthly China Industrials Chartbook. Positives for June include positive YoY growth in excavator sales, still-resilient container port throughput growth, and elevated container shipping freight rates. On the negative side, expressway truck traffic growth turned negative YoY, cement shipments remained weak together with a deceleration of infrastructure FAI growth, HDT sales dropped significantly YoY, and automation recovery remained slow.

## Limited improvement in domestic travel demand; freight growth mixed

Per Flight Master, June MTD domestic flight volume recovered to 107% of the 2019 level, similar with May. MTD int'l flight volume recovery was \~73% of the 2019 level, with Japan/Korea routes back to 76%/81%. UBS Evidence Lab's China Railway Traffic Monitor (> Access Dataset) revealed a 9% YoY increase of national railway service numbers in June, while ticket prices remain weak. China Freight Monitor showed mixed signals for June MTD, with national railway freight volume up 7% YoY, expressway truck traffic down 2% YoY, and container throughput at ports rising 11% YoY.

## Deceleration of infrastructure FAI YoY, divergent excavator and HDT growth

May infrastructure FAI YoY (excluding utilities) decelerated to 4.9% from 6.0% in 4M24; while cement shipments remained well below the past few years. For June, our channel check indicates domestic excavator sales grew 10% YoY (mainly driven by small-sized excavators) but fell 20% MoM. Some OEMs attributed this to machines replacing humans in rural areas and some infra projects breaking ground as special central bonds are in place. We forecast June HDT sales to drop 10% MoM and 20% YoY.

## Automation: worse solar demand, low season for traditional downstream

We find automation demand from solar downstream continued to worsen from late May into June, which we think is due to industry oversupply and regulations calling for more disciplined capacity expansion. In response, we note that automation companies have expanded product offerings and solutions to partially mitigate the decline in demand from solar downstream. Together with the slow season for the traditional downstream, we think automation demand growth will moderate YoY in June. Meanwhile, humanoid robot sector share prices corrected as Tesla's plan to deploy more than1,000 Optimus in its factory missed market expectations.

## Industry channels/expert check

## Disappointing H1 demand; expect implementation of trade-in policy in H2

We visited the HDT industry chain and gathered several key takeaways: 1) Domestic HDT demand was rather muted during the low season in June. Dealers indicated order intake shrank 20% MoM, by more than usual for the season. The 618 Shopping Festival’s boost to logistics-related HDT demand was limited. 2) HDT exports remained stable, with flattish to slightly higher YoY growth, despite a significant slowdown, exceeding what we expected in early 2024. 3) Some dealers expect some provinces and municipalities to unveil policies to phase out China IV vehicles in H224, including mobility restrictions or subsidies for the trade-in of outdated vehicles, which may drive growth in HDT replacement demand in H224. Hence, we forecast HDT sales of 70k units in June, down 10% MoM and down nearly 20% YoY. This implies H124 sales of 500k units, up 3% YoY, below the full-year forecast of 15% growth that we made earlier in the year.

## June domestic construction machinery – our corporate and dealer checks

Domestic excavator sales sustained momentum in June. Our construction machinery channel check with dealers and OEMs found: 1) some dealers indicate domestic excavator sales volume amounted to 7k units in June, implying +15% YoY and -20% MoM. Some dealers partially attribute the YoY increase to the dealers’ short-term behaviour as they seek to obtain higher rebates from OEMs by selling more units before end-June. 2) similar to last month, demand mainly came from small-size excavators. Some attribute this to machines replacing humans in rural areas and to some infra projects breaking ground as special central bonds are in place; 3) most dealers say real demand is still weak as the property downstream has yet to improve, and demand from the infrastructure downstream is comprised more of projects under construction instead of new projects; 4) prices remained overall stable in June; and 5) cash collection saw marginal improvement in June but overall is still relatively poor.

Domestic excavator sales to come to c.7k units in June, implying a 15% YoY rise and roughly 20% MoM drop

## Methodology

## China Railway Traffic Monitor

UBS Evidence Lab collects time series data on passenger train departures from the official Chinarailway ticket booking website to provide a comprehensive view on train traffic volume for selected routes. This report leverages the following UBS Evidence Lab asset: China Railway Traffic Monitor. Each data harvest is tested for accuracy and completeness using a proprietary set of data quality checks. The data cleansing processes includes redundant record removal, data normalisation, unit conversions, and taxonomy verification. Passenger train traffic data is collected daily for key departure points—in the case of larger cities with multiple railway terminals, the data is collected for each station and then merged into a city-level measurement. Daily train counts can be also aggregated into weekly or monthly statistics.

## China Railway Utilization Monitor

UBS Evidence Lab tracks ticket price and ticket availability status of high-speed passenger train traffic for the busiest railway routes in China. The asset provides weekly information on the ticket availability and ticket price of high-speed passenger trains. UBS Evidence Lab collects time series data on ticket price and ticket availability status for high-speed passenger trains from the official China railway ticket booking website to provide a unique view on train traffic utilization for target routes. This report leverages the following UBS Evidence Lab asset: China Railway Utilization Monitor. Each data harvest is tested for accuracy and completeness using a proprietary set of data quality checks. The data cleansing process includes redundant record removal, data normalization, unit conversions, and taxonomy verification. High Speed passenger train traffic data is collected daily for target routes. The data is collected for each route and aggregated into weekly statistics.

## The Global Maritime Trade Monitor

This framework uses real-time AIS data on more than 35,200 container, dry bulk and liquid tanker ships. Monitoring shipping activity can give an advanced read on economic activity, company-specific sales/purchase volumes and real asset utilisation rates.

# 4Q Preview: Improving EPS Outlook

Expect better 4Q earnings given market rally into year-end boosting fund NAVs that drives our 5% upward EPS revision. Higher AUM levels support improved 2024-25 outlook with 12%/8% upward EPS revision. We also see scope for a better flow outlook in '24 as we anticipate gradual risk re-engagement.

## Key Takeaways:

• Better market performance into year-end relative to the soft Oct start supports upward revisions though not enough to drive seq. EPS growth. Expect flows remain pressured by weak risk appetite and seasonally softer MF flows that likely see \~3% outflows (vs \~1% outflows in 3Q23).

• Looking to 2024, we anticipate risk re-engagement given views of our economists for falling inflation, rate cuts from June and a soft landing. In such a backdrop, we expect large piles of cash on the sidelines to rotate into fixed income and would also expect improved equity flows over time.

• On 4Q calls, we'll be listening for mgmt commentary around; 1) client risk re-engagement trends, particularly Jan YTD and organic growth outlook, 2) expense outlook and growth initiatives, 3) capital mgmt priorities including scope for strategic actions/M&A to drive improved growth prospects.

• The group rallied 21% over the past 3-months (vs. S&P up 11%), but declined -3% YTD (vs. S&P -0.6% YTD) vs. our broader coverage -4% YTD. Valuations at 9.3x EV/EBITDA are roughly in line with 9.4x EV/EBITDA that the group historically traded on avg, and well above prior troughs at \~6x.

• We raise 4Q estimates by +5% on an average basis, and we're -0.4% below cons; We're most below the street in 4Q for BSIG and TROW, by -6.5% and - 4.8% and most above for VCTR and BEN, by +4.5% and +2.3%

• Our 2024-25 EPS estimates rise +11.7%/+7.6% on average basis on higher AUM levels given the recent broad based uplift in financial markets. 2024 ests increase most for IVZ (and tactically more positive) and least for WT.

• Remain cautious on the outlook for the Trads but have selectively added risk as we see greater prospects for a soft landing and improved macro outlook that can support flows and fund NAVs. However we see structural challenges to organic growth and fee rates (given evolving customer preferences and performance challenges), and expenses (given need to invest in the business linked to regulation, data and technology and to drive growth) that weigh on earnings power and profitability.

• Remain Overweight BLK and recently elevated to Top Pick, given exposure to secular growth zones (privates, EM, solutions, FI, Aladdin tech+AI efficiencies) and best mix of product, distribution breadth and scale to support organic growth and better than expected earnings power.

Executive Summary

Uplift in financial markets towards the end of 4Q23 and higher EOP and average AUM relative to prior forecasts drives near-term upwards earnings revisions. We revise longterm AUM levels +5.3% higher on average for 4Q23e and +5.8% higher for 2024e as a result of higher equity market indices with S&P 500 +11.7% and MSCI World +10% for the quarter. This leads us to revise net revenue expectations+7.7%/3.5% higher for '24/'25, and our EBITDA estimates are up +10%/6.6% on avg in '24/'25. Flows are still muted as we see annualized organic decay of -2.8% in 4Q, which reflects a deterioration from -1.3% ann in 3Q on avg (excl WT). Into the 4Q print, flows are largely de-risked as most mgrs have already reported AUM and flows for the quarter (see our Dec monthly AUM notes here and here). For 2024e however, we look for organic growth of 0.4% on average (an improvement from -0.3% in 2023e), accelerating to +1.1% in 2025e as the market settles and investors move money back into risk assets from cash. Asset managers who best position themselves to capture money in motion once it comes off the sidelines could experience upwards revisions in forward year earnings.

While we retain our cautious stance on the traditional asset mgrs given structural challenges, we have selectively added risk (recently adding BLK to to Top Pick) given greater prospects for a soft landing and improved macro outlook that can support flows and fund NAVs. Market volatility along with structural challenges is driving firms to revisit the cost base in search of greater efficiencies. We see a number of firms already announcing re-organization to drive cost saves. Expense initiatives to reduce fixed/variable operating costs and reorganization to realize structural efficiencies are among what mgmt teams can control to support margins but also free-up resources for investment toward growth initiatives.

This leads to our selective preference for Top Pick BLK (OW). We see BLK as best positioned to capture flows into fixed income and continue to deliver positive organic growth given exposure to attractive opportunities across fixed income, thematics/impact, alternatives, and ETFs. Coming out of 4Q23 Earnings (see our takeaways here), with their newly announced transformational acquisition of GIP as well as their recently announced structural reorganization to prioritize investment in areas of growth and positioning ahead of secular trends (i.e. infrastructure and the ETF revolution), we see BLK as having the ability to successfully navigate through a volatile macro backdrop, take share and deliver organic growth, harness Aladdin tech capabilities to unlock growth, and act strategically from a position of strength. Our \$1,000 PT offers 27% upside, and reflects a 2x premium vs the S&P \~18x P/E on '25, which we feel as warranted given expectations for double digits earnings growth (\~15% CAGR across '23-'25) and high quality compounding franchise.

## 4Q23 Preview

Earnings kicked off for the traditional asset mgrs with BLK on January 12 and next up is IVZ on January 23rd. Into the 4Q print, we focus on 1) organic growth trends as investors begin to re-risk and move money out of cash and into risk assets 2) outlook and ability to execute on expense efficiency initiatives to better protect operating margins and free up resources for growth initiatives, and 3) capital management priorities (including strategic M&A, debt paydown and capital return). We're listening for color

around the institutional pipeline, sales trends, new product launches, distribution initiatives, and management confidence to improve organic growth. We'll look out for updates on key themes that are best positioned to capture flows such as fixed income, private markets, infrastructure, EM, solutions, and thematics/impact. Reinvestment for growth, incremental margin expansion, and realization of expense efficiencies continue to be top of mind even as market outlook improves. Further, we continue to listen for updates on integration and execution progress with recent acquisitions (BEN/Putnam, TROW/Oak Hill, VRTS/AlphaSimplex). We're also keen to hear any updates around pipeline for new acquisitions.

Across our coverage, we see BLK and IVZ as best positioned in the current market environment given strong ETF franchises that are gathering assets. BEN has scope for improvement given scope for harnessing greater efficiencies and synergies from a number of acquisitions.

• BLK has scope for continued organic growth and margin expansion, supported by the firm's scale, diversification, disciplined investments made across market cycles and efficient operations with a growing focus on expenses. We also see inorganic growth as a potential to drive upside, as BLK capitalizes on secular trends such as the growing supply/demand imbalance in infrastructure investing through their recently announced transformational acquisition of GIP (set to close in 3Q24).We believe BLK's breadth of capabilities, unrivaled distribution prowess and top brand uniquely position the firm to drive better earnings than the market expects and higher earnings than is priced into the shares.

• IVZ announced a simplification initiative earlier in 2023 to drive revenue acceleration as well as expense savings that could lead to sustainable profit growth and margin expansion into 2024. IVZ has also identified \$50m of annual run rate expense savings to be realized by the beginning of 2024 and we'll listen for any additional color and progress on the simplification initiatives. If these expense initiatives can help IVZ tightly manage costs into 2024, with lower comp ratio y/y and modest growth in non-comp expense, we could see meaningful upward EPS revisions. Notably, IVZ reported +2.1% annualized LT inflows for 4Q23 demonstrating ability to capture money in motion and setting IVZ up as a beneficiary for investor re-risking on the horizon.

• BEN appears as if it would benefit most from rotation into fixed income given that the asset class represents \~40% of their AUM (as of 9/30/2023), but investment performance overall remains challenged which could challenge the flow outlook. However, BEN has expense initiatives in place, mentioning the opportunity to be more efficient in terms of the organization as well as opportunity to modernize, reduce capex, and scale with the right partners. Notably, BEN closed on their acquisition of Putnam in 1Q24 and we'll listen for any update on deal benefits and synergies, including extent of mandate wins from their strategic partnership with Great West Lifeco.

We revise estimates for the Trads upwards by +11.7% in 2024 and +7.6% in 2025 on average, to reflect mark to market following the increase in 4Q financial asset values, with S&P up +11.7% and Barclays US agg up +6.2% in 4Q, Our estimates rise most for IVZ in 2024 by +17.7% on higher management fees and service and distribution fees and least for WT by +8.3% on softer fixed income flows. For the group, we increase price targets by +8% on average, with Adjusted EBITDA that's +10%/+6.6% higher in 2024/25e. Our '25 target EV/EBITDA multiple is unchanged across the group. On a median basis, we see -2% downside to our new price targets.

4Q EPS estimates are up +6.8% on a median basis, given +5.3% higher AUM levels on the back of recent market uplift. Estimates for the quarter increase the most for WT (by +13.3% as a result of higher average AUM in 4Q23) and decrease only for BSIG (by -7.9%, a result of higher operating expenses). We're -0.4% below consensus for the quarter on average (-0.5% on a median basis). For the quarter, We're most below on BSIG (-6.5% driven by lower performance fees and lower average AUM) and most above on VCTR (+4.5% driven by higher management fees as a result of higher average AUM).

State of Play in 4Q: Equity markets for 4Q23 show meaningful uplift coming out of 3Q23 with the S&P up +11.7%. Fixed income markets also saw uplift, with the Barclays Global Agg up +5.1% in 4Q23. As a result, we look for median market appreciation of +7.4%. We see dampened flow trajectory as we look for -2.8% ann flow rate in 3Q, a decrease from the -1.3% ann flow rate in 2Q on avg ex WT. We expect net revenues for the group to increase by +2.3% q/q (1.7% on a median basis) with elevated expenses for -3.6% q/q decay in operating income, and largely stable operating margins at 33% on average.

Macro backdrop in 4Q23: S&P up +11.7% sequentially on a point-to-point basis and up-15% on an avg daily basis from 3Q23; Russell 2000 up +13.6%, MSCI AC World up +4.98%, STOXX Europe 600 up +7.8%, MSCI EM up +5.6%. Credit: US Agg up +6.2%, BOFA ML US High Yield Master II Constrained up +5.8%, and S&P Leveraged Loan down -3.6%.

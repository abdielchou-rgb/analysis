## U.S. Return Decomposition - January 2024

Returns have swung meaningfully over the past 2 years, with Growth and Value rotating in and out of favor. In 2022, for example, the S&P 500 returned -18.1% (including dividends), with Growth down -29.1%. By contrast, in 2023, the market returned +26.3%, +42.7% for Growth. Taken together, over the past 2 years, the S&P 500 returned a paltry +3.4% (+1.7% annualized).

The source of returns has also been quite disparate between styles, sector and market cap, with roughly half of Large Cap Growth's 2023 returns attributable to earnings, but virtually none for Value and Small Caps.

The largest seven companies returned +76.2% in 2023, a marked reversal from their 2022 performance of -39.7%. While virtually all of their 2022 underperformance came from multiple contraction, the group's 2023 outperformance was largely split between earnings growth and increasing valuations.

# US Equity Derivatives Strategy All the small things: Does this small cap 2.0 rally have legs?

We didn't start the fire: Small cap resurgence amidst Fed pivot and US electiondriven rotation [Figures 1-9]

We noted the swift rotation into small caps post the cooling June CPI print last week. We have seen this story before, most notably when the Fed 'pivot' in November-December 2023 drove similarly lofty price action. The confluence of a stronger probability of a goldilocks environment, rate cuts repriced mitigating any potential slowdown in growth, and the Trump 2.0 trade has spurred a resurgence rally in small caps, amongst other lower quality pockets (e.g. Regional Banks). This follows a slug of YTD underperformance in RTY relative to major index peers, particularly NDX. The sharp rally has again been accompanied by notable exuberance in call option activity, eclipsing even that witnessed through 2023 year-end.

Our US Equity Strategy team suggests the rotation into small caps could continue, predicated on slowing megacap Tech earnings. News regarding potential curbing of chip exports supporting the recent rotation out of Growth. While the momentum trade has started to roll over, we do not see evidence of a full-on momentum unwind just yet. Macro-wise, for this rotation to be real and sustainable, we think you would need to see:

1. NFPs sustain modestly over the next few months,

2. Economic growth similar to above,

3. Further CPI cooling – note Alan Detmeister (US Inflation Economist) expects an uptick into the fall,

4. Rate cuts priced post-June CPI to stay priced – the chief reason RTY exuberance faded earlier this year was because the fully-priced Fed 'pivot' was swiftly priced out following a string of data suggesting ‘higher for longer’,

5. A peaking of AI-centric earnings tailwind, and

6. Hard evidence of investors willing to reallocate capital towards small caps à la the post-Covid 2020-21 era – this was clearly not the case late last year, with investors preferring to rent upside via call optionality instead

Same old story: Room to run, but the fundamental backdrop remains concerning [Figures 10-20]

We think it is unsurprising to see the lower quality pockets of the market having rallied sharply – Those due for a catch up are now catching up amidst the risk-on, in essence. Fundamentally speaking, however, the low quality aspects exhibited by small caps continue to hold true:

1. Over one-third of RTY corporates remain unprofitable,

2. RTY earnings forecast momentum has slowed considerably relative to SPX in recent quarters,

3. Equity risk premia for RTY is close to all-time lows,

4. RTY corporates are more levered relative to their higher quality counterparts, and ability to service this debt has fallen, and

5. >45% of RTY aggregate corporate debt consists of floating rate paper (vs. SPX
10%), though this could act as a windfall if interest rates continue to move lower

## Success isn't owned, it's rented: Reloading on IWM calls to rent upside [Figures 21-25]

One of our highest conviction right tail trades for 2024 was buying calls in US small caps. Sticking with our view from the outlook, we prefer to rent upside in small caps via IWM call optionality until we see more evidence of investors willing to re-allocate towards the space. IWM calls screen as cheap despite the rampant activity. We prefer call spreads given the inverted call skew, and September regular expiry to capture FOMC and all major macro data events preceding. Potential structured product reissuance with a slew of RTY-linked notes now breaching their autocall barriers could be supportive of RTY volatility going forward, as well.

Trade idea to consider (pricing indicative a/o 17 July): IWM Sep 230/240 call spread, pay \$2.90 (1.3% premium; 17% delta; 2.5x max net payout; 222.14 spot ref)

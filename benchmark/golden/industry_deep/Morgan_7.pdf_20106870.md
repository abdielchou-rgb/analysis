# Venture Vision: AIn't Seen Nothing Like It

In spite of Crypto's resurgence, AI is still the topic of the moment at our TMT conference. The cost to scale and the point in the cycle means this theme is more concentrated in private markets than any before it. More so than even Mobile postiPhone.

With our TMT conference in San Francisco ongoing, we provide a shorter update in chart form on the concentration risk in the AI theme - both public and private. A growing drum-beat of comparisons to dot-com froth in public market stocks appears oversimplistic. However, given the pull back in the wider private markets combined with the up front capital requirements to scale up foundational models - or the finetuning thereof - means the concentration in private markets is unlike anything in history. This concentration should normalise as market dry powder is deployed across a wider spectrum of start-up verticals beyond AI. However, for the time being, the hunt for application layer winners in the GenAI race is as heated as Mobile post-iPhone.

## Real-time Valuation Monitor:

A simple top-down metric to look at is the Refinitiv Venture Capital Index, which provides an aggregated and rebased index of US venture capital-funded firms. The index:

• Leverages Refinitiv private company data of over 22,000 US firms;

• Estimates the value of each company over time by compiling known firm values, estimating missing values and interpolating values between events; and

• Aggregates month-to-month returns to compute a market cap-weighted index tracking the broad US venture capital universe since 1995.

While the log-scale sees a more modest pull-back in valuations of US VC-backed companies, the index as a whole is nonetheless nearly 18% down YoY and 40% off its highs.

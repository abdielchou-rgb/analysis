## US Credit | Domestic Data Supportive, Demand Outlook Heathy, Modest Supply in 2H

• Corporate credit flows have improved after a soft patch in April, as the rates rally boosts performance. IG total returns are in the green on the year now, while HY is up close to 3%. With all-in yields still historically high, we expect fund flows to remain strong going into the cutting cycle.

• Supply has been front-loaded this year, as funding costs were lower in Jan/Feb, and companies looked to get ahead of potential uncertainty around the US election in the back half. On a net basis, we think IG supply could be very modest in the second half.

## Valuations | Not as Rich as You Think

• Our macro playbook increasingly resembles the mid-1990s. Spreads were a fair bit tighter than today’s levels.

• In IG, very low dollar prices, all-in yield buyer demand, and improving ratings (versus the past five years) suggest that index valuations might be overstating the richness relative to history.

• In HY, index quality is historically high, with BBs accounting for the majority of the index exposure. Accounting for the lower index price, and a larger skew to secured bonds, we estimate that the comparable spreads at prior cycle tights should be 30bp through the levels that index spreads imply.

## US Credit | Fundamentals Stable on the Quarter

• In HY and loans, fundamentals held up in aggregate. 1Q24 was the second quarter in a row where interest coverage for sub-IG names stabilized, following six quarters of substantial declines. Earnings growth showed resilience, but sales growth continued to decelerate.

• Dispersion remains high across ratings and sectors. Cash flow and EBITDA metrics held up for BBs/Bs, but deteriorated for CCCs.

In 1Q24, IG ICR improved for the first time since 2Q22 as HY and loans saw stabilization

## Credit Positioning | Continue to Like Loans, Bullish Risk Reversals for a Rally Back

• Loans look better than bonds even as rates rally as spread valuations are more reasonable, providing a better cushion against weaker data. All-in yields should stay compelling even with a few rate cuts, keeping demand supportive.

• We like bullish risk reversals (20D/40D) in CDX IG and iTraxx Main to position for a snapback rally after the softness over the past two weeks. Compared to outright index longs, this trade provides more cushion against some more near-term volatility. The risk to this trade is that spreads widen sharply, in which case downside is unlimited.

## Global Credit Outlook | Everything in Moderation

• Favorable macro: Morgan Stanley’s macro forecasts continue to look ideal for credit: moderate growth, moderating inflation, moderating policy rates.

• ‘Conservative’ central banks = good is good: The Fed and ECB have both struck a cautious tone on further easing. While we think the data co-operates (and both cut in September), that conservativism increases the risk that weak data would be taken badly. ‘Good is good’!

# Playbook After a Crowded Long Unwind

Momentum volatility turned into a crowded long unwinding in Japan (but less so in APxJ/EM). In this piece, we address the major issues from investors including: 1) whether to reengage; 2) style factors in favor after such events; and 3) signposts to monitor for future momentum unwind.

QUANTWISE[]

## Key Takeaways

Momentum factor underperformed in early August, driven by the unwind of crowded longs, but recovered in the second week in both APxJ and Japan.

■ Historically, the top 20 crowded long unwind episodes have seen \~40-60% of the index drop recover within two months.

To identify momentum drawdown risk, we recommend monitoring cumulative momentum factor return, volatility and volatility skew.

Crowded long unwind in Japan, less so in APxJ: As discussed in our previous Monthly Perspectives, momentum volatility was rising amid narrowing market breadth, with USD/JPY reversal into BoJ/FOMC convergence a key risk catalyst. Momentum drawdown materialized in Japan on August 2, -3.5%, and August 5, - 4.9%, both making the top 20 crowd long unwinds since 1995 ( Exhibit 5 ). Crowd long unwind was less severe in APxJ/EM. According to the average index return calculated from the top 20 crowd long unwinds in APxJ and Japan, around 40-60% of the index losses will be recouped within the next two months while direction afterward varies in APxJ and Japan ( Exhibit 8 , Exhibit 9 ).

Continue to favor Quality and Revisions factors after crowd long unwind: Our scenario analysis after the top 20 crowd long unwinds in APxJ and JP ( Exhibit 10 , Exhibit 11 ) shows that sentiment factors performed well in the next one-week to six-month horizon. We also recommend to tactically long stocks with strong 12M-1M residual momentum (idiosyncratic after removing market beta, size and value). Quality remains our core style preference given defensive nature and long-term outperformance record in APxJ/EM.

Macro factor drivers and preferences: We reiterate the soft-landing view from our global economics team, which expects the Fed to start cutting rates by 25bp each meeting through mid-2025. The market is pulling back from a US recession scare. US 10Y yields have trended lower to 3.9% ( Exhibit 18 ) and our Morgan Stanley Asia Risk Sentiment Indicator dropped to 42th percentile amid stronger performance from risk-off assets.

Momentum factor is flat August MTD: Market volatility rose sharply amid growth scare and risk of hard landing. The top 20 occurrence concentrated in Asia Financial Crisis, Dot Com Bubble, GFC Crisis and Covid when market volatility is high. However, there are notable exceptions.

## Factor Monitor – Monthly Update August 16, 2024

## Methodology

Factor return calculation: To evaluate historical factor performance, we rank stocks as at month-end within defined universes, and form quintile portfolios based on the underlying factor values. We go long the top quintile and short the bottom quintile (i.e., long the top 20th percentile of stocks within the defined universe and short those in the bottom 20th percentile), rebalanced monthly. We then calculate the performance of the time series of this L/S market-neutral portfolio over various time horizons.

Composite formation: To calculate the composite factors, we first z-score the individual factor values at each monthly observation within the defined universe. Individual factor zscores are then averaged into composite scores for each stock on an equal-weighted basis.

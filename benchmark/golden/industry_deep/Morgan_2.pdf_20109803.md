# US Banks and Consumer Finance | North America

## Dollars and Cents

In this weekly note, we track performance, EPS revision, valuation, sentiment, and positioning data across US Banks and Consumer Finance stocks. We also track the latest trends on deposits, loans and cash from the Fed H.8 release.

## Inside you will find:

• Performance and consensus EPS revisions over the past 5 days, 30 days, 90 days, and YTD.

• Dividend and buyback yields/payout ratios by company.

• Sell-side sentiment vs. short-interest trends.

• Valuation metrics (P/E and P/TBVPS) versus historical benchmarks.

• Industry-wide bank loan and deposit trends from the Fed H.8 release.

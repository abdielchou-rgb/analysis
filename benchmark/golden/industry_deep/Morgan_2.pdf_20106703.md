## Market Commentary, Week of Feb. 26, 2024

• The current policy mix of restrictive front end rates, loose fiscal policy and accommodative liquidity is unusual and may be crowding out the private economy. This is making it challenging for the average company/consumer as rates remain elevated, pricing power has faded and ample liquidity is benefiting the largest, higher quality companies.

• In this type of backdrop, we think high quality growth and operational efficiency factors outperform and we continue to recommend these cohorts. We also see large caps continuing to outperform small caps.

• This dynamic and leadership base is typical in most late cycle environments, but we think the current policy mix is helping make it more notable this cycle.

• We do not expect a sustainable broadening to small caps and lower quality areas of the market until this policy mix shifts to a regime of much lower front-end interest rates and less crowding out.

• With earnings season now largely behind us, interest rates will be key to watch over the coming couple of weeks. PCE inflation (on 2/29) and payrolls (on 3/8) will be important events in this respect. The 10-year yield in a 4%-4.35% range can continue to be supportive of risk assets. In the event of hot data and an associated break above that range, we’d expect large caps to outperform small caps given the increased rate sensitivity small caps have shown to upside moves in yields.

• We held our recurring micro/macro roundtable with equity analysts last week. Analysts described operational efficiency as a key focus from corporates amid cost cutting efforts and a focus on AI, which is supportive of our preference for this factor.

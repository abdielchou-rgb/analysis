## U.S. Return Decomposition - May 2024

The S&P 500 was down -4.1% in April (including dividends), as multiple contraction more than offset improving EPS. The influence of the largest six technology stocks remains an important contributor to returns, although less so than the first two months of the year. In April, GOOGL added 30 bps to returns while the remaining five (MSFT, META, NVDA, AMZN and AAPL) subtracted 1.2%.

Among styles, performance between Growth (-4.2%) and Value (-4.3%) was largely undifferentiated in April. Small (-7.0%) underperformed Large (-4.1%) given a greater decline in multiples for Russell 2000 stocks.

YTD, international stocks (+9.3%) have outperformed US (+6.0%) on a local-currency basis. On a currency-adjusted basis, the story looks different. More specifically, in dollar terms, international returned only +3.3%, less than the S&P 500. Currency translation is most acute in Japan, with YTD returns going from +18.1% in local terms to +5.8% in USD.

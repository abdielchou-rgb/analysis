# Global Economics & Strategy Core Convictions (Multi-Asset Rundown)

## All our views on markets in a single page

This 'core conviction' document is our weekly one-page multi-asset rundown where we highlight key events in the week ahead and any view changes (see page 2). We've also published World at a Glance which is a longer overview of all our economic & strategy views in one place.

## Highlights this week

We expect the FOMC on Wednesday to preserve 2 rate cuts this year (Sep and Dec), but if they pencil in just one cut that would essentially shut the door on any action before December (we don’t think the FOMC would move in the week of the election). US CPI is released Wednesday morning, and we expect the soft core CPI print (24bp) to impact both the forecasts and deliberations. The Fed will have received the data after close of business Tuesday (the release is early Wednesday) and we believe participants will be given an opportunity to revise their projections in the SEP beyond the normal 9am Wednesday deadline.

Friday’s strong payroll report reinforces already reduced Fed cut expectations, but the ECB’s rate cut is also somewhat unclear, leaving EURUSD in a range. The BoC, by contrast, looks comfortable delivering at least two more cuts this year (independent of the Fed) allowing USDCAD to continue pushing higher. In rates, we mainly like receiving the very front end of the UK curve (August MPC) and Swiss curve (Dec meeting). In equity, we’ve lifted our year end target for MSCI AC World (to 830). We continue to believe Europe can outperform US, are overweight China and Brazil, and by sector prefer defensives such as software, healthcare equipment, GDP 1s T&D utilities (banks screen well within cyclicals). Within EM, YTD gains in carry currencies have been eliminated. Although MXN risk premia have repriced rapidly and positioning is much cleaner, that is not true for others. We are long TRY and EGP, but short ZAR, COP and HUF.

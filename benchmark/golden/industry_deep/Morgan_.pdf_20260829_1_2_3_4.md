## Global Credit | Inflation ‘Fine’. Growth Still Needs to Hold Up. We Think It Does

• What do rate cuts mean for credit? History says this is an “and” statement. Rate cuts and decent growth = “soft landing”, a good outcome for credit. Rate cuts and weak growth = bad for credit. Weak data are the risk, meaning “good is good”. That should mean a lower correlation between spreads and yields.

We are constructive because we think cyclical indicators are improving. This needs to be monitored PMI (lvl) and LEI (YoY, %)

## The Risk of Asia Credit’s Shrinking Market Size

Since Asia credit’s market size peaked in late 2021, it has been declining for 31 straight months and is now at close to US\$900 billion. To manage this risk, we recommend that Asia credit investors explore the possibility of adding the Asia LC corp bond market to their Asia credit universe.

We believe that they should start with the LC corp bond market that has their government bonds potentially included or included in the major global indices in the past five years. We narrow down our LC corp bond universe to three markets – China, India and Korea.

## Combining Two Paths to Shape Asia Credit’s Future

We see two paths that Asia credit can take to mitigate its shrinking market risk. The first path consists of adding more USD corp bonds into Asia credit and the second is to add LC corp bonds into Asia credit.

By expanding Asia credit through two different paths, Asia credit investors would not only manage to mitigate the risk of a shrinking Asia credit market, but they would also end up with a more diversified portfolio with more investment opportunities compared to the current Asia credit universe.

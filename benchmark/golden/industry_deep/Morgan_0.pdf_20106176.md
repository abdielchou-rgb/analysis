# China IC deficit decline and SiC investment

## Key Takeaways

We think the decline in China's semi deficit is because of weak demand, impact of US export controls and increasing self-sufficiency.

We believe chiplet technology will be important for China's HPC industry.

China's continuous investment in SiC bodes well for Japan equipment vendors like DISCO and Lasertec.

## China self-sufficiency

• China's IC imports fell 15.4% in value as semiconductor self-sufficiency accelerated amid US sanctions: According to data from the General Administration of Customs of China, China's IC imports dropped in value by 15.4 percent year-on-year in US dollar terms, while the total import volume saw a decrease of 10.8 percent ( Exhibit 1 & Exhibit 2 ). (Global Times, Jan 13, 2024)

## Advanced packaging

Expansion to 1,600-Core Big Chip. In pursuit of big chip technology, a team from the Chinese Academy of Sciences has designed an advanced 256-core processor system based on 16 chiplets and aims to expand this design to a 1,600-core big chip. This design is composed of 16 chiplets, each housing 16 CPUs based on the RISC-V architecture. (Trendforce, January 8, 2024)

## SiC/Power semis

• Total investment amount exceeds US\$2.8bn for 13 SiC related projects: Recently provinces in China began to disclose 2024 major projects list. There are 13 SiC related projects listed in projects of Jiangsu, Zhejiang Henan provinces. The total investment amount exceeds Rmb20bn (US\$2.8bn). (EET China, January 19, 2024).

Stock implications: We are positive on equipment players like ACM Research (ACMR.O) and NAURA (002371.SZ), as we think they will benefit from CXMT, YMTC, Hua Hong (1347.HK) and other China fabs' capacity expansion (link). China's continuous investment in SiC will benefit equipment makers like DISCO (6146.T, OW) and Lasertec (6920.T, OW), both covered by Tetsuya Wadaki.

## China Localization Progress

We expect China semiconductor self-sufficiency ratio to reach 25% by 2025e, up 7ppt from 18% in 2022. Upstream vendors like equipment and EDA are likely to achieve selfsufficiency ratio increase, as these are the chokepoints in China semi localization and local development of these industry has become a strategic imperative for China. We are positive on self-sufficiency ratio increase of semiconductors components using mature node like image sensors, power semis given local foundry capacity expansion, while we are relatively cautious on logic semis using advanced node, like CPUs GPUs, given US export restrictions.

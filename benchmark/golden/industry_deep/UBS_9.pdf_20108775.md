# APAC Economic Perspectives Tracking China Plus One in 2024

## Relocation intention the highest on record

UBS Evidence Lab conducted the 13th wave China CFO survey of 423 senior corporate executives between 18 April and 14 May (> Access Dataset). The survey indicated that the proportion of respondents planning to relocate export production overseas reached the highest level of all surveys. Most respondents expect additional US tariffs if ex-President Trump is re-elected. Exploring alternative markets ranked top if tariff hikes take place with relocating overseas in second place, which could partly explain the increase in the intention to relocate.

## Korea/Taiwan top EM Asia destination list; Malaysia and Indonesia moved up

Korea was the most popular destination for exporters who have or plan to move, despite the proportion declining vs. the 2023 survey. Taiwan took second place. With significant gains in popularity, Malaysia climbed to third place. Indonesia also significantly gained in popularity. Vietnam's popularity did not change, but its ranking within ASEAN dropped vs. 2023. Similarly, Korea and Taiwan stood out as the top two destinations for incremental investment in the near-term, followed by Malaysia.

## Real data support some previous survey results

Vietnam, Taiwan, Korea and India were the top four US import market share gainers in Asia when comparing the 2023 and 2017 surveys. The four economies also ranked the most popular destinations for relocation in most previous surveys. Global import share dynamics display a similar trend with the exception of Korea. The Philippines and Thailand were in the bottom half in previous surveys and have not gained much trade market share since. FDI to EM Asia was higher than before the US-China trade friction, led by Korea and Taiwan. Vietnam led FDI inflow in 2023 whereas other ASEAN economies saw FDI contractions, consistent with Vietnam's top ranked position within ASEAN in the 2023 survey, but relative momentum may weaken based on the latest survey. The increase in popularity of other ASEAN economies in the latest survey may indicate more FDI flow to these economies in upcoming quarters.

## Planned relocation is expected to be completed in the near-term

This survey indicated that exporters who intend to relocate plan to move 31-50% of export production capacity abroad with the majority planning to complete the move by 2026. The survey suggests that the most popular funding source for overseas relocation remains China, followed by the destination country.

## Recent US tariffs may have limited direct impact, but entail spillover

Supply chain connectivity, the possibility to be exempted from additional US tariffs, and the destination’s policy on foreign businesses are the top three considerations for exporters that plan to relocate. Of those who have started the move, the destination's policy on foreign business, production costs, and supply chain connectivity are the topthree obstacles. The US has announced that exporters from Cambodia, Malaysia, Thailand and Vietnam who have been found to be circumventing anti-dumping and countervailing duties on some solar products from China will be subject to duties. Our analysis indicates that solar exports to the US are small compared to total exports and some producers can still qualify for exemption. Thus the direct impact should be small. However, the risk of additional tariffs after relocating production may become a more significant obstacle for those corporates considering relocation.

The dynamics of global import market share changes also show some agreements with previous survey records. Similar to the US import market share changes, Vietnam, Taiwan, India are the top gainers in the global import market comparing 2023 vs. 2017. Indonesia was not well -ranked by previous surveys, however, its share gains in global trade could be attributed to mineral downstreaming, which has increased export values of nickel related products especially.

Thailand and Philippines lost some shares in the global import market, neither of them have been wellranked in the historical survey. In contrast to survey results and the market share gains in the US market, Korea lost most market share in the global import market comparing 2023 vs. 2017. In our assessment, this could be attributed to cyclical factor, e.g. 2023 was a low for memory cycle as well as changes in market share in mainland China market.

FDI under Balance of Payment shows that foreign investment to Asia ex-China has generally climbed higher in the recent post-COVID quarters vs. those quarters ahead of US-China trade war in 2018. Taiwan and Korea were among the top Asia FDI winners during the period.

2023 was not a strong year for FDI, with most Asia economies registered FDI contraction vs. 2022 with the exception of Vietnam. Indeed, Vietnam was the most popular relocation destination within ASEAN according to the previous 2023 China CFO survey and other data also show that FDI from China is a significant contributor of FDI to Vietnam.

Increase of popularity of other ASEAN economies in the latest survey may indicate more FDI flow to these economies in the coming quarters.

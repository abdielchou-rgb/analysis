# How Willing Is Your Bank to Pay Up for Deposits? February 2024

What's New: Banks continued to decrease their CD offers during the last two weeks of February. The average highest rate paid decreased by another 2 bps. Six banks cut their highest rate over the past two weeks (ALLY, CFR, DFS, GS, SNV, and WBS) versus only two increases (MTB and ZION).

Rates on new CD offers decreased again: In the past two weeks, the average highest rate paid decreased another 2 bps to 4.91%, while the average 13-36 month rate decreased 2 bps to 3.51% and the average 1-12 month rate decreased 2 bps to 4.78%. Among the primarily online deposit gatherers (ALLY, AXP, COF, DFS, and SYF), the average highest CD rate decreased by 5 bps to 4.82%, while the average online savings rate was unchanged.

Shifting focus to downside betas as the Fed eventually cuts rates: The pace at which banks can bring down their deposit costs as the Fed cuts rates in 2024 will be an important driver of net interest income outlooks across our coverage universe. We expect most banks to continue shortening their liability duration by 1) Cutting longer dated CD offers (13-36 months); and 2) Adding more money market deposits versus CDs over the next couple of quarters. Of the banks that have provided guidance, downside interest bearing deposit betas are expected to be around the 40-50% range in 2024 across our coverage. We have published a table detailing the assumptions that are baked into 2024 NII guidance across our coverage universe here.

• Highest CD Rates: NYCB is offering its highest CD rate for maturities under 36 months at 5.50%, followed by OZK at 5.30%, and BKU, CMA and PNC at 5.25%.

• Lowest CD Rates: CBSH, AXP, and FITB are offering their highest CD rate for maturities under 36 months at 4.60%, 4.50% and 4.25% respectively.

• Largest Increases: MTB increased its 1-12 month rate by 15 bps while SNV increased its 13-36 month rate by 5 bps.

• Largest Decreases: WBS decreased its 1-12 month rate by 30 bps while ZION decreased its 13-36 rate by 52 bps.

• Online Rates: For banks offering online rates, we use the higher of each bank's money market or online savings rates. No banks decreased or increased their online rates in the past two weeks.

Investment recommendations: Our highest conviction Overweight-rated banks (in order) are C, GS, and JPM for Large Cap Banks, HBAN, MTB, and EWBC for Midcap Banks, and ALLY and SLM in Consumer Finance.

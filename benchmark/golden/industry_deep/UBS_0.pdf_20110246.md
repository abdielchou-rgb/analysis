## UBS’ strongest views across the 22 biggest EM economies

In this note we synthesise UBS' economics, equity, FX, rates and credit strategy views on the 22 largest EM economies. We outline the core themes per economy, our preferred trades, and identify key signposts and dates to monitor over the next 6m. See page 3 for our latest trade recommendations and pages 5-7 for 10 key charts.

## 3 themes into year end

1) US catching down not as positive as EM catching up, but still helpful. After being cautious on EM risk assets all year, in early August we highlighted improving risk/ reward. This isn’t because the EM growth picture has dramatically improved, though it's improving at the margin. Instead, it is because we expect the US’ shift from exceptional to pedestrian growth will be helpful for EM in the context of very clean positioning (longest stretch of portfolio outflows in \~20y). A Harris win is more likely to see a potential window of EM outperformance realising in Q4.

2) China stimulus needed for EM outperformance beyond Q4: EM's transit through the left tail of the dollar smile may be shorter and less painful than in the past, but China stimulus is likely needed for extended EM outperformance beyond Q4. We see China weakness well priced into local stocks, but worry that the disconnect between largely stable import volumes and continued property weakness will be resolved via weaker trade & commodity demand ahead.

3) Local debt the most direct beneficiary of moderating US growth and contained oil prices, particularly as risks of higher DM equity volatility could tactically spill over to EM. Asia equities & FX should outperform Latam and CEE. The rally in local debt could broaden.

## FX: Hold upside optionality for Q4 gains; avoid 3 "C"s

We recommended selected long Asia FX exposure last month and see further tradeable upside from foreign asset repatriation, shifting hedge ratios and low oil prices, particularly under a potential Democrat win. We prefer to avoid"3 Cs": CNH, COP and CZK amid growth risks and unattractive valuation. We remain highly selective on carry currencies.

## Equities: Bulk of historical Fed easing gains already realised; US elections key

The historical gains seen through prior Fed easing have already played out, and challenges from a synchronised US & China slowdown lie ahead. We prefer EM debt over equities into YE. Tactical EM outperformance vs. DM is more likely under a Democrat win, with ASEAN stocks likely to display greater resilience under a Trump win (see here for our report on APAC equities and the US election). We prefer China, Malaysia, South Africa & Poland within EM.

## FI: Local debt rally to broaden. Asia rates to outperform JGBs

South Africa aside, the decline in EM 10y yields this year has been entirely an Asian phenomenon. We expect some broadening ahead on our below consensus views on US & China growth, and add duration risk. We remain positioned for EM Asia outperformance vs. JGBs and lower front end rates in Latam. In credit, spreads remain firmly priced for a US soft landing scenario despite growing uncertainty - overweight local debt, FX neutral.

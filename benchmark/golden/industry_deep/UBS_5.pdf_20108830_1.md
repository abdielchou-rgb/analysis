# European Economic Perspectives European inflation monthly: June preview

## June inflation previews across Europe

Below we present expectations for June inflation for the Eurozone, UK, Switzerland and Sweden.

## Eurozone HICP: Headline -0.2pp to 2.4% y/y, core -0.1pp to 2.8%

We expect June headline inflation (out on 2 July) to decline 0.2pp to 2.4% y/y (unrounded 2.44%), reversing the increase in May. While our forecast assumes that the 0.1pp easing in core inflation to 2.8% y/y will be the key driver of the decline in headline, food and energy inflation are also likely to fall in June (-0.2pp each to 2.4% y/y and 0.1% y/y, respectively). Within core, we expect both goods and services inflation to fall, by 0.2pp to 0.5% y/y and 0.1pp to 4% y/y, respectively. However, we think that the risk to our forecast is skewed to the upside, particularly in services inflation, not least due to travel and recreation related items amid various sporting and entertainment events. See also Eurozone: Inflation likely to decline in June.

## UK CPI: Headline and core +0.1pp to 2.1% y/y and 3.6% y/y, respectively

Following the decline in headline inflation to 2% y/y in May, we expect a 0.1pp tick-up in headline inflation to 2.1% y/y in June (out on 17 July). While the 0.1pp increase in core inflation (to 3.6% y/y) is expected to be the key driver, higher energy inflation (+0.6pp to -15.3% y/y) should also contribute to the rise in headline inflation. In contrast, food inflation is likely to continue to fall in June (-0.2pp to 3.1% y/y). Within core, we expect higher goods inflation (+0.4pp to 0.3% y/y) to be the main driver of the overall increase, largely reflecting a base effect from the decline in goods prices in June 2023. In contrast, services inflation is expected to show another small decline (-0.1pp to 5.6% y/y).

## Swiss CPI: Headline unchanged for the third consecutive month at 1.4% y/y

We expect Swiss headline inflation to remain unchanged in June (out on 4 July), at 1.4% y/y, marking the third consecutive months of stability. We expect a 0.1pp increase in core inflation (headline excluding energy and food) to 1.3% y/y, which we think will be fully offset by lower food (-0.4pp to -0.2% y/y) and energy inflation (-0.4pp to 6.2% y/y). The next increase in rents (triggered by a hike in the mortgage reference rate, which played an important role in May) will only come in August. Against this backdrop, and taking into account recent CHF appreciation, the key downside risk for June inflation is likely to stem from foreign inflation.

## Swedish CPIF: Headline and core down 0.4pp to 1.9% y/y and 2.6% y/y, resp.

We do not expect the May increase in (seasonal) travel-related services inflation to be repeated, which should imply a substantial step down in Swedish inflation in June (out on 12 July). Specifically, we expect both CPIF headline inflation and CPIF excluding energy to fall by 0.4pp to 1.9% y/y and 2.6%, respectively. The deceleration in our forecast is broad-based across services (-0.8pp to 3.6% y/y), food (-0.5pp to 1.6% y/y) and energy (-1.1pp to -9.5% y/y). In contrast, we expect goods inflation to remain unchanged at 0.8% y/y after declining by 0.7pp in May.

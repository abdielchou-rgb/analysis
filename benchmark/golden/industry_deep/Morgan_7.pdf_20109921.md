# Stock Performance Review: PYPL, FOUR, GLBE

As we look across stock performance and valuations, we see stock performance dispersion has been substantial in '23-'24 even as Fintech multiples have widely compressed, and there are 3 key areas that stick out to us across the payment networks, FinTech, and smaller players.

PYPL (EW, PT \$71) was up 5% week-to-date (11% month-to-date) on the back of Apple opening its NFC chip to third party developers in the US/Elsewhere. The opening of the NFC payment credential had been mandated in Continental Europe, and many, including us, felt like that portended an opening elsewhere, including the U.S. Nevertheless, the timing has come sooner than most would have expected. We expect that the financial impact to PYPL is likely to be minimal because of the limited potential economics of in-store transactions (below the 3% standard take rate for Branded PYPL txns) and we wouldn’t expect full availability of an integrated PayPal payment solution for iOS until sometime in CY25. But while financial impact may be minimal, the most likely positive impact would be to create and build habituation as local credential storage could reduce friction offline, in turn improving likelihood of usage online, where economics are better. We're looking for incremental understanding around potential gross margin impact, and Apple hardware utility for online purchases. See more here: Apple Opening NFC in US/ Elsewhere Should Drive PayPal Habituation & Relieve Competitive Pressure.

FOUR (EW, PT \$70) was up 14% this past week, the best performer across our coverage ( Exhibit 1 ) off of changing investor sentiment post their 2Q24 print (8/8). We think the narrative surrounding FOUR has changed slightly as many investors were negative into the print (including the vast majority in our preearnings Bull/Bear roundtable), so management's 2024 outlook raise and increased backlog disclosures were particularly encouraging. We think the 2Q24 print gave investors more conviction on the 2H ramp given a robust installation pipeline, ongoing SkyTab strength, and international expansion, supported by distribution improvements via acquisitions of Finaro, Vectron and Revel. Additionally, US consumer trends are showing signs of stabilization in recent weeks (vs. July), so macro concerns are likely better-than-feared. See more here: Pieces in Place to Deliver on Faster Back-Half Growth Targets.

GLBE (OW, PT \$40) illustrated a shoot first and ask questions later dynamic this past week. Post a GMV guide-down, the stock was down as much as 15% but closed the day down \~6%. However, after backing out the impact of a large customer bankruptcy (Ted Baker), results were quite positive across several fronts (enterprise wins including Victoria's Secret and Managed Markets in particular), enabling the stock to finish up \~4% week-to-date. ## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Shift4 Payments Inc. (FOUR.N)

We base our price target on a P/E valuation methodology using a 14x multiple on our '25 adj. EPS estimate. On a growth-adjusted basis, our valuation assumption is generally in-line with merchant processing peers and accounts for FOUR's more aggressive capitalization of customer acquisition costs.

## Risks to Upside

n FCF conversion improves, easing profitability concerns

n Faster/greater conversion of existing Gateway merchants to End-to-End offering

n Faster-than-expected recovery of travel, hotels and/or restaurants

n M&A

## Risks to Downside

n Competitive losses

n Inability to convert Gateway merchants

n FCF conversion worsens, hurting profitability

n Key man risk associated with CEO/Founder

## Global-e Online Ltd. (GLBE.O)

We value GLBE using a '25 EV / EBITDA multiple of 35x, pricing GLBE at a modest premium to peers on a growth-adjusted basis, mindful of the company's strong competitive positioning and growth trajectory.

## Risks to Upside

n Incremental confidence in GLBE's enabling role for cross-border e-commerce growth

n Greater than expected growth of existing merchants

n Faster than expected SHOP Markets Pro contribution

## Risks to Downside

n Persistent regional conflict

n International tourism could replace more key category purchases than expected

n Expansion of free-trade zones or store footprint by key brands could diminish GLBE's value-add

## PayPal Holdings, Inc. (PYPL.O)

Our PT is based on a \~15x multiple on our SBC-burdened CY25 EPS estimate. We think such a P/E valuation makes sense as it's in-line with most payment processing peers on an earnings growth adjusted basis, and gives PYPL credit for retaining market share and growing in-line with eCommerce.

## Risks to Upside

n Faster eCommerce (ex Amazon) growth

n Greater usage in existing markets and greater adoption in new markets

n Faster margin expansion

n Accretive acquisitions

n Traction in Venmo monetization/new partnerships

## Risks to Downside

n Slowdown of eCommerce growth (ex-Amazon)

n Underperformance at eBay or faster conversion of volumes to eBay's managed platform

n Other market players with leads in offline could gain traction online

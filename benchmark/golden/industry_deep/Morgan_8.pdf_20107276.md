## Only a few eggs in EM Easter basket

Inflows into EM Equities sharply decelerated and were flattish (+\$12mn, from +\$354mn). Among regional funds, subscriptions into Asia ex-Japan picked up (+ \$415mn, from +\$169mn), whereas EMEA continued to post small inflows (+ \$14mn, from +\$17mn). GEMs reverted to outflows (-\$330mn, from +\$379mn) while redemptions in LatAm slowed down to -\$87mn (from -\$211mn) marking an outflow streak of seven weeks (-\$1.3bn in total). ETFs posted strong subscriptions (+\$1.9bn), which were nearly counterbalanced by the sell-off in non-ETFs (- \$1.8bn). EM Equity flows YTD are +\$2.8bn.

Mixed flows across EM markets, Korea stands out. After two weeks of outflows Korea posted strong inflows (+\$3.0bn, from -\$121mn), while Turkey (+\$429, 1 week lag), Thailand (+\$68mn, from -\$1.0bn) and Malaysia (+\$24mn, from - \$217mn) also flipped to subscriptions. Sell-off in India accelerated to -\$519mn (from -\$168mn), while Brazil posted outflows of -\$393mn (from +\$316mn). Redemptions in Taiwan eased to -\$185mn (from -\$2.7bn) in the last week of March. Saudi (-\$45mn, 1 week lag), Philippines (-\$44mn, from -\$55mn), Indonesia (-\$41mn, from +\$104mn) and South Africa (-\$28mn, from -\$113mn) also posted small outflows.

EM bond flows were -\$1.8bn this week, -\$10.1bn YTD - see report.

## Consensus Asset Allocation

We publish Consensus Asset Allocation report every month, where we review the asset allocation of major emerging market fund managers relative to the MSCI EM index as at the end of the reporting month. We use two approaches. Our preferred is to count the number of managers that are meaningfully OW or UW a market, defined as greater than 2% deviation from the benchmark. The results are displayed in the tables. The other is a simple average of the managers<sup>’</sup> country allocation compared with the benchmark. Extreme positions of a few managers distorts this analysis.

This report reviews the allocation of emerging market funds for 54 managers surveyed by EPFR Global as of end January 2024.

## Consensus overweight markets: Brazil, Indonesia, and Mexico

Consensus underweight markets:China, Taiwan, S. Africa, India, Malaysia, Thailand, Saudi Arabia, Philippines, Turkey, Czech, Egypt, Hungary, Peru, Poland, Chile, Greece, Colombia, UAE, Qatar, and Kuwait

Consensus increased allocation in Mexico, China and EM ASEAN. Net Net overweights in Mexico increased to 16 from 11. Net underweights in China + HK decreased to 11 from 15 in January. Net underweights in India increased to 9 from 7 in January. Median EM fund allocation is 70bps below benchmark. India<sup>’</sup>s foreign investors have seen their allocations turn UW as benchmark weights have risen rapidly. EM funds increased net OW in Indonesia to 17 from 16. Net UW in Philippines and Malaysia decreased to 26 (from 29) and 40 (from 43), respectively.

The median fund outperformed the benchmark by 0.6%.The sectors and markets that significantly outperformed in the past month were Korea Consumer Discretionary, Korea Financials, India Energy, India Consumer Discretionary, Indonesia Financials, China Consumer Discretionary, Poland, Indonesia and Korea. Reversion, earnings momentum and net analyst revision are outperforming quant factors month to date. Volatility, forecast PE and dividend yield are key underperformers. For more, please see “Consensus Asset Allocation – EM funds asset allocation and performance<sup>”</sup> Martins Jr. et al, 29 February 2024.

# Global Rates Strategy

# Rates Map: Wading through the first Fed cut but direction is clear

## United States - US curve to bear flatten on a 25bp start for the Fed

 We acknowledge the risk of a 50bp cut by the FOMC, but are putting more weight on the comments of FOMC members just before the quiet period. Assuming the Fed cuts 25bp, the curve should bear flatten. UBS expects a 25bp rate cut at every meeting until 3.0% to 3.25% is reached in Sep 2025.

 Since 1990, both US 2y and 10y yields have traded lower in the trading window around the first Fed cut, except for Nov 2002 when the US 2y yield rose 6 bps, and Sep 2007 when the US 10y yield increased 9bp (Figure 4). 2s10s curves generally steepened around first Fed cuts with the exception of Sep 1998, Nov 2002 and July 2019.

Bull steepening the 3m into a first cut is a common theme, but current 2s10s steepening has only been matched by the 2007 episode. Looking 3m ahead of the first Fed cut in our sample, we observe only one instance in which the curve has flattened, specifically 3m after the July 1995 cut. Interestingly, the moves in the 10s30s curve were more subdued, with the curve relatively flat over the 3m period following the first Fed cut. See Figure 5 and US rates around the first Fed cut.

 US 30y spreads should remain under pressure until STRIPS activity picks up sharply. While some expect loosening of SLR requirements to cause bank portfolios to aggressively buy 30yr spreads, it could be difficult to dramatically reduce capital requirements on Treasuries after SVB.

## Euro area - Fed cut should support EUR duration while long 2y bunds, Z4Z5 flattener, and fading Oct cut

 A first Fed cut should support European duration. Since US 10y yields peaked around 5% last year, FOMC guidance around Fed meetings has driven German10y yields lower (Figure 9 . The wave of EGB supply is also behind us (Figure 10).

 We went long 2y bunds (entry: 2.23%) and lowered our end 2024 forecast on 2y bunds to 1.80% (from 2% previously) after the third ECB meeting in a row where bund yields traded higher. The ECB has been following a meeting-by-meeting approach even with its risk assessment and recent data pointing to downside risks to growth and its inflation outlook for 2026 back at target.

 We expect the market to price in at least 5 cuts for 2025 (so a Z4Z5 flattener) as headline inflation is set to fall below 2% in September and energy prices are settling at materially lower levels (Figure 8). We would also pay the Oct ’24 meeting given the President’s comment that the meeting is a relatively short period away.

## United Kingdom - Keep receiving SONIA 1y1y as curve steepens further

We have updated the profit target for our receive SONIA 1y1y trade to 3.1%, just below the lowest levels seen in this cycle in December 2023. We have been recommending this long UK front-end position since late June when the swap was trading at 4.05%. We continue to see a strong possibility for a stop in active gilt sales beginning in October given FY 24-25 supply, significant APF redemptions and signs that the Preferred Minimum Range of Reserves may be higher than the BoE expected.

## January 10

Fed's Williams says point of slowing runoff is not close; USTs twist-steepen; JPY displays broad weakness after soft labor earnings; EUR gains, EGBs flatten after Schnabel comments; KRW falls with large equity outflows; IBR rates gain; DXY at 102.36 (-0.2%); US 10y at 4.028% (+1.5bp).

• New York Fed President Williams cites "no signs of adverse effects on market functioning" from QT, and he does not believe the FOMC is close to the point of slowing its pace of asset runoff.

• USTs twist-steepen in muted session ahead of December CPI; a strong result in the \$37bn 10y re-opening auction is on display, with only a minor tail, before Thursday's first-tier risk event.

• JPY exhibits broad-based weakness after total cash earnings decelerate significantly in November while real wages remain negative.

• EUR/USD (+0.4%) rises and EGB curves flatten, with front-end underperformance, following comments by ECB Governing Council Member Schnabel that is it "too early to discuss rate cuts."

• KRW falls 0.3%, to 1320 against USD, amid a decline in KOSPI and the largest foreign outflow from local equity markets in nearly two months.

• IBR curve outperforms peers as the 2y rallies 17bp after the prior session's downside CPI surprise.

## Japan:

Disappointing November wage growth in Japan drove another JGB rally, although the 10y JGB auction loomed around noon in Tokyo hours. Long-end JGBs continued to outperform as market participants sought carry now that it appears the BoJ will not move anytime soon. However, the initial rally retraced after the weak 10y JGB auction result (new issue: JB373 with tail yield at 0.609%), a 1.2bp tail compared to market pricing (0.597%). The JGB curve reversed its initial rally, led by the 10y tenor. With 10y JGB yields below 0.60%, domestic market participants seemed reluctant to chase yields lower as market participants broadly still expect BoJ normalization at the upcoming April monetary policy meeting. However, JGBs traded relatively firmly, supported by demand to buy this dip. While 10y JGBs sold off 0.5bp d/d to 0.59% on a simple yield basis, 30y JGBs rallied 1.6bp d/d to 1.58%.

TONA OIS rallied across the curve, but long-end swaps lagged matched maturity JGBs. The 2y tenor outperformed the most, as market pricing of future BoJ normalization was priced out. XCCY basis curve continued to tighten, led by the 10y tenor.

JPY weakened broadly from the Tokyo morning as weak November wage growth data discouraged market pricing of further BoJ normalization. Although wage growth data remained solid on a common sample basis, market participants appeared to instead focus on the large decline in real wage growth. Accordingly, USD/JPY ground higher toward 145.00 and eclipsed this level at some points. EUR/ JPY ground higher to 158.50 into the London morning. JPY carry trades appeared to resume as markets priced in BoJ dovishness. USD/JPY trades nearly 400 pips above fair value implied by US-Japan policy divergence (140.80 at the time of writing).

## Europe:

NOK brushed off weak CPI data, as both USD/NOK and EUR/NOK moved lower overnight. However, throughout the NY trading session, NOK reversed course and ended roughly unchanged against the US dollar and slightly weaker against EUR (EUR/NOK: +0.2%). EUR outperformed in the G10 as Bunds sold off after comments from ECB Executive Board Member Schnabel, who said it is premature to talk about interest rate cuts. The ECB’s implied policy path for 2024 repriced higher by \~4bp.

In a quiet session data-wise, European duration opened higher and performed well early in the London session but eventually sold off (10y Bunds: +2bp to 2.21%). Once again, the move was led by real yields as inflation expectations were broadly lower (2y2y: -3bp). Curves flattened most of the London session, initially led by the long end and then by the front end. In Germany, both 2s10s and 10s30s flattened 2bp. Following a well-received 10y Spanish syndication, peripherals outperformed in the G4 and the 10y BTP-Bund spread tightened by 3bp d/d to 163bp. The core ASW complex cheapened, led by Schatz (-1.5bp) after stabilizing earlier in the week.

## Dollar Bloc:

G10 FX performance was mixed, but the DXY USD Index (-0.1%) dipped ahead of US CPI. JPY underperformed as November labor cash earnings underwhelmed, while the latest BoJ estimate on the output gap suggests the economy remained in excess supply. Global factors were a more important driver for AUD (+0.1%), while in-line November CPI did little to impact RBA pricing (\~2bp are priced in for the February RBA meeting).

## Emerging Markets

## Asia:

AxJ currencies underperformed broadly alongside lower AxJ equities. PHP led the losses, 0.4% lower to 56.28, weighed down by a wider trade deficit in November. TWD was the second-worst performer, down 0.4% to 31.13, amid over US\$100mn equity outflows. USD/KRW rose 0.3%, to 1320, amid a 0.8% decline in KOSPI, and US\$318.4mn of foreign outflows from local equity markets, the largest amount in nearly two months. THB had relatively modest performance, stable \~34.95, given the Thailand PM acknowledged the central bank’s autonomy after Wednesday’s meeting with BoT governor. CNY and CNH traded in a narrow range, \~7.1710 and \~7.1830, respectively.

Most AxJ rates rallied. In Malaysia, the swap curve fell 4bp and 10y bond yields fell 1bp. In Hong Kong, given HKMA injected HKD1.0bn liquidity via discount window, 3m HIBOR shifted down by 7bp, and the 2s10s swap curve bull-steepened by 2bp. In India, the NDOIS curve fell by 2bp, while the bond market remained subdued. In Thailand, the 2s10s ThaiGB curve bull-flattened by 2bp. China and Korea were the outliers. In China, the NDIRS curve rose by 2bp and the 2s10s CGB curve bearflattened by 1bp. In Korea, the 2s10s NDIRS curve bear-steepened by 3bp.

## Latin America:

Amid few local developments, LatAm currencies ended the session mixed. MXN (- 0.1%) and COP (-0.1%) underperformed, while BRL (+0.3%) rallied. LatAm rates were mostly lower in the session, except for front-end DIs, which sold off slightly (January 2026s: +2bp). TIIEs ended mostly lower with gains concentrated in the front end (2y: -3bp). The IBR curve outperformed as the 2y rallied 17bp after Tuesday evening’s downside CPI surprise.

## Trading Strategy 1 – "Trade Longs/Fade Shorts"

When the 5-day moving average crosses above the 20-day moving average, buy the futures contract (long duration) and hold for a 25-business-day period. When the 5-day moving average crosses below the 20-day moving average, buy the futures contract and hold for a 25-business-day period. In short, this strategy buys futures when the Simple Moving Average Crossover (SMAX) generates both a long and a short signal, given the historical outperformance of long signals traded long and the underperformance of short signals traded short. Given that the SMAX could generate both a long and a short signal within the predefined holding period, an investor may have a 200% long position since each of the two signals would be traded in separate portfolio sleeves.

## Trading Strategy 2 – Trade "Longs Only"

When the 5-day moving average crosses above the 20-day moving average, buy the futures contract (long duration) and hold for a 25-business-day period. When the 5-day moving average crosses below the 20-day moving average, do nothing. In short, an investor ONLY trades long signals initiated by the SMAX given their historical precedent to outperform.

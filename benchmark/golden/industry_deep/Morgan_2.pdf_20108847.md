# Positions of Active Long-Only Managers in Asia/EM

We publish our June 2024 Asia/EM positioning guide to summarize allocation changes at the stock, country and sector level over time from a regional perspective. On top of that, we share the distribution of active weights and compare the allocation difference between US and EU managers.

## Key Takeaways

US\$3.2bn outflow from active GEM funds and US\$284m inflow from GEM passive products

GEM active managers reduced o/w in TSMC from 3.1% in April to 2.4% in May for risk management purpose

At a sector level, funds reduced o/w in Semis/Equipment, while added to Industrials and Communication Services

■ QTD country rebalance focus on reducing o/w India and from u/w to neutral Korea

In Japan, shorts in Financials are being covered with outstanding short interest down by 30% June MTD

## Focus of The Month - Active GEM managers taking profits from TSMC:

According to our bottom-up fund position analysis of the largest 80 EM active long only mutual funds (with average fund AUM at US\$4.1bn), the dominant rebalancing activity among these managers was reducing o/w in TSMC. Although Semis/ Equipment has been on a good run, EM managers have reduced o/w in Semi/ Equipments to o/w 2.9% in May, closer to levels at the start of the year ( Exhibit 1 ).

In Japan, active managers remained u/w in Financials while hedge fund managers are covering shorts in Financials in June MTD ( Exhibit 21 Exhibit 16 ). Active managers added to Capital Goods while trimming o/w in Discretionary Retail ( Exhibit 19 ).

Distribution of China active weight among EM funds shifted left ( Exhibit 6 ) - Estimated simple average underweight increases from -1.5% in April to -1.7% in May 2024. We see an increase in the number of funds in the -4% to -8% groups while the majority of funds are within the -2% to 2% range.

Our SICAV fund universe in this analysis is formed by taking the 40 largest active funds in "International Funds: Morningstar Emerging Markets Equity", covering total AUM of US \$76bn.

We carve out EM equities to form a composite portfolio using weighted average by market value of each security.

Rationale: We track only the funds with the largest AUM because of their better data quality. Changes in individual securities positions across different funds are aggregated based on market value. Please note that not every individual fund discloses month-end positions, so our estimations are for best-effort market indication purposes rather than factual reported data.

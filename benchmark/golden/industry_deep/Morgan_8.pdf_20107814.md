# ADRs Flows Master – Institutional Ownership and Short Interest

Institutional net outflows were recorded among Chinese, Japanese and Taiwanese ADRs in April MTD. Hedge funds led this long selling. Flows sentiment was particularly cautious among Chinese ADRs as outstanding shorts were increased concurrently.

## Key Takeaways

■ Trading sentiment in Asian ADRs have moderated in April MTD, as the aggregated trading activities of US-listed Asian ADRs was down by 7.7% in April.

Hedge funds were reducing gross exposure on the ADRs segment, with net outflows of US\$1.6bn during the month. TSMC dominated the sell flows.

Mutual funds have turned to be net sellers on Asian ADRs in April, PDD was net sold the most by them during the month.

For Chinese equities, we note an increase of short interest among ADRs, and a spike in short positions on H-shares amid the recent market uptrend.

## Sentiment continued to cool down among Asian ADRs in April:

Although net outflows were recorded among Asian ADRs from March onward, YTD net inflows to Asian ADRs remained strong at US\$12.5bn. For their trading liquidity, it has declined from March to April by 7.7%, seeing 30-day average-daily volume of all Asian ADRs down from US\$12.2bn to US\$11.2bn.

In April MTD, mutual funds have turned to be net sellers on the segment, contributing outflows of US\$0.75bn, while hedge funds continued to sell with outflows of US\$1.6bn, in which Asian hedge funds took the lead, and US and EU also started to trim.

Per our estimates, TSMC, Trip.com, Legend Biotech, NIO, and iQIYI were sold down the most by hedge funds. For long-only mutual funds and ETFs, the net selling were seen on PDD, ARM Holdings, Sea Limited, Legend Biotech and Baidu.com ( Exhibit 6 ). Inflows were toward TSMC and Vipshop.

We also note that short interest on China H-shares has been on the rise quickly over the five trading days amid market risk rally. It does not seem like a result of hedge fund adding gross exposure given the recorded outflows. We believe it is more about investors' cautious sentiment on the market outlook ahead ( Exhibit 11 ).

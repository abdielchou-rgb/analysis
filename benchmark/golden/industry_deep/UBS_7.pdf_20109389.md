# APAC Economic Perspectives Vietnam: So far so good?

## Cyclical upswing continues with strong export and production momentum

Vietnam's GDP growth accelerated to 6.9% in 2Q24, achieving 6.4% growth in 1H24. Vietnam is likely to record the strongest growth among ASEAN economies in 1H24. The export upswing has been a key driver of the economic growth recovery, with more than 14% y/y export growth in 1H24, the highest in EM Asia and exceeding that of Taiwan and Korea. Strong export momentum supported production momentum, resulting in positive spillover to domestic labour income and investment. Export and industrial production strength likely continued into 2H24, as indicated by the extension of the new orders upswing and further acceleration in export growth in July.

## Real estate activity accelerated, retail sales showed signs of improvement

Real estate activity growth accelerated further in 2Q24. Retail sales growth remained sub-trend and auto sales remained weak, but signs of improvement are emerging. Vietnam recorded one of the strongest international visitors recoveries in Asia in 1H24. Together with the continuing support from international tourism, the recent wave of wage hikes and a stronger labour market could support retail sales and consumption ahead.

## Foreign direct investment accelerated

Vietnam foreign direct investment inflow was already stronger than other EM Asia economies in previous quarters and recent data suggests further acceleration. The growth of FDI implemented capital accelerated to 8.2% y/y in 1H24 vs 3.5% in 2023, pushing the actual investment above the upward trend observed in recent years. The growth of FDI registered capital had slowed earlier but bounced back sharply in May/ June. Importantly, manufacturing FDI grew 26.3% y/y in 1H24. A challenge to FDI last summer were electricity outages; we have not noted significant cases of power cuts so far.

## Inflation picked up, exchange rate weakness has been an concern

CPI inflation increased in 1H24 to 4.3% by June—at the high end of the government's estimate of 4.0-4.5%. The recent wage hikes could increase inflation, especially given the strong economic growth recovery. The depreciation of VND (especially in early 2Q) against the strong USD and persistent interest rate differentials between USD and VND fuelled concerns about external stability. Though the VND has been the weakest against the USD in recent history, its real effective exchange is at the upper end of historical distribution. Vietnam's current account surplus reached a historical high to above 6.3% of GDP by 1Q24 and a strong trade balance and FDI in recent months suggest the basic Balance of Payment balance remains strong.

## Government to meet goals; maintain above-consensus growth forecast

Despite recent leadership changes, the government has vowed to achieve its 2024 socio-economic development targets (growth target 6.0-6.5%). The government published Resolution 111/NQ-CP on 22 July, vowing to achieve a series of medium- and long-term goals, including average GDP growth of 7% and per capita income to approximately USD7,500 by 2030. The effective date of the new land law—a significant economic reform— has been moved forward to1 Aug 2024 from 1 Jan 2025. Given the strong growth so far and signs of growing momentum, we maintain our aboveconsensus GDP growth forecast of 6.4% vs. consensus of 6.0%. We raise our CPI inflation projection from 3.1% to 3.7%. We believe further interest rate cuts would occur only after US interest rate cuts and inflation pressure easing comfortably; our baseline assumes a 25bps rate cut in 4Q24.

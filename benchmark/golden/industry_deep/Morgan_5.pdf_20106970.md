# China – Large Appliances | Asia Pacific

# What's after the Rally

Stable earnings growth outlook, still resilient production data, relatively attractive valuation/yield, and favorable policy expectations have led large appliances stocks to outperform YTD. The above factors are likely to continue in the next few months, so we see upside support for valuation.

## Key Takeaways

■ We expect domestic demand growth to stay low at \~2% in value in 2024, without factoring in extra boost from potential subsidy support.

Concrete trade-in policies, with more details on subsidies when executed, would provide upside to our growth forecasts.

Leading players should continue to gain market share domestically. Overseas business is a stabilizing factor for growth and/or margin in 2024.

We expect minor margin upside, mainly from continued efficiency improvement.

Near-term catalysts: 1) follow-up on trade-in policies; 2) AC peak season sales trend (volume and pricing); 3) dividend payout; 4) overseas market development.

Backdrop: Large appliances stocks are up 10-28% YTD, outperforming MSCI China's -5% and the Chinese consumer stock average of -5%. We expect fundamental support in the next 1-2 quarters.

• Domestic: Recent production trends remain resilient, especially for air conditioners. Replacement demand would be the major driver. Potential subsidy policy execution could provide further buffer to property headwind (we expect a 12% yoy drop in property completions in 2H24, which will likely start to affect new demand of large appliances toward 4Q24/end-2024; new demand accounts for a smaller fraction of total demand than in previous years). Rational competitive strategy among leaders may protect margins.

• Overseas: Industry growth may improve slightly in 2024, to +0-2% yoy. We expect market share gain and efficiency improvement to drive profit growth.

We tweak our earnings forecasts for Midea, Gree, and Haier: Changes in basic EPS estimates: 0% to +2% for 2023, 0% to -2% for 2024, and 0% to -3% for 2025.

We maintain our stock ratings and raise price targets moderately: We employ the same relative valuation methodology, benchmarking against average historical P/Es. We increase target multiples slightly to reflect recent sector wide re-rating amid better earnings visibility and growth outlook.

Valuation after the rally: Forward P/Es are 15-25% below their average since 2017. We still see upside from current levels for Midea and Gree. See the following section for 2023 Earnings Preview and 2024 Outlook .

1. Low rural home appliance penetration rate vs. now,

2. Property up-cycle during the period, and

3. Major product advancements.

We don't expect similar level of stimulus from the current subsidy scheme, given that the backdrop factors have largely subsided. However, the subsidy scheme should still give an incremental boost to replacement demand.

## Headwinds for new demand – residential sales and completion trends deteriorating further in 2024

New homes: According to China Real Estate Information Corp (CRIC), new home sales in 50 cities have remained weak in 2024. For example, new unit sales surrounding the Chinese New Year Holiday period in 50 cities decreased 48% YoY vs. the similar period in 2023. Our property team for China forecasts that new home sales volume will decrease a further 4.5% yoy in 2024 (1H: -9%; 2H: flat yoy), but that's a narrowing decline vs. -8.5% in 2023.

Property completion: The peak of the property completion cycle may come in 1H24, according to our China property team, followed by slowdown in 3Q24 and a sharp decline in 4Q24. The team forecasts a 4.6% yoy decline in property completions in 2024 (1Q: +5%, 2Q: +15%, 3Q: flat, 4Q: -15% yoy), after strong growth of +16% yoy in 2023.

## Overseas outlook

Haier:: The company expects mid-single-digit (%) yoy revenue growth (in Rmb terms) in overseas markets in 2024, supported by better-than-feared low- to mid-single-digit growth in the US and double-digit growth in Europe and other regions (in local currencies). Market share gain and ASP lift from new product launches will be key for growth. It also sees profitability improvement in overseas markets in 2024, with the EU achieving break-even and US margin to expand.

Whirlpool (WHR.N, not covered): During its 4Q23 results briefing, the company guided for global large appliance industry growth of +0-2% yoy (vs. 2023 guidance at 0% yoy), with North America, Latin America, and Asia at +0-2%, 0-3%, and 4-6% yoy respectively. For its core market, the US, it expects strong replacement demand throughout 2024 given 4-5% installed base growth during 2015-17 and elevated appliance usage since 2020. Discretionary demand from existing home sales may also improve as mortgage rates may decline from 20-plus-year highs.

## What's Changed

## Midea

We update our forecasts by factoring in recent updates and macro trends. We keep 2023 revenue forecast unchanged, and trim our revenue estimates 1-2% for 2024-25, reflecting macro uncertainties in both domestic and international markets. We nudge up our NPM forecasts for 2023-25, driven by better GPM in 2023 and opex saving in 2024-25. As a result, our 2023 net profit estimate edges up 2%, while 2024 and 2025 EPS are unchanged. Considering share count adjustments, our diluted EPS estimate for 2023 rises 1%, while those 2024 and 2025 decline 1% and 2%, respectively.

We lift our price target from Rmb71 to Rmb79: We continue to apply SOTP methodology.

• Home appliances (and related 2B business): We lift our target P/E to 14x 2024e EPS (13x previously), which is slightly above the company's average at 13x since 2017, weighted by gross profit contribution, reflecting its high earnings visibility and potential upside from better To-business segment growth.

• Robots and automation (KUKA business): our estimated value of Rmb5 per share is the average value derived from EV/sales and EV/EBITDA. We continue to apply 1.1x 2024e EV/sales and 13x 2024e EV/EBITDA, similar to the global peer average, reflecting its strong global competitiveness (>80% of total revenue from overseas).

Our bull case scenario value is also lifted 11%, from Rmb83 to Rmb92, considering potential earnings upside from ToB business and consumer trade-in stimulus.

Our bear case scenario value is unchanged.

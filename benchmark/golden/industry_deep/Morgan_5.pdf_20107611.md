## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## SK hynix (000660.KS)

Base case, residual income model. We assume a cost of equity of 10.5% (4% risk-free rate, 6.5% risk premium, 1.0 beta) and a terminal growth rate of 5%.

## Risks to Upside

n DRAM and NAND pricing increases more than expected.

n AI-induced demand for HBM, DRAM and NAND by hyperscalers stronger than expected.

n Edge AI memory content growth accelerates.

## Risks to Downside

n End demand may turn weaker than expected.

n Rising competition for DDR5 may result in overspending on the supply side.

n Inventories remain elevated at cloud and Chinese smartphone customers.

## Tokyo Electron (8035.T)

P/E is calculated using the average forward P/E based on next-FY estimates during the previous cycle recovery phase (August 2019 to May 2022), which is 20.0x, and adjusting for an additional average 1.5% of annual sales growth if a 35% share of etchers is achieved by 2035 brings this to 28.5x. Our EPS estimate base year is F3/26 (¥1,616.2).

## Risks to Upside

n Surpassing expectations, smartphone demand recovers, driving semiconductor demand. Manufacturers invest in flash memory and adopt cryo-etching equipment for mass production

## Risks to Downside

n US-China trade tensions restrict export of semiconductor manufacturing equipment to China, competitors release competitive cryo-etcher products, reduction of coater developer market with dry resist technology adoption.

## 群联 (8299.TWO)

Base case, residual income model. Key assumptions: a cost of equity of 9.0%, a medium-term growth rate of 6.5%, payout ratio of 40%, and a terminal growth rate of 4.0%.

## Risks to Upside

n Faster-than-expected eMMC controller IC outsourcing rate

n Faster SSD market growth

n Market share gain in ultrabooks and SSDs

## Risks to Downside

n Weaker-than expected NAND demand

n Milder impact on global NAND supply

n Non-acceptance of price hikes on NAND modules

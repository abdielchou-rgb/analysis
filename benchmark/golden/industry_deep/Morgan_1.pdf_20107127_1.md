# Positions of Active Long-Only Asia/EM Managers

We publish our March 2024 Asia/EM positioning guide to summarize allocation changes at the stock, country and sector level over time from a regional perspective. On top of that, we share the distribution of active weights and compare the allocation difference between US and EU managers.

## Key Takeaways

3 consecutive months of EM passive ETF inflow. Outflow from active increased to \$1.5bn in Feb from \$0.5bn in Jan

TSMC led weight additions in Feb and YTD, active overweight increased to 3.3% in Feb

■ GEM active managers added further to Taiwan but reduced YTD net add in Korea and China. India and Brazil o/w are trimmed further

GEM active managers increased allocation to Info Tech but reduced YTD net add to Discretionary

In Japan, active managers continued to u/w Automobiles and trimmed Capital goods. They added Staples, Real Estate, Telecom and Transportation.

## Focus of The Month - More bullish on Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment:

According to our bottom-up fund position analysis of the largest 80 EM active longonly mutual funds (with average fund AUM at US\$4.1bn), the dominating rebalancing activities among these managers were adding TSMC ( Exhibit 1 ) resulting in a higher Taiwan and Info Tech active weight. The trade was funded by a reduction of YTD net add to China and Korea and further reduction in their overweight positions in India and Brazil. At the Sector/Industry level, they added to Semiconductors & Semiconductor Equipment and reduced YTD net add to Automobiles & Components and Consumer Services ( Exhibit 10 ).

Distribution of China active weights shifted left among active GEM funds, with the estimated simple average underweight up from 1.2% in January to 1.7% in February 2024. However, it is still materially lower than that of 3.2% underweight in December 2023.

Coverage: Our US fund universe in this analysis is formed by taking the 40 largest active funds in "US Funds: Morningstar Emerging Markets Equity", covering total AUM of US \$248bn.

Our SICAV fund universe in this analysis is formed by taking the 40 largest active funds in "International Funds: Morningstar Emerging Markets Equity", covering total AUM of US \$80bn.

# Tracking China’s Semi Localization | Asia Pacific

# China local CPU adoption; Big IC Fund injection

## Key Takeaways

China government PC demand is c.10% in China, or 2% globally, thus limited impact to Intel/AMD.

Three key themes of SEMICON China 2024: China localization, advanced packaging, SiC.

## China CPU

• China blocks use of Intel and AMD chips in government computers. China has introduced new guidelines that will mean US microprocessors from Intel and AMD are phased out of government PCs and servers, as Beijing ramps up a campaign to replace foreign technology with homegrown solutions. (Nikkei, Mar 25, 2024) (link). We think it is a reiteration of previous policy – please refer to our research China's Localization: Computing from the ground up (28 Jun 2022) for the background and our related forecasts. The impact to global semi vendors could be limited, as China government PC demand is c.10% in China, or 2% globally.

## China government fund and equipment trade-show

• China readies US\$27bn fund: China is reportedly in the process of establishing a third-phase of its Big IC Fund, with plans to inject US\$27bn in funding aimed at supporting top-tier technology development, as per Bloomberg’s report. The goal is said to be enhancing China’s semiconductor self-sufficiency and overcoming export restriction measures imposed by the US. (Bloomberg, Mar 8, 2024) (link)

• SEMICON China 2024 key themes: China localization, advanced packaging, SiC: We attended SEMICON China 2024 and saw local equipment vendors like NAURA with heavy visitor traffic around its exhibition booths. Heterogeneous Integration (Advanced Packaging) conferences were full house. China SiC development is well on track with 16 Chinese companies working on 8-inch SiC substrates. (link)

Stock implications: Local PC/CPU/OS are good enough for China government administrative affairs, but consumers or enterprise users may demand better quality (details). We believe China's semi equipment vendors like NAURA, AMEC and ACM Research will be beneficiaries of the third-phase big IC fund. SICC is our preferred pick among SiC Substrate suppliers.

## China Localization Progress

We expect China's semiconductor self-sufficiency ratio to reach 25% by 2025e, up 7ppt from 18% in 2022. Upstream vendors like equipment suppliers and EDA are likely to achieve a higher self-sufficiency ratio, as these are the chokepoints in China's semi localization and local development of these industries has become a strategic imperative for China. We are positive on the self-sufficiency ratio of semiconductor components increasing using mature nodes like image sensors and power semis given local foundry capacity expansion, while we are relatively cautious on logic semis using advanced nodes, like CPUs and GPUs, given US export restrictions.

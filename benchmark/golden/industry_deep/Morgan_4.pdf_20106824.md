# Hong Kong/China Transportation & Infrastructure | Asia Pacific

# Week in Review (Issue 9-24)

February 2024 in review: we stay positive on Spring Airlines, ZTO and tanker shipping.

## China Air Travel:

• Airlines were +3.1% in February vs. HSI +6.6% and SSEC +8.1%. Airports recorded +13.1% in February, total January pax of HMIA, GBIA, Shanghai (PVG+SHA) and BCIA, recovered to 120%, 104%, 97% and 63%, respectively, of their Jan-19 levels. Spring Airlines outperformed with 9.6% monthly return in airlines, and HMIA outperformed with 26.7% return in airports.

• Air travel demand rose during CNY, but normalized after the holiday, and may bring pressure to yields. We continue to favor Spring Airlines the most in our airlines coverage.

## Shipping:

• Containers were +5.3% in Feb-23: Shanghai Containerized Freight Index dropped by 8.6% after the CNY holiday, led by corrections on both US and Europe routs. We expect the spot correction to continue, before we see a robust resumption in China's export demand.

• Tankers were +5.6% in this month: VLCC rates rallied in the first half of February, likely driven by a potential shortage of supply in the Mideast Gulf. We stay with our high conviction in a multiyear tanker up-cycle. OW CSE-H and CMES.

• Bulkers +5.5% in February: We observed continuously increasing time charter rates and asset prices for bulkers. Spot market picked up robustly post Chinese New Year. Pacific Basin's 2023 operating results beat our estimate slightly, and management holds a positive outlook on the segment.

Express and Logistics +8.9% in February. The strong growth in parcel volume during the Chinese New Year, along with the healthier-than-expected January data, supported market sentiment. ZTO outperformed with +17.3% monthly return. We continue to favor ZTO for its leadership in China's parcel segment consolidation, solid earnings growth track record, best-in-class management quality and efforts to increase shareholder returns. We have also moved SF to EW due to the presence of macro risks and increased competition.

## Data Observations:

• 2024 Spring Rush: 2024 Spring Festival total pax traffic during the first 35 days increased 8% vs. 2019. Total pax growth vs. 2019 has turned negative after the holiday. Highway traffic has shifted from public transportation to private cars after the Covid-19 pandemic.

## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## China Merchants Port Holdings (0144.HK)

Sum of the parts, probability-weighted: 10% bull, 70% base, 20% bear. We see a potential demand weakness amid macro headwinds and low visibility of tariff hikes, with some possibility for our bull case to develop in view of faster-than-expected export growth. We apply DCF models (WACC: 12.6%, terminal growth rate: 0%) or P/E multiples of 6-10x to value most of CMP's port projects.

## Risks to Upside

n Higher-than-expected container traffic growth.

n Higher-than-expected ASP growth.

## Risks to Downside

n Worse-than-expected slowdown in container traffic growth in response to trade tensions and depressed global economic growth.

n ASP declines.

n US-China trade tensions escalate again.

## COSCO SHIPPING Energy Transportation (1138.HK)

Price/book, probability-weighted 15% bull, 70% base, 15% bear. The stronger-than-expected spot market has increased our confidence in the tanker bull cycle.

Target 2024e P/B multiples:

Base - 1.2x (0.6 SD above historical mean since 2009)

Bull - 1.7x (1.7 SD above historical mean since 2009)

Bear - 0.8x (0.2 SD below historical mean since 2009)

## Risks to Upside

n Crude oil contango

n More crude oil shipping demand thanks to production increase from crude producers

n Longer shipment distances

## Risks to Downside

n Weaker global economy leading to lower crude demand

n Lower-than-expected crude oil output from OPEC

n The return of shadow fleets

## China Merchants Energy Shipping Co. Ltd. (601872.SS)

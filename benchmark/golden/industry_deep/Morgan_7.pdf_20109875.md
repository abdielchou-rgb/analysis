# US Rate Volatility Screener

We analyze price action in US interest rate volatility markets, particularly in OTC (vanilla, curve, and forward vol) and CBOT options. Data are as of August 15, 2024.

## Key Takeaways

The vol surface was lower this week, led by upper left products

Both expiry and tail term structures steepened

一 Curve vol was lower, yet continues to screen rich on an I/R basis

Forward vol was lower, and screens cheap to midcurves

Implieds on V4 expiry CBOT options decrease, but remain above the 75thpercentile of realized vol

## Vanilla vol surface

• The vol surface was lower this week, led by upper left products (see Exhibit 2 and Exhibit 3 )

• On 1y lookbacks, upper table products are at the upper end of their ranges. Vega products are near the lower end of their range over the past 1m (see Exhibit 7 )

• On an implied/realized basis for a 1w lookback, the surface screens rich. On a 1m lookback, 1y and 2y tails are trading below parity (see Exhibit 9 , Exhibit 11 , Exhibit 13 )

• Vols with <3m expiries have negative vol roll-down across tails, most notably so for right side tails

## US Equity Strategy Roadmap - June 2024

## TECH+ Fuels S&P 500 Advance; NVDA Drives TECH+

The S&P 500 rose 5.0% in May, 11.3% YTD. Large Growth outperformed significantly, helped by superior earnings growth in the largest Tech stocks. Excluding NVDA, S&P 500 performance drops to 7.8%. Large Value and the equal-weighted S&P 500 underperformed YTD, returning 3.2% and 2.8%.

## Momentum and Growth Define Factor Leadership

High EPS Growth and Momentum stocks have delivered particularly strong returns YTD, as have those experiencing strong Price Momentum. Companies benefiting from higher interest rates and GDP also outperformed over this period.

## 1Q Delivers Strong Results

1Q EPS grew 10.7% on 3.8% revenues. However, growth between groups varied significantly with TECH+ EPS expanding 40.2% and Energy/Materials contracting 22.7%. 2Q EPS is expected to grow 10.5%, but is likely to come in stronger once beats are taken into account.

## S&P 500 to 5600 on Profit Strength

On May 28, we increased our 2024 S&P 500 target to 5600 from 5400, 6.1% upside through year end. We also increased our 2024-25 EPS estimates to \$245 from \$240, and to \$260 from \$255, implying 11.4% and 6.1% growth over the next 2 years (vs. consensus estimates of 11.1% and 14.0%).

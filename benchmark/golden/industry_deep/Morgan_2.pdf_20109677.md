# How Willing Is Your Bank to Pay Up for Deposits? July 2024

In line with commentary from 2Q24 earnings that deposit rates have stabilized, rates on new CD offers across all durations remained relatively unchanged in the month of July: During July the average highest rate and 1-12 month rates were both flat, and the highest 13-36 month rate increased 1 bp to 3.26%. Among the primarily online deposit gatherers (ALLY, AXP, COF, DFS, and SYF), the average highest CD rate increased 8 bps from 4.84% to 4.92%, the average online savings rate was flat at 4.56%.

Positive Deposit Trends pave the way for NII/NIM inflection in 2025: At 2Q24 earnings, we heard generally positive commentary on both deposit mix and pricing trends which should begin to pave the path for NIM/NII inflection. On pricing, IB deposit pressure continues to slow, up on average only +4bps q/q in 2Q24 vs +9bps q/q in 1Q24. On mix, Avg NIB outflows decelerated, down on average -1% q/q in 2Q24 vs -4% q/q in 1Q24. We expect these positive trends should continue as rates come down, driving lower deposit costs and an inflection in NIM and NII through the end of the year. Given NII is 80% of revenues for Midcap Banks, lower cost of funds driving higher NIM/NII in a falling rate environment is a key driver in our recent upgrade of the Midcap Bank industry from 'In-Line' to 'Attractive'. More details on our Midcap Banks industry upgrade here.

• Highest CD Rates: OZK is offering its highest CD rate for maturities under 36 months at 5.40%, followed by FHN and GS at 5.15%.

• Lowest CD Rates: MTB, FITB and CIT are offering their highest CD rate for maturities under 36 months at 4.25%, 4.20% and 3.50%, respectively.

• Largest Increases: DFS increased its 1-12 month and highest rate paid by 40 bps, BAC/RF increased their 13-36 month rate by 25 bps.

• Largest Decreases: ZION decreased its 1-12 month and highest rate paid by 25 bps, NYCB decreased its 13-36 month rate by 15 bps.

• Online Rates: For banks offering online rates, we use the higher of each bank's money market or online savings rates. All banks kept their online rates flat in the last two weeks.

Investment recommendations: Our Overweight-rated banks (in order) are C, GS, BAC, WFC, RF, and JPM for Large Cap Banks, MTB, HBAN, EWBC, and PB for Midcap Banks, and ALLY and SLM in Consumer Finance.

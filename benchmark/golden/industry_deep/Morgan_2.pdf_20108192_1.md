# Global FX Positioning: New Short JPY Positions

In the week ending Friday, May 10, options pricing data indicate that investors added long NOK (versus EUR) and NZD positions, and increased short JPY and AUD positions. In the futures market, USD shorts were added against JPY and GBP, while investors increased short USD (DXY) and CHF in the week ending Tuesday, May 7 Exhibit 4 . Overall, options data suggest that tactical investors are long AUD, while being short SEK (versus EUR) and JPY. Positioning in the futures market remains long EUR and is short CAD and AUD.

# Global Rates Strategy

# Rates Map: Slow pace towards consequential cuts

## United States - Stay long US 2y and flatter 30y US swap spreads

10y US yields broke below 4.20% on US CPI but stronger signs of a US slowdown are needed to move substantially lower. UBS now expects a first cut of 25 bps in September, but it will be hard to get 50 bps priced as the FOMC should begin clearer communication on the path ahead (Figure 3). In June, Chair Powell said that the start of monetary policy easing would be “a consequential decision for the economy” but the market is waiting for further guidance.

The equity selloff after US CPI was concerning given the rally in US rates (Figure 4). But UBS tactical indicators do not point to further weakness as US net financial assets rose again (Figure 5).

 We still prefer bull steepeners with 10y10y US Tsy yields close to 5% (Figure). Attention turns to election trades (bear steepeners, long forward vol, etc) even if wide deficits did not prevent the US growth slowdown to a sub-2% pace. 10y US swap spreads have moved to new lows, but we think there remains room to run to -90bp.

## Euro area - Receive Dec 25, higher Z5Z7 spread, and reach for yield in summer trading

We are still recommending clients to receive the Dec'25 ECB meeting and expect further steepening in Dec 27 vs Dec 25 as more cuts are priced but concerns on a higher level of short-term neutral rates and budget deficits persist (Figure 8). ECB GC members warned just yesterday that lax fiscal policy make it harder to send a rate cut signal (Figure 2).

The reach for yield in anticipation of less volatile summer trading may have arrived. We had been waiting for levels above 140 bps to buy 10y Italy vs Germany, but we now expect the spread to test 120 bps over the summer. We estimate that supply will remain a supporting factor for Italy and Spain beyond summer. Our views on France are little changed. We see 10y France versus Germany spreads falling to \~60 bps in case of progress on a 2025 budget but spreads at \~70 bps or higher if the formation of a new government drags.

## United Kingdom - Steepening trend in front-end to persist

 We still like our Aug/Nov flattener and to receive SONIA 1y1y. The UK June CPI print and labour market report are crucial inputs for the August MPC meeting. UBS expects a tick-up in headline inflation in June to 2.1% (consensus: 2% y/y), largely driven by a slight increase in core (+0.1pp to 3.6% y/y; consensus: 3.5%).

Relatively hawkish comments from Huw Pill, combined with the departure of deputy governor Broadbent, who never dissented from Governor Bailey, and will be replaced by Clare Lombardelli, put the August cut at odds.

 Gilt issuance in the short and medium sectors and lower rates volatility will drive the gilt ASW curve steeper.

## Switzerland - SARON 5y5y near target and looking to pay 1y1y

 SARON 5y5y is close to our 1% target at 1.09%. We would look to tactically pay the SARON 1y1y rate below the 0.80% level, after the 5 bps drop to 0.85% last week.

 We expect the SNB to maintain a dovish stance given CHF strength and inflation in line with SNB forecasts. We do not see a strong catalyst to enter ESTR vs SARON

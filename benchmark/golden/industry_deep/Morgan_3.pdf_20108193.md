# Monthly Perspectives: What's next after Momentum Reversal?

Asia/EM equities were led by high volatility and weak momentum stocks MTD, which we think will be short-lived. We suggest adding to Quality and Revisions exposure post recent price action. In Japan, Value underperformed MTD due to FX volatility and we expect a bumpy uptrend for the factor ahead.

QUANTWISE

## Key Takeaways

Factor performance continued to be dominated by technical rather than fundamental factors in April.

Another round of momentum reversal in Asia/EM/China, which saw stocks with high volatility, weak momentum and high short interest outperform.

In Japan, Value underperformed given Yen strength in May and slight momentum drawdown.

Our model recommends refocusing on fundamentals, such as Quality and Earnings Revision, to achieve long-term alpha and expects Momentum to regain traction.

Market dynamics: Another round of repricing due to changing US Fed rate scenarios saw UST 10Y yields drop towards 4.5% amid softer-than-expected US data. The market is pricing in 1-2 rate cuts in 2024, with the first cut likely in September. The latest US economic release reduced uncertainty in yields and saw the MOVE index trend below 100. VIX was down to 12.55 and we see a pickup in risk sentiment in Asia, according to our Morgan Stanley Asia Risk Sentiment Indicator ( Exhibit 8 ).

The recent China rally remains the key debate among investors, and our factor monitor ( Exhibit 1 ) shows that: 1) high beta, 2) high volatility, 3) weak momentum and 4) stocks with high short interest outperforming in China's offshore market. The underlying companies show a natural sector skew towards the Consumer Discretionary and Real Estate sectors. From a Quant perspective, the rallies in technical factors tend to be short-lived unless they are significant and can sustain changes in macro fundamentals. Please also refer to our latest report: Thoughts on the recent Rally for details incorporating our fundamental perspectives and in a global context.

## Market Dynamics and Style Implications

## Latest Style Performance Overview in Asia/EM

Stocks with high beta and weak momentum led outperformance in APxJ/EM MTD, which is unlikely to be sustained: This is because the current market dynamic is being driven more by flows from short-term investors, such as hedge funds and trend-following investors. The high volatility earlier this year in China saw long-short hedge fund managers de-gross their portfolios and long-only managers reduce their China underweights around February 2024. This was followed by US and Japan market reversal in April, forming a market backdrop for investors with trend-following strategies to step in and build long beta exposure in China.

Our factor monitor shows that technical factors dominate YTD ( Exhibit 2 ) - High beta and weak momentum stocks outperformed in APxJ/EM. We see similar behavior in China with ( Exhibit 3 ) fundamental factor performance such as Value, Growth and Quality detracting, while high Beta and weak Momentum stocks outperformed. We see early signs that this is slowing in China offshore and expect the trend to reverse ( Exhibit 1 ).

We recommend investors refocus on Quality and Earnings Revisions that deliver longterm alpha, and remain neutral on Value vs. Growth.

## Macro & Market Dynamics That Matter

The charts and paragraphs below illustrate our latest views on market dynamics and style recommendations.

i) Value has underperformed Growth since 19 April as UST 10Y Yield eased to 4.5% ( Exhibit 6 ): UST 10Y yield has eased since softer-than-expected US economic data, especially the Non-farm payroll miss of 175k vs market expectations of 235k. Volatility in yield decreased. Value style benchmarks underperformed their equivalent Growth version by 0.4-2.5%, this was most severe in China given the recent high volatility and beta rally.

Although Value underperformed, we recommend investors stay neutral between Growth and Value as our Fed Pivot Playbook for Asia/EM equities suggests a muted Value vs Growth relative performance in historical soft landing scenarios. We would need to see a more sustained downtrend in UST 10Y yields before revisiting this idea. We see better timing in Q4 this year.

In Japan, we see muted relative performance between MSCI Japan Value vs Growth but we do observe some early signs of Value weakness in our factor monitors. We think the strong Yen amid BOJ intervention together with a fall in US 10Y yields contributed to the move.

## Factor Monitor – Monthly Update May 10, 2024

## Methodology

Factor return calculation: To evaluate historical factor performance, we rank stocks as at month-end within defined universes, and form quintile portfolios based on the underlying factor values. We go long the top quintile and short the bottom quintile (i.e., long the top 20th percentile of stocks within the defined universe and short those in the bottom 20th percentile), rebalanced monthly. We then calculate the performance of the time series of this L/S market-neutral portfolio over various time horizons.

Composite formation: To calculate the composite factors, we first z-score the individual factor values at each monthly observation within the defined universe. Individual factor zscores are then averaged into composite scores for each stock on an equal-weighted basis.

# Taiwan Equity Strategy

# Be opportunistic if summer fades the recent rally

## H224 focus: AI tech, new products, cyclical recovery

Taiex rallied to a record high in the first three weeks of June as Computex provided a clearer roadmap for AI products and applications into 2025/26 to a broader group of investors and generalists, beyond the usual tech specialists. Institutional investor trading picked up, concentrated on the four largest stocks by market cap (Top Four): TSMC, MediaTek, Hon Hai and Quanta. Following the recent rally and slower pace of events over the summer, we could see some pause in momentum although it should not be too sharp. We believe potential earnings upgrades around the next earnings season with tech monthly sales fairly strong through May, as well as the rollout of new products (Blackwell, AI PC/smartphone, iPhone 16) at the end of Q3, might continue to support the index. We stay positive on the cloud AI supply chain and incrementally more positive on mature foundry and AI smartphones. For non-tech, we remain positive on telco, textile and bikes, but turn neutral on financials as the insurers' book value recovery, driven by strong equity markets, seems largely in the price.

## Possible upward earnings revisions

Post the Q124 results beat, MSCI Taiwan consensus earnings estimates were raised 2- 3%. We think consensus earnings could be revised up a further 2-3% as April and May sales are both tracking above seasonal: 69% of Q224 consensus estimates versus seasonal 65%. Tech sectors are tracking ahead of consensus, led by foundry, hardware, and components, while non-tech sectors are largely in line. Export order growth, which tracks closely with Taiwan monthly sales momentum, slowed to 7% YoY in May versus 10% in April, mainly due to a decline in IC design orders. Export orders for AI applications/cloud AI and foundry (HPC) remain robust. Chemicals and machinery orders also improved in May, according to the Ministry of Economy Affairs.

## Taiex not expensive if we exclude the Top Four; lifting our Taiex target range

If we exclude the Top Four, the Taiex is trading at 17x 2025E PE (0-1SD above 26-year average), while the Top 4 have re-rated to +1SD as tech AI drives above-trend growth. We raise our end-2024 Taiex target from 19,125 to 23,915 as we roll over to 2025E earnings with PE multiple unchanged at 14.5x for companies excluding the Top Four, and include the market cap of the Top Four based on UBS price targets. Our new index target implies 16.8x 2025E PE, at +1SD above its 26-year average. We think this is not stretched considering a 26% earnings CAGR during 2024-25E, versus the 26-year average of 14%. Our upside/downside scenario range is changed to 25,420/21,305, versus 21,670/18,175 previously.

## Changes to our most/least preferred stocks

We add MediaTek (edge AI), Nanya Tech (prolonged memory upcycle), ASMedia (content gain in AMD), and UMC (firming margins on a rising utilization rate and sharply lagging peers) to our most preferred list. We replace Global Unichip with Alchip on more attractive valuation and remove Novatek, Gigabyte, Fubon Financial (approaching price target) from our most preferred list. For the least preferred list, we add Vanguard (new capacity resulting in weaker margin) and Realtek (increasing competition).

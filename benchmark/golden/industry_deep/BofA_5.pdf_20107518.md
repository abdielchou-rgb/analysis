# Chart book series #139: Mar excavator sales improved despite weak construction

## A better consumption recovery during Qingming holiday

Both number of tourists and tourism revenue (112% and 113% of 2019 level, Exhibit 6) came in at above pre-Covid level during the Qingming holiday (Apr 4-6). The implied percapita tourism spending has also surpassed the pre-Covid level for the first time, suggesting a resilient recovery in services consumption. Box office revenue also rose during Qingming holiday (Exhibit 7). For means of travel, highway and railway saw a better recovery in passenger volume (Exhibit 5). According to CAAC, scheduled international flights in China is expected to retore to 85% of 2019's level in the next six months, higher than the current recovery ratio of sub-70% (Exhibit 4).

## Domestic excavator sales improved in March

According to CCMA, monthly domestic excavator sales increased to 15,188 units (+9.3% yoy), the highest level in 2 years. This is usually a leading indicator for construction demand, and to be a key to watch if the sales improvement can be sustained. That said, both rebar and coal consumption edged down sequentially (Exhibit 9 & Exhibit 10), while cement shipment ratio inched down amid weakened demand negatively impacted by rainy weather in the past week (Exhibit 8). China Container freight index trended down further last week (Exhibit 12). LGSB issuance pace remained below par level (Exhibit 13).

## Home sales weakened amid holiday

New home sales in key cities dipped during the Qingming holiday (Exhibit 14). While that of secondary home sales also softened (Exhibit 15). That said, land auction price premium improved sequentially in the past weeks (Exhibit 16).

## Long-end treasury yield lower; CNY remained weak

Domestic market liquidity remained largely stable (Exhibit 19), while both 10-year and 30-year treasury bond yield inched down last week (Exhibit 17). USDCNY continued to remain weak at above 7.2 level with a strong daily fixing from the PBoC (Exhibit 18).

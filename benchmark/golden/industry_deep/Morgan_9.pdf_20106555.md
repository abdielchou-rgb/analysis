# Sustainability Fund Flows Turn Positive in 4Q23

Flows into Sustainability funds domiciled in APAC turned positive to US\$1.6bn in 4Q23 (from outflows of US\$0.8bn in 3Q23), but were still down by 53% YoY. Taiwan and Australia contributed to most inflows in 4Q23. Flows into Fixed Income funds rose by 9x YoY in 4Q23.

![](images/453b43ebbd73ec5078b12a6c7ccdceb8cf199afbbfe2ebe8fdf2102f4df12e4b.jpg)

SustainabilityS

## Key Takeaways

■ Flows into Sustainability funds domiciled in APAC turned positive to US\$1.6bn in 4Q23 (from outflows of US\$0.8bn in 3Q23, or -53% YoY).

Taiwan contributed to the most inflows of US\$1.6bn, followed by Australia at US \$0.4bn in 4Q23. On the contrary, China recorded outflows of US\$0.4bn in 4Q23.

By category, flows into Fixed Income Sustainability funds domiciled in APAC came in highest at US\$1.8bn in 4Q23 (up by 9x from US\$0.2bn in 4Q22).

一 54% of the funds domiciled in Asia conducted 'general ESG investment' by Morningstar definition, followed by 46% adopting thematic investing.

Fund launches in APAC exhibit similar trends, although the number of fund launches declined by two-fifths YoY in 2023.

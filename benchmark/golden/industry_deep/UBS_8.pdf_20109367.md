# How should investors reallocate their portfolios this summer to maximize carry?

Details on the methodology/model are in our report - What is the optimal way to add income to Multi-Asset portfolios amidst heightened uncertainty?

We expect relatively rangebound spreads in Q3, with slight tightening bias in Europe

After the recent spike in volatility triggered by the French elections, we retraced most of the move in EU IG (as expected), but uncertainties around US growth and earnings keep EU HY spreads slightly wider. In our baseline, we see a slight outperformance for EU HY and a full retracement to early June levels over the next few months. In Europe, the core component points to some stalling in disinflation progress but long term inflation expectations remaining well anchored should keep spreads close to current levels. In the US, we see rangebound spreads with strong total returns as inflation and growth move sequentially lower through the summer. Short-term focus remains on Q2 earnings especially on guidance in cyclicals to gauge how much slowing growth is feeding into profit outlooks. In the US, we expect good enough earnings for credit – up \~10-11% for large caps. In Europe, the focus should remain on growth recovery in the manufacturing sectors. As we seem to slowly shift to a new macro environment, we highlight our best carry allocation/trade ideas to implement for Q2’24: front-end of credit curves remains the sweet spot, GBP IG 5-7Yr screens attractive and is a great source of diversification, US credit duration looks appealing for the first time in almost 24m, the model suggests coming back to neutral on EM.

## Where is the carry? We compare C/V ratios by various measures across regions

In yield terms, the front-end of the EU IG universe still looks extremely cheap given the inversion of the curve but even more so as both the French elections outcome and US CPI pushed volatility back in a lower range. In spread terms, the model still suggests the front-end is the most attractive part of the credit curve. Having said that, the model now also suggests the US IG 7-10Yr bucket is attractive from a carry perspective for the first time in almost 24 months. GBP IG remains a great source of diversification with a low correlation to S&P performance and a 12m breakeven around \~50bp. In the CDS space, ITRX Main looks cheaper than ITRX Xover from a carry perspective relative to history, and also has the highest 10yr z-score across the board at 0.9 (followed by EU HY 1-3y at 0.78). Overall, cash still looks more attractive than CDS.

## What is the optimized carry allocation suggested by the model today?

Every 3 months, our model suggests the best allocation/reallocation to implement to optimize the carry of a multi-asset portfolio. Here's what the model suggests today: - For Cash + Synthetic investors, the model suggests (Figure 11Optimzed Porfl anc vs.Bhk 2014 (+x) to (i) increase risk exposure in US IG 3-5Yr; (ii) reduce risk exposure in EU HY 3-5Yr; (iii) reduce risk exposure in ITRX Main; (iv) cover the short in EM and bring exposure back to benchmark weight; (v) max out risk exposure in GBP IG 5-7Yr.

\- For Synthetic Only investors, the model suggests (Figure 12 to (i) increase risk exposure in EM as well; (ii) remain long HY vs. IG in both region; (iii) reduce risk exposure in ITRX Main.

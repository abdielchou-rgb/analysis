## Summary of Our Views: Japan and India Still Top Picks in a New Era for Asia Equities

Japan’s ROE journey: ROE is up 2.5x since 2012 to around 10% and our base case is 12% by 2025 with 1.8x P/B. We forecast robust EPS growth of 9% in 2024 and 9% in 2025, assuming our FX strategists’ USD/JPY forecast of 140 for 4Q24. We look for the market to re-rate to 15.0x, supported by strong foreign interest and stock buybacks (and potentially NISA flows) for a base case Topix target at end-2024 of 2,800. In the most recent quarter earnings grew by 20%Y with an all-time high quantum of buybacks at JPY 9.4 trillion.

India’s decade: Multipolar world trends are supporting FDI and portfolio flows, while positive demographics and success in macro-stability underpin a strong capex and profit outlook. We see a secular trend towards sustained superior USD EPS growth versus EM over the cycle, with a young demographic profile supporting equity inflows. Valuations are at a premium (circa 20.0x forward P/E), but this is warranted by superior EPS CAGR (most recent quarter +23%Y).

China’s deflationary challenge: ROE has fallen steadily in recent years and earnings have missed for nine straight quarters. More forceful countercyclical measures are needed to boost demand given an incipient balance sheet recession due to headwinds from property and local government austerity. Rebalancing towards household consumption is needed given that an export-oriented approach faces risks from trade tensions.

Sector and thematic views: We still like Korea and Taiwan Semis into an expected 2024 recovery as well as AI-related demand, Financials in India, Indonesia and Singapore, Industrials in India and Mexico and Consumer Discretionary in India.

Quant and style preference: We are neutral on Value versus Growth in APxJ/EM to balance our house view on the macro factor of US yield moderation, but with the US dollar likely well supported. We recently turned negative on momentum.

P/B vs. ROE for Global Equities – Japan and China ROE Are Heading in Opposite Directions; for the US and India the Debate Is on Valuation

## Key Takeaways

• Cross-asset strategy: Recent bumpy data have not changed our base case assumptions and we continue to prefer fixed income over equities. But this doesn’t mean we don’t see opportunities in stocks – our most preferred equity markets currently are Japan, India, Mexico and Brazil, all of which benefit from strong secular trends.

• Japan and India to be outperformers in a new era for Asian equities: We forecast robust EPS growth of 9% in 2024 and 9% in 2025 for Japan and see the market re-rating to 15.0x on the back of strong flows and stock buybacks. We expect Indian equities to be supported by secular themes of the multipolar world, positive demographics and macro-stability. Our base case Topix target is 2,800 and Sensex at 74,000 by end-2024.

• Mexico and Brazil our preferred plays in LatAm: LatAm equities look cheap, but this is a function of risks from declining growth and higher rates. What we want to lean into are secular themes – e.g., nearshoring in Mexico and expansion of energy and agriculture exports for Brazil. Our base case MSCI LatAm target is 2,900 by end-2024.

• Sector and thematic views: Korea and Taiwan Semis, Financials in India, Indonesia and Singapore, Industrials in India and Consumer Discretionary in India. For LatAm, we like Info Tech, Energy, Real Estate and Utilities at the sector level.

## AI's Impact on S&P 500 Margins

## Methodology

1. Isolated periods with a combination of rising tech spending, rising productivity growth and a subsequent rise in profit margins.

2. Leveraging the historical precedent, we calculated the predicted impact of techdriven productivity growth on margins beyond what the normal business cycle explained.

## Key Takeaways

Early-to-mid 1990s appear to be the most compelling historical analogy.

2. Change in profit margins tends to lag tech-driven productivity growth by 1 year.

3. Tech capex has been productivity enhancing; non-tech capex has not.

4. We see AI-driven productivity adding 30 bps to 2025 net margin for the S&P 500; risk is skewed to the upside/our bull case—50 bps of added impact.

# Where Are We Trading Now #24: JD/YMM Results, Temu, Recruitment, Parcel

JD/YMM reported 4Q23 results this week. As of Feb, Temu's app reached 163mn MAU and 401mn accumulated downloads globally. Zhaopin and Boss Zhipin's data show resilient recruitment demand post CNY. Parcel volume +21% yoy YTD.

JD 4Q23 Results: In 4Q23, JD's revenue beat consensus by 2% and non-GAAP NP beat consensus by 16%. JD announced a US\$1.2bn cash dividend (24% payout ratio, vs. US\$1bn annual cash dividend and 24% payout ratio in 2022) and another US\$3bn share buyback effective until March 2027. For 2024, management remained confident JD's 1P/3P/total GMV and JD/JDR's revenue growth will outpace China's total retail sales (6% yoy, per Bloomberg consensus). It aims to at least maintain the absolute amount of JDR OP and sees group-level NP flattish yoy in 2024. It said it is committed to its annual cash dividend program and more aggressive 2024 buybacks than in 2023.

YMM 4Q23 Results: In 4Q23, FTA's total revenue grew 25% yoy, 5% above consensus, driven by fulfilled orders up 41% yoy. FTA's non-GAAP NP reached Rmb729mn (15% above Bloomberg consensus) and implied a 30.3% non-GAAP NPM (vs. 23.1% in 4Q22). For 2024, We model total revenue to grow 27% yoy, non-GAAP NP to reach Rmb3.6bn (NPM at 33.4% vs. 33.0% in 2023). For 1Q24, FTA guided for total revenue to grow 24-27% yoy to Rmb2.11-2.16bn. We model non-GAAP NP to reach Rmb628mn (NPM at 28.9%).

Temu 's Feb Sensor Tower data: Temu app's MAU reached 163.1mn globally – with 47.3mn or 29% from the US (72% of Amazon's US MAU) and 60.6mn or 37% from Europe; monthly average DAU was 19.9mn (vs. 20.0mn in Jan); monthly downloads were 29.3mn (vs. 36.6mn in Jan, 6.9mn or 24% from the US); as of Feb the Temu app had accumulated 401.3mn downloads globally (151.5mn or 38% from the US).

Zhilian Zhaopin: In the 2nd week post CNY, the number of job postings grew 8% WoW and the number of submitted CVs grew 40% WoW. Enterprises with smaller scale saw faster WoW growth in job postings; by industry, advertising/exhibitions/ PR, logistics/warehousing and entertainment/sports/leisure saw the highest wow growth in job postings, at 17%, 14% and 14% wow, respectively ( Exhibit 18 ).

Boss Zhipin's IOS grossing: Per Sensor Tower, Kanzhun's (BZ.O) IOS grossing in the 19 days post CNY (Feb 16-Mar 5 2024 vs. Jan 28-Feb 15 2023) grew 61% yoy ( Exhibit 21 ), suggesting strong recruitment demand for SMEs.

Parcel volume + 20.9% yoy YTD as of Mar 3 ( Exhibit 3 ).

# ADRs Flows Master – Institutional Ownership and Short Interest

We are seeing inflow into Asian ADRs in July, primarily into China/HK, Taiwan and India (around US\$3bn each). Inflows from hedge funds are estimated at US\$15bn, while long-only trimmed US\$4bn. Short interest in China ADRs and H-shares are being covered MTD.

## Key Takeaways

Asian ADRs saw inflow of US\$11bn, dominated by hedge fund inflow, partly offset by long-only outflow

Hedge funds inflow US\$15bn on Asian ADRs in July – concentrated in TSMC, Indian Banks, Alibaba Group and Tencent Music Entertainment

一 Mutual funds net sold US\$4bn on Asian ADRs in July – top sold are ARM Holdings, Tencent Music Entertainment, ReNew Energy Global and TSMC

Short interest in China ADRs and H-Shares are both covered July MTD

Japan ADR flow was neutral but still stands as largest YTD recipient (US\$10B)

## Inflow into China/HK, Taiwan and India

Trading activities in Asian ADRs decreased 4% MTD with the 30-day average daily volume standing at US\$10.6bn vs. US\$11.1 in June. That said, activity in 2024 remained high vs. average of US\$8.1bn in 2023.

July MTD fund inflow into Asian ADRs is estimated at US\$11bn, dominated by hedge fund buying. Inflows have been concentrated in China/HK, Taiwan and India ADRs. Key companies with strong inflows include TSMC, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and Alibaba ( Exhibit 4 ).

On the other side, long-only mutual funds and ETFs recorded outflows in July. Key companies are ARM Holdings, Tencent Music Entertainment, ReNew Energy Global and TSMC. The two brighter spots with inflow are PDD Holdings and Flex Ltd. ( Exhibit 6 )

On the short side, we observed short covering on both ADR lines and H-shares. Key short covering includes NIO, JD.com, Alibaba and KE holdings; short interest is added to PDD Holdings.

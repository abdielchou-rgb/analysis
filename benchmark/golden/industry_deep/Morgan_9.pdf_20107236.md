# ADRs Flows Master – Institutional Ownership and Short Interest

There were signs of sentiment cooling down in Asian ADRs in March MTD. Our flows tracker recorded net outflows on Japanese ADRs, which was dominated by hedge funds' long selling. For Chinese ADRs, hedge funds were selling long positions on ADRs and adding shorts on H-shares.

## Key Takeaways

Despite trading activities of US-listed Asian ADRs being up by +5% in March MTD, our flows tracker recorded net outflows of US\$5.6bn from the segment.

■ Hedge funds led this selling, with net outflows of US\$7.2bn during the month. Japan Banks, China Consumer names, TSMC and Sea Ltd. were sold the most.

Mutual funds remained to be net buyers on Asian ADRs in MTD and YTD. TSMC was net bought the most while funds were net sellers on Pinduoduo in March.

一 Short interests of ADRs were flat; new shorts were mainly adding domestic share classes in China and Japan, in which H-shares were added short the most.

Signs of sentiment cooling in Asian ADRs in March: QTD net inflows to Asian ADRs remained strong (inflows US\$15.7bn) vs. historical average (outflows US \$5.9bn). For their trading liquidity, it is still on the rise, seeing 30-days average-daily volume of all Asian ADRs increasing 5% in March to US\$12.2bn, rounding up YTD liquidity increase to 33%. In March MTD, mutual funds continued to be net buyers on the segment, contributing inflows of US\$1.6bn, but hedge funds have turned to be net sellers with outflows of US\$7.2bn. This selling was mostly done to partially unwind what was bought YTD.

Per our estimates, Asian-based hedge funds have been leading the net selling on ADRs, while US and EU hedge funds have not participated much. The selling from Asian hedge funds has been highly stock specific during the month – TSMC, Sumitomo Mitsui Financial, HDFC Bank, Sea Ltd, and New Oriental Education were on top of the selling list.

For long-only mutual funds and ETFs, net buying in ADRs segment was mainly toward TSMC in March, but selling into strength on some Chinese ADRs, like Pinduoduo and NetEase ( Exhibit 6 ). We do note that short interest on China Hshares has been picking up quickly over the last week, which we believe is a sign of sentiment cooling down from recent rallies.

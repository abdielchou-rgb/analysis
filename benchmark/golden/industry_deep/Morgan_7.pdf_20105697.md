## Performance – Equities and bonds post strong finish

Global equities had their sixth best year on record with the ACWI closing at +23%. S&P 500 (+26.3%) led DM returns, and Mexico led (+40.9%) EM returns. Technology (+51.4%) outperformed among global equity sectors. Fixed income rallied into year-end on rate-cut hopes; US HY (+13.4%) led total returns. DXY (-1.9%) sold off. Commodities saw Precious Metal (+12%) outperformance, while Energy lagged (-5%).

## Technicals – HY issuance rises; inflows into US large-cap

DM HY gross issuance increased by 55%Y in December and EM issuance rose by 12%Y. For US mutual funds and ETFs, on a T12M basis, US large-caps and long-term Treasuries saw largest net inflows, while HY credit and Agency MBS saw net outflows.

## Sentiment – MSI turns negative; vols at four-year lows

Our MSI flagged a risk-off signal in December after a 2-month surge in sentiment level (see here for more on the methodology). AAII bulls-bears touched its highest level since March ’21. VIX finished at 4Y lows.

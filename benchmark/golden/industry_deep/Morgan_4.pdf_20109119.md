## EM Asia Local Market Flows, Positioning and Issuance

## Monthly Highlights

Foreign Flows: Inflows have returned to the local bond market in the past two months, with the outflows seen earlier this year now being reversed. Over the last two months we have seen \$12.4bn of inflows across the region, with the main beneficiaries being: South Korea (+\$3.8bn), China (+\$3.5bn) and India (+\$2.7bn).

## Local bond issuance:

(1) India: Given the elevated levels of cash, as well as the better fiscal starting point, we expect authorities will look to lean on a larger share of cash utilisation in the July budget, and reduce their level domestic bond financing. We have revised lower our FY25 forecast to INR13.6tn (from INR14.13tn previously). (2) Indonesia: Authorities have announced that they will look to lower their projected bond issuance this year, and instead drawdown on cash balances. We revise lower our forecast for conventional INDOGB issuance from IDR580tn down to INR490tn for FY24.

JPM EM Client Survey: Investors have been increasingly focused on the bottom up idiosyncratic developments in the region, rather than top down themes. On the duration front, investors have increased bullish positioning in India, with OW's now at all time highs, while bearish positions were scaled up in Taiwan. Elsewhere, on the FX front investors materially increased their UW THB stance, which now sits at the largest level since Feb-16.

Morgan's Emerging Markets Sovereign Research, we assign a rating to each sovereign issuer (Overweight, Marketweight or Underweight) based on our view of whether the combination of the issuer’s fundamentals, market technicals, and the relative value of its securities will cause it to outperform, perform in line with, or underperform the credit returns of the EMBIGD index over the next three months.

## Hot’s Tough

## Macro Data – A Hot Mess

We expect the US to achieve a soft landing, akin to the mellow mid-cycle pivot we saw in the 1990s. But hotter and messier data – including bumpy inflation in 1H – should see markets question the Fed policy path from here. Elsewhere, eurozone growth should be sluggish, and China’s recovery likely stays weak.

## Markets – A Hot Streak

Risk assets look stretched and detached from fundamentals – breadth and valuations for risk assets are close to prior cycles at their peaks. For the risk rally to be sustained, it’d require better-than-Goldilocks growth…which a too-hot market may feel is too hot to handle.

## Strategy: OW Fixed Income, Play Thematics in Regional Equities

We maintain our fixed income > equities preference over the medium term, but admit that value is now hard to find in credit and stocks. Stick to fixed income with good carry and convexity – e.g., long USTs, UKTs. For equities, focus on long-term secular investment themes rather than chase the cycles – e.g., prefer Japan, India, Mexico.

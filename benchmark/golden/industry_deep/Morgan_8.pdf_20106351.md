## Weak External Trade

Euro area exports benefitted from a strong rebound of world trade in 2021 and 2022. World trade has been stagnant since end-2022 and euro area exports fell by 3.0%Y in 3Q23. Forward-looking indicators and our global forecast suggest that euro area exports will remain subdued in the coming quarters, but as global trade is expected to accelerate, we think that euro area exports will also gain momentum.

# How do different assets and economic variables perform after rate cuts start?

Where the S&P 500 goes after rate cuts depends on whether a recession occurs within a few months either side of the first cut. If a recession doesn’t happen the market rallies aggressively. However, it’s weak in easing cycles around recessions.

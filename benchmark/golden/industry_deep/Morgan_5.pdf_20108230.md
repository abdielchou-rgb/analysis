## Japan:

JGBs continued to sell off across the curve ahead of the 5y JGB auction. The unexpected reduction in BoJ JGB purchases on Monday led to concern for another reduction in the future, which weighed on JGBs ahead of the auction. The 5y auction tailed by 4 cents, although the allotment for bids at the low price was relatively low (3.3552%). After the auction, aggressive sell-off eased and 5y JGBs rallied back from session lows, in part due to demand to square short positions ahead of US CPI on Wednesday. There seemingly was less demand to buy the dip from the real money community in the 30y tenor, even though yields surpassed 2.00%, as JGBs bearsteepened. 5y JGBs sold off by 1.5bp d/d to 0.555% on a simple yield basis, while 30y JGBs sold off by 2.5bp d/d to 2.05%. TONA OIS also bear-steepened and outperformed JGBs in the long end. XCCY basis widened across the curve.

USD/JPY broke above 156.00 overnight on Monday as US Treasury Secretary Yellen mentioned intervention should be rare and communicated. Market participants seemingly believed it would be difficult for the MoF to intervene again, as it had been suspected to have done recently; USD/JPY ground higher in the absence of tier-one US macro data until US CPI data on Wednesday. Japanese Finance Minister Suzuki made comments that did not contain a warning tone for potential imminent intervention, despite a further rally in USD/JPY. The Tokyo afternoon session had fewer catalysts for USD/JPY, which remained range-bound from 156.4-156.5. Other CCY/JPY pairs generally moved in tandem with USD/JPY; EUR/JPY continued to rise to 168.75 toward the London morning.

## Asia:

Most AxJ currencies exhibited mixed moves, but were largely stable ahead of US CPI on Wednesday. CNH weakened marginally for the third consecutive session, as Chinese equities underperformed (CSI 300: -0.2%). USD/CNH was not affected by media reports of US tariffs on US\$18bn of Chinese imports, and finished the session up by just 0.04%, at \~7.2425. IDR was the main underperformer in the region following weakness in IndoGBs and local equities; USD/IDR rose 0.1% to 16,100. USD/KRW gained 0.1% to 1,369 as the local equity market remained steady (KOSPI: +0.1%). THB and MYR outperformed; both USD/THB (-0.4%) and USD/MYR (-0.3%) fell, with the former tracking the rise in gold prices. Thailand’s cabinet also passed a minimum wage bill. Other AxJ currencies were stable.

Moves in AxJ rates were mixed. China rates outperformed ahead of the 1y MLF operation on Wednesday; the 1s5s NDIRS curve bull-flattened by 2bp, the 1y yield fell 1bp. India rates also rallied slightly, as 5y NDOIS yields fell 1bp and 10y G-Sec yields fell 2bp after India April CPI (released on Monday after Asia market close) decelerated in-line with consensus expectations, even though wholesale prices were higher-than-expected. Like FX, Indonesia rates underperformed ahead of the IDR22trn bond/bill auction. 10y IndoGB yields rose another 4bp to return to the 7% handle. Hong Kong and Korea rates also underperformed, particularly in the frontend of the curve. 1y swap yields rose 2bp and 1bp, respectively. Malaysia, Singapore, and Thailand rates all rallied \~1bp across the swaps curve.

## Emerging Markets

Enhancements to a G4 10y government bond futures momentum strategy have produced higher Sharpe ratios and stronger returns, relative to total return government bond indices for the G4, US, Germany, Japan, and the UK since 2000. See A "Smarter" (Beta) Way to Trade G4 10y Futures Duration? for more information on these strategies.

## Trading Strategy 1 – "Trade Longs/Fade Shorts"

When the 5-day moving average crosses above the 20-day moving average, buy the futures contract (long duration) and hold for a 25-business-day period. When the 5-day moving average crosses below the 20-day moving average, buy the futures contract and hold for a 25-business-day period. In short, this strategy buys futures when the Simple Moving Average Crossover (SMAX) generates both a long and a short signal, given the historical outperformance of long signals traded long and the underperformance of short signals traded short. Given that the SMAX could generate both a long and a short signal within the predefined holding period, an investor may have a 200% long position since each of the two signals would be traded in separate portfolio sleeves.

## Trading Strategy 2 – Trade "Longs Only"

When the 5-day moving average crosses above the 20-day moving average, buy the futures contract (long duration) and hold for a 25-business-day period. When the 5-day moving average crosses below the 20-day moving average, do nothing. In short, an investor ONLY trades long signals initiated by the SMAX given their historical precedent to outperform.

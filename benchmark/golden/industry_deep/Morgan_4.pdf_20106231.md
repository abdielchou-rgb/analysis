## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## 长亮科技 (300348.SZ)

We derive our price target from a 10-year DCF model. We favor DCF because we believe it best reflects the company's long-term growth potential and it is also consistent with our software company coverage. We assume a 2% terminal growth rate and 10.6% WACC (4% risk-free rate, 11.2% cost of equity, 1.1 beta, 6% cost of debt, 10:90 debt to equity weighting). Our WACC assumption is benchmarked against other financial IT software companies.

## Risks to Upside

n Faster-than-expected rollout of core banking localization and technology upgrades.

n Better breakthrough in penetrating SOE banks and other large banks.

n Reaccelerating overseas expansion.

## Risks to Downside

n Softer-than-expected bank IT spending.

n Stricter regulation on emerging business.

n Intense competition in overseas markets.

## 宇信科技 (300674.SZ)

Base case, derived from a 10-year DCF model. We favor DCF because we believe it best reflects the company's long-term growth potential and it is also consistent with all our software company coverage. We assume a 10.6% WACC, based on an 11.2% cost of equity, 6% cost of debt, and 10:90 debt to equity weighting. Our WACC assumption is benchmarked against other financial IT software companies. Our terminal growth rate is 2%.

## Risks to Upside

n Faster overseas expansion

n Successful launch of other innovative business in corporate banking area

n Stronger-than-expected demand in traditional software development business

## Risks to Downside

n Softer-than-expected bank IT spending

n Stricter regulation on emerging business

n Intense competition in overseas markets

## 恒生电子 (600570.SS)

Base case, 10-year discounted cash flow (DCF) model. We favor DCF because we believe it best reflects the long-term growth potential of the company. Key assumptions: weighted average cost of capital (WACC) of 12.3% (with a cost of equity of 13.1% and a cost of debt of 6.0%), terminal growth rate of 3.0%.

## Risks to Upside

n Regulatory changes that can bring new financial IT demand

n Breakthrough in new business from subsidiaries

## Risks to Downside

n Regulatory risk in China's financial markets

n Capital market volatility, leading to fluctuations in customer IT budgets

n Opex increases due to R&D investment for new business

n New business development disappointment

## 中科软 (603927.SS)

We derived our price target from our 10-year discounted cash flow model, as we believe DCF best reflects the company's long-term growth potential. Our key assumptions include a 2% terminal growth rate and 10.6% WACC (4% risk-free rate, 11.2% cost of equity, 1.1 beta, 6% cost of debt, 10:90 debt-to-equity ratio). The WACC that we use is in line with that for other software names in our coverage. Our terminal growth rate is 2%.

## Risks to Upside

n Faster-than-expected recovery of insurance industry

n Innovative policies and regulations

n Successful expansion in non-insurance segments

## Risks to Downside

n Weak insurance industry growth due to further macro downturn

n More intense competition in non-insurance segments, leading to lower gross margin

n Increase in cash conversion cycle

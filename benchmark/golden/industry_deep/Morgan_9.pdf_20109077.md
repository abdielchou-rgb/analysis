Our price target (base case) remains unchanged. ## BULL CASE

Rs2,935.00

## 2.4x Sept-26e core BVPS

Sharp rebound in economic growth (quick capex recovery and benign in ation trends): Loan growth improves sharply, thanks to a flsharp pickup in the macro climate and higher-than-expected bene ts from digitization. Margin performance is better than expected, helped by sharp increase in the share of high-yielding loans and improvement in the retail liability franchise. Fee growth picks up sharply & regulatory and competitive pressures are lower than expected. Credit costs are better than expected.

## BASE CASE

Rs1,925.00

## 1.7x Sept-26e core BVPS

Continued macro recovery but for global macro/geopolitical concerns: Loan growth at 18%/19% in F25/F26 vs. 18% in F24. Margins are estimated at 4.2%/4.3% during F25/F26 vs. 4.3% in F24. Impaired loan formation moderates from F23 levels - credit costs moderate toward normalized levels in F25-26 to 113bps , vs. \~120bp in F24.

## BEAR CASE

Rs1,085.00

## 1.0x Sept 2026e core BVPS

Sharp slowdown in economic growth weighing on loan growth: Loan growth and margins are lower than in the base case. Impaired loan formation is higher than expected, driven by high slippages from SME loans and retail loan book.

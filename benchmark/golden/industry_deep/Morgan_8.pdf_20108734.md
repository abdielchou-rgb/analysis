## Global Macro Strategy View

 History guides us to regard the election outcome, and related policy uncertainty, as volatility-inducing for both Treasuries and the US dollar.

 The impact of policy paths across outcomes tends towards strengthening USD. In addition to the already constructive set-up for USD, history suggests that tariff talk resulting from a Republican White House win could boost the currency further, though a Trump administration’s posture towards the Fed is a key risk to this view.

 For Treasuries, election outcomes mostly affirm our current expectation of a path to lower nominal yields, though a Republican sweep is more complicated, with potential for a higher term premium and divergence between lower real yields and higher breakevens as investors try to parse through the uncertainty of combined policy impacts on the US economic outlook.

## United versus Divided Government Not a Sufficient Framework

In previous elections, a united government came with higher prospects of fiscal spending and higher rates, and vice versa. However, the upcoming election will likely have significant dependence on executive power, i.e., the president, for many of the policies above – especially tariffs and immigration – making a united versus divided framework insufficient.

## International Policy Confrontation Is Mixed for USD, but Bullish for USTs

During Trump’s presidency, international policy confrontation was associated with USD gains against EM currencies and risk-sensitive currencies like AUD, and USD declines against low-yielding currencies like EUR. USD declined against EUR and JPY on days when traderelated actions were announced. The fall in USD/JPY and rise in EUR/USD aligned with notable declines in US real yields.

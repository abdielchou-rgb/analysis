# 4Q Preview: Watching DPS and FCF

Market concerns over the retrospective new resource tax are overdone, in our view. We expect PetroChina's 2023 net profit to grow 7% YoY, with the best FCF generation among the big three; the other two are likely to see declining earnings and stretched FCF. To play yield, we would pick PetroChina.

## Key Takeaways

We note one-off losses related to the new domestic resource tax (0.8%/0.6% of onshore/offshore oil & gas revenue) between 2H17 and 2022 in 2023 P&L.

■ The retrospective tax impact (2H17-2022) on market value is limited as it's a oneoff item. The new tax has been factored in our models from 2024 onwards.

一 With US\$78 Brent in 1H24, the big three would have similar 2H23-1H24 dividend yields of \~10%, while PetroChina has the highest FCF yield ensuring payout ratio.

■ We are concerned with Sinopec's FCF generation, which was still negative in 1- 3Q23. To maintain payout ratio, it will likely lever up its balance sheet.

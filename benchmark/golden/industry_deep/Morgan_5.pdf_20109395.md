# Positions of Active Long-Only Managers in Asia/EM

We publish our July 2024 Asia/EM positioning guide to summarize allocation changes at the stock, market and sector level over time from a regional perspective. On top of that, we share the distribution of active weights and compare the allocation difference between US and EU managers.

## Key Takeaways

US\$1.6bn outflow from active GEM funds and US\$280m outflow from GEM passive products

■ GEM active managers rotated from Mexico, India, Brazil into Taiwan, China and Korea in June

■ As of June, Semis/Equipment remained the most concentrated industry group - active weight at 3.5%, back up from May and similar to Q1 2024

In Japan, shorts were added in Financials but covered in Energy July MTD

## Focus of The Month - Regional rebalancing: from Latin America into Asia

According to our bottom-up fund position analysis of the largest 80 EM active long only mutual funds (with average fund AUM at US\$3.2bn), we see signs of regional rebalancing. Overweights in Mexico, India and Brazil were trimmed while underweights in China and Taiwan were reduced ( Exhibit 1 ). Korea's positioning is now neutral vs benchmark ( Exhibit 4 ) . As of June month-end, Semis/Equipment remained the most crowded industry group with 3.5% active weight, up from May's level and back to 1Q 2024 level ( Exhibit 10 ).

In Japan, active managers increased active weights in Keyence and Mitsui & Co but trimmed Sony and Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group in June. Hedge fund managers also added shorts in Financials while covering Energy shorts July MTD. Short interest was added most on Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group at security level ( Exhibit 17 ).

## Methodology of Our Fund Analysis

## Global Emerging Markets Fund Universe

Coverage: Our US fund universe in this analysis is formed by taking the 40 largest active funds in "US Funds: Morningstar Emerging Markets Equity", covering total AUM of US \$186bn.

Our SICAV fund universe in this analysis is formed by taking the 40 largest active funds in "International Funds: Morningstar Emerging Markets Equity", covering total AUM of US \$69bn.

We carve out EM equities to form a composite portfolio using weighted average by market value of each security.

Rationale: We track only the funds with the largest AUM because of their better data quality. Changes in individual securities positions across different funds are aggregated based on market value. Please note that not every individual fund discloses month-end positions, so our estimations are for best-effort market indication purposes rather than factual reported data.

## Japan Fund Universe

Methodology: We take the 40 largest active funds by AUM under the Morningstar categories of "Global Equity", "Japan Equity" and "Asia Pacific Equity" to aggregate a composite portfolio of 160 individual funds, covering AUM of US\$884bn as of June 30, 2024, in which Japanese equities came to US\$72bn. We carve out these Japanese equities to form a composite portfolio using weighted average by market value of each security.

Rationale: We track only the funds with the largest AUM, because these tend to have better data quality. The changes in individual securities positions across different funds are aggregated based on market value. Please note that not every fund discloses month-end positions, so our estimates serve as a best-efforts gauge of market indications, rather than factual data reporting.

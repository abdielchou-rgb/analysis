## On Landings:

• Soft landing = bullish = breadth improves = HY bonds, global cyclicals & EM,

No landing = bubble = sell bonds (until real rates bite), sell US\$ into strength, barbel stocks with Nasdaq & inflation hedges (gold, commodities, crypto, energy, China),

• Hard landing = bust = buy bonds, sell stocks.

We say consensus soft landing probabilities too high (Chart 5)...inflation, no Fed cuts for now, and pandemic, US-China tech protectionism, war, CRE bust, AI “bubble,” Asia FX devaluation race, US government debt +\$1tn every 100 days…decade of extreme macro & market outcomes to continue (caveat is extreme resilience of IG).

We say no landing correctly in vogue…note market price action of higher yields & higher Nasdaq typical of bubbly markets (e.g. ’99 – Chart 7)…no landing = bubble risk = period in which stocks lead yields higher…but also means higher inflation (which hurts consumer) & cost of capital (which hurts corporations…AI not cheap).

We say rising no landing risks = rising hard landing risks…monetary tightening resumes (markets now pricing in 15% probability of Fed hike) & contagion risks form from REITs, regional banks, small cap up; March US small business optimism at 11-year low (Chart 8), ominously (sector creates 2/3 US jobs) hiring plans at 8-year lows (Chart 9).

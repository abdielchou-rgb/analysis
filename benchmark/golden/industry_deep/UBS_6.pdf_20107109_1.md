# Taiwan Equity Strategy

## Take a breather, suggest sector rotation [Erratum]

## Taiex has rallied and reached our upside scenario target.

We expect the Taiex to consolidate as earnings upward revisions stay limited, both for 2024/25. Our three major investment themes: growth, cyclical, and high yield, have worked well. Following that, we would turn more selective due to mixed demand dynamics continuing in the tech sectors, turbulence from ETF funds, and less attractive valuation. We remain positive on AI application demand and NAND but turn conservative on smartphone chipset suppliers and niche DRAM. We also suggest to position in selected high yield stocks (Figure 5A listofhg yedck wBu/Nra nv ) at the beginning of the dividend yield season. We would be mindful of stocks which might be exposed to reverse trades ahead of the launch of new ETF funds and the upcoming ETF funds rebalancing in May/June. Our positive stance on cyclical recovery in bikes/apparels and telco M&A synergies also remains intact.

## Muted earnings upward revisions; semi, financial, and telco upside risks

We believe the recent Taiex rally has largely priced in the growth potential in 2024 and 2025. The Taiex is now trading at 15.2x 2025E PE, 0.4SD above 26-year average. However, consensus earnings estimates recorded muted upward revisions during YTD24. Only 37% of total companies reported a beat/in-line Q423 results among the 90% of total MSCI index stocks reported. Monthly sales during 2M24 were largely inline with consensus estimates and seasonality, achieving 65% of Q124 estimates. We notice three sectors are tracking ahead, Semis (mainly foundry/back-end), Financials, and Telco, while Materials are lagging.

## Dividend season approaching, watch out for ETF fund flow turbulence

Our study shows the earlier investors position for the dividend yield season (March through August), the higher the potential investment return. However, we would be careful about the reverse trades prior to the launch of new high yield funds, representing potential more than US\$7bn in the next 1 month and 5 more in the pipeline. Investors might position in stocks these funds plan to position and take profit before these funds officially launch. In addition, existing high yield funds might rebalance their portfolios, deleting low yield stocks in May and June. Our studies show a heightened trade volume and strong share price performances prior to these ETF events but share price performance post the event would reflect the fundamentals. Our suggested high dividend yield stocks are listed in page 13.

## Lift Taiex target, s most/least preferred stocks changes

We factor in UBS earnings revisions and revise our year end index target to 19,125, with an upside/downside scenario at 21,670/18,175. For most preferred stocks, we remove MediaTek on cooling of China's Android restocking, Nanya Tech on easing DRAM price momentum, and add Faraday on pullback into ramps of 28nm projects, Alchip with high AI leverage, and Cathay Financial on outlook of higher dividend yield, eased equity raising concern, and relatively attractive valuation. For the least preferred stocks, we add Taiwan Cement on lukewarm construction demand and intensifying price competition in the Southern China market (full list in Figure 20Most andle prfck).

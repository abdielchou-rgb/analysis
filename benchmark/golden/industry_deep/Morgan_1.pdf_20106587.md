# Read-out of the 2024 LNY holiday activity

After the eight-day Lunar New Year holiday (February 10-17), all eyes are on China<sup>’</sup>s holiday activity data. We summarize our key takeaways below, along with update for our alt-data trackers since our last update on Feb 8.

The number of tourists were up 34.3%oya or 119% of the 2019 level and tourism revenue was up 47.3%oya or 107.7% of the 2019 level. Tourism expenditure per capita retreated to 91% of the 2019 level.

Daily average domestic passenger flows were up 21.2%oya, but down 34.9% compared to 2019. Railway and air travel outperformed, up 44.1% or 25.1%.

Domestic passenger flows via non-commercial passenger car trips accounted for 86.3%pts, related to high proportion of privately-owned auto vehicles, and rising preference for road trips for holidays.

The Ministry of Commerce reported 8.5%oya growth for major retail and catering firms, including 17% jump for catering, close to 10% gain for large supermarkets, etc.

## 1. LNY holiday activity

Tourism spending: The number of tourists reached 474mn person-time for the 8-day LNY holiday (February 10-17), up 34.3%oya or reaching 119% of the 2019 level. Tourism revenue recorded 632.7bn yuan, up 47.3%oya or reaching 107.7% of the 2019 level. Tourism expenditure per capita retreated further to 91% of the 2019 level on a comparable basis (vs. 97% for the 2024 New Year<sup>’</sup>s Day holiday).

Domestic passenger traffic: The Ministry of Transport reported that domestic passenger flow during the holiday reached 2.293bn person-time, including 1.98bn (or 86.3%pts) for non-commercial passenger car trips.It is related to high proportion of privately-owned auto vehicles, and rising preference for road trips for holidays.On a comparable basis (with consistent statistical coverage over time), daily average domestic passenger flows during the holiday via major transport methods were up 21.2%oya, but down 34.9% compared to 2019. Railway and air travel outperformed, up 44.1% or 25.1% compared to 2019, while commercial vehicle highway travel was down 51.8% compared to 2019.

• Retails/Catering: The Ministry of Commerce reported 8.5%oya growth for major retail and catering firms, including a 17% jump for catering, close to 10% gain for large supermarkets, and around 20% growth for green organic and jewelry sales.

Online payment: The PBOC reported that daily average transaction of the NetsUnion Clearing Corporation and UnionPay during the LNY holiday reached 2.63bn in volume terms, or 1.25 trillion yuan in value terms, up 18.6%oya and 8.0% respectively. It points to around 10%oya decline per transaction ticket on average.

Cross-border travel: Alipay reported their users<sup>’</sup> overseas transaction volume during the holiday reached 107% of the 2019 level, and the transaction value jumped 140%oya. Singapore, Thailand and Malaysia stood out, with Alipay spending up 580%oya, partly benefitting from the visa-free travel efforts. China<sup>’</sup>s outbound flights to major destinations saw solid gains during the LNY holiday, with Singapore, ANZ surpassing the 2019 average. Inbound tourists<sup>’</sup> Alipay spending amount jumped 500%oya.

Movie box: According Maoyan, China<sup>’</sup>s box office revenue generated during the eightday holiday reached a new historical high at 8.05 billion yuan, compared to the then historical peak of 7.84bn yuan in 2021.

Housing transactions: New home sales remained sluggish while secondary home sales outperformed, though transaction volumes are usually low during the LNY, partly due to delay in the government<sup>’</sup>s registration system. According to CREIS, 25 major cities daily average new home sales fell 27%oya during the 8-day LNY holiday, with a 108% rise in Tier 1 cities, 46% drop in Tier 2 cities, and 29% fall in Tier 3/4 cities. According to Beike, 50 major cities<sup>’</sup> secondary home sales were up more than 70%oya, with a 3% marginal fall in Tier 1 cities, 98% and 65% jump in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities respectively.

## 2. Domestic and international flights

Solid flights execution for both domestic and international flights during the LNY holiday, reaching 115% of the 2019 average for domestic flights (vs. 86% for the 2023 LNY holiday), and 116% for international flights (vs. 12% for the 2023 LNY holiday)

Malaysia, Singapore, Philippines, Macau, Italy, and Spain led China’s outbound flights recovery in the two weeks running into the LNY holiday, while flights to ANZ, Singapore, Philippines, Macau and Malaysia retreated immediately last week, likely followed by further decline into the end of February as LNY demand booming effect fades.

China<sup>’</sup>s outbound flights to major destinations picked up in the two weeks through Feb 13 on LNY tourism demand, including Malaysia (81%-->93%), Singapore (106%-- >113%), Philippines (57%-->64%), Macau (83%-->90%), Italy (124%-->131%) and Spain (104%-->108% of the 2019 average).

• Last week, OAG data shows China<sup>’</sup>s outbound flights retreated, including to Australia (109%-->99%), New Zealand (137%-->117%), Singapore (113%-->103%), Philippines (64%-->56%), Macau (90%-->80%) and Malaysia (93%-->87%).

## 3. From high-frequency trackers to official monthly activity

Regarding the mapping efforts from high-frequency data to monthly official activity tracking:

• Operating rates for semi-/full-steel tires point to a sharp slide in February auto IP growth after the January surge on LNY-related seasonality.

• Operating rate for steel rebar suggests steel IP growth likely will turn negative in February after the January rebound on LNY seasonality.

Housing transactions of 30 major cities may decline 69%oya in February (vs. -18.0%in January), if the month-to-date pace extends to the month-end; the drop may have been overestimated due to LNY seasonal distortion and a delay in government<sup>’</sup>s registration system.

• Deadweight tonnage tracking points to a fall in February departing ships<sup>’</sup> deadweight tonnage %oya growth after the solid gain in January, partly due to LNY seasonality as LNY holiday was in January last year, but February this year.

## 4. Logistics and port freight

The BEISL global container freight index stabilized with a tick lower as of February 16, while the BEISL Baltic exchange dry index ticked up marginally.

For China<sup>’</sup>s containerized freight index (CCFI) breakdown by route, USEC (US east coast) and USWC (US west coast) picked up further, up 5.8% and 8.4% week-on-week, and both rising 59% compared to the near-term low in late October 2023. The CCFI to the Mediterranean, Europe and Red Sea were down 1.9%wow, 3.4% and 9.1%, but still up 139%, 147%, and 69% compared to the late October 2023 low.

• Container ships usually carry consumer goods as well as some machinery equipment and electronics. As of February 18, month-to-date tracking suggests departing container ships<sup>’</sup> deadweight tonnage may grow 8.7%oya in February (vs 4.8% in January) if the daily average pace extends to the rest of the month. Similarly, arriving container ships deadweight tonnage may grow 1.1%oya (vs 7.2% in January). Both are softer than the growth tracked as of February 7, partly due to seasonal slowdown during the LNY holiday as expected.

Bulk carriers transport unpackaged bulk cargo for grain, coal, iron ore, steel, etc., in their cargo holds. As of February 18, month-to-date tracking suggests departing bulk ships<sup>’</sup> deadweight tonnage may grow 2.3%oya in February (vs 11.6% in January) if the daily average pace extends to the rest of the month. Similarly, arriving bulk ships<sup>’</sup> deadweight tonnage may grow 3.9%oya (vs 11.5% in January).

## 5. Food and commodity prices

Agricultural food price reached recent peak at the start of LNY holiday, and trended down in the past week. As of February 18, agricultural food price was up 2.4% compared to the same period last February. If the food wholesale price trends down further for the rest of month, agricultural food price likely will be a marginal drag on headline CPI in February.

Pork price also picked up on LNY holiday demand, with a tick lower after the holiday. As of February 18, we estimate pork wholesale price grew 6.3%oya on average. We think pork price will turn into a modest lift on headline CPI in the current month.

• Commodity prices: Cement and aluminum prices ticked lower, with no update for steel rebar prices since early February yet.

## 7. Temperature and influenza tracking

Several central and eastern areas in China were hit by freezing rain and heavy snow in early February, including Hubei, Hunan and Henan provinces, etc. It was followed by a broad-based temperature rise, a warm weather facilitating cross-regional travels for the LNY holiday.

The share of influenza-like illness cases in total outpatient cases ticked up in both the northern and southern parts of China for the week ending February 11.

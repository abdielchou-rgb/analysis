## What does this mean for the property market?

• Lowered threshold for 2<sup>nd</sup> home and foreign buyers: Stamp duty for locals buying 2<sup>nd</sup> flats and non-HK permanent residents is up to 4.25% now (vs. 7.5% and 15% previously).

• Secondary housing transactions to increase: The Special Stamp Duty – which was 10-20% on properties resold within two years – is now removed.

Eased mortgage restrictions: HKMA has raised maximum LTV for residential properties (70% below HK\$30m), and called off the "200bps rise in mortgage rate" stress test for residential mortgages

Our HK Property team expects residential price to rise by 5% this year (vs. previous estimate of -10%; -7% in 2023)

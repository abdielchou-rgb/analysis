# Rates to the Rescue

Hong Kong is uniquely positioned to benefit from lower US rates and higher China economic growth. Even without China growth, the expected Fed rate cut (Sep 17) is positive for HK real estate companies which are trading at all-time low valuations, while offering sustainable and high dividend yields.

## Key Takeaways

■ We predict a 5% home price recovery in 2025 helped by US rate cuts, following an 8% drop in 2024 (30% lower than its peak).

The residential segment is likely to benefit more directly from lower US rates than retail and office.

We estimate that every 100bps drop in HIBOR could drive an average earnings increase of 5% for HK property companies.

We prefer SHKP, Link REIT, and Wharf REIC due to their stable earnings outlooks and potential for higher dividends resulting from lower rates.

Henderson and Kerry should also benefit. Stay cautious on NWD, HLP and Hysan.

Home prices to drop 8% in 2024 and recover by 5% in 2025: We expect 1-month HIBOR to drop to 2.75% (1H24: 4.5%) and the effective mortgage rate (P-1.75%) to fall to 3.25% in 2025, based on our US economist's Fed rate forecast. With the current rental yield of 3.2% and positive rent outlook, carry could turn neutral in 2025 while affordability (monthly payment/median household income) should improve to a healthier level of 45%. In our view, rates was a key driver for the 30% decline in home price since 2021. Hence, this should drive HK home prices to bottom and show a cyclical recovery of +5% in 2025.

Lower interest expense = higher EPS: HK property companies have \~60% of their debt exposure at floating rates. We calculate that a 100 bps drop in HIBOR could raise HK property companies' earnings by 5% on average. Companies with high gearing (NWD, Henderson, Kerry, Hysan) or a high percentage of floating rates (Wharf REIC) could benefit more, in our view.

Stock implications: Lower US rates should more directly benefit residential (improving affordability and carry) > retail (weaker HKD) > office (better GDP outlook). We see developers/REITs as proxy for US rate cuts. Stocks' dividend yield spread to 10-year UST remains >1SD wider than LT history, suggesting US rate cuts are not yet fully priced in. We prefer SHKP, Link REIT and Wharf REIC which have stable earnings outlooks and the ability to maintain or pay higher dividends as a result of lower rates. We also expect Henderson (strong dividend intention) and Kerry (gearing to peak in 2025) to benefit. We are cautious on NWD, HLP and Hysan as their fundamentals remain challenging and our analysis shows risk to 2025 DPS even with rate cuts.

## Investment Summary

Why does US rates matter? Since HKD is pegged to USD, HK's monetary policy largely follows that of the US and has big implications on rates that drive HK's mortgage rates and interest rates on debt. The sharp increase in interest rates since 2022 drove the decline in HK home prices and resulted in sharp underperformance for HK's property sector in the past three years. Our US economist, Ellen Zentner, expects the Fed rate to drop from 5.375% currently to 4.625% by Dec 2024 and 3.625% by June 2025.

HIBOR and mortgage rate outlook: New mortgages are capped at P-1.75% (effective rate of 4.125%). HK Prime rate (up 87.5bps) has increased at a much more modest pace than the Fed rate (up 525bps) throughout this rate hike cycle. Hence, the reduction in Prime rate will likely also be more modest than the Fed cuts. Our HK banks analyst Nick Lord expects HK's Prime rate to be reduced by 12.5bps for every 25bps Fed cut. Based on our Fed rate outlook, we estimate that the effective mortgage rate could reach 3.25% by end-2025.

Meanwhile, 1m HIBOR averaged 4.5% in 1H24 and we expect it to largely follow the movement of Fed rates. This suggests 1m HIBOR dropping by 175bps to 2.75% by end-2025. The resulting H-based mortgage rate would be 4.0% (H+1.3%) and remains higher than the Prime cap.

## Implications on HK residential prices

(1) Negative rental carry to fade: With a gross rental yield of 3.2%, rental carry is negative. We expect HK mortgage rate to come down from 4.1% to 3.3% after a 175bps Fed rate cut by mid-2025 based on our US economist's forecasts. Together with a positive rent outlook (up 5% YTD) due to talent inflows from mainland China, we see rental carry turning neutral in 2025, which should attract investment demand.

(2) Affordability to improve: Despite a 25% home price correction since 2021, the affordability ratio (mortgage payment/median household income) is currently 50%, at the higher end of the historical range. This is a result of substantial interest payments with the mortgage rate at 4.125%. Our sensitivity analysis shows that the affordability ratio will become much healthier at 45% if the mortgage rate goes down to 3.3%.

## Implications for HK Property Stocks

Developers' stock prices move with rate expectations: The pace of rate cut expectations, denoted by MSP0KE Index, accelerated from 2.0 times in May 2024 to 7.0 times in Aug 2024. We observe an inverse correlation between HK property stock performance vs. HSI and MSP0KE before COVID. HK property stocks outperformed HSI when expectations around the number of Fed rate hikes declined in 2018/19.

Landlords/REITs and lower US 10Y treasury yields: As yield plays, landlords/REITs' dividend yields tend to move with US 10-year treasury yields. With the rise in US treasury yields since 2022, dividend yields of landlords/REITs have expanded more than the increase in risk-free rates. Hence, the yield spread vs US 10-year treasury yields is already at a historical wide level, reflecting the challenging industry outlook. Potential rate cuts would further increase the yield spread and make stock valuations even more attractive, in our view.

We believe companies that are valued on 10-year UST and with more stable dividend outlooks (Link REIT and Wharf REIC) should benefit from lower rates.

## TECH+ EPS Masks Unexceptional 2Q24 Growth

With 76% of S&P 500 market cap having reported, 2Q EPS is forecasted to grow 9.7% (3.3% ex-TECH+), but is on track for 10.8% assuming continued surprises. The current beat rate of 4.8% is in line with the long-term average.

## Look to 4Q24 Profits for Inspiration

EPS growth for the largest TECH+ stocks is expected to fall from 68% in 4Q23 to 20% in 4Q24, while growth reaccelerates from 1% to 11% for the rest of the market. Taken together, EPS growth is forecasted to increase from 11% to 13%, with greater breadth.

## Jump in VIX Means Greater Market Upside

The VIX jumped from 12.4 in June to 23.4 on 8/2. Market returns tend to be depressed when volatility is low, and greatest when the VIX is extended. See recent note.

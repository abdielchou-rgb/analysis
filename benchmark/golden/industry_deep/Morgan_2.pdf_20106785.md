# February 2024 Retrospective – February Farrago

MSCI India gained 2.5% but underperformed the EM index by 2ppt in February. Domestic Institutions continue to bid stocks. Volatility declined on a sequential basis.

India's underperforms EM in February: MSCI India gained 2.4% during the month, underperforming the EM Index by over 2ppt with its performance, ranking #12 out of 24 EMs. In February, relative performance of Growth to Value declined to its lowest level in 12 years. The Mid-cap index beat the Sensex by 50bps, while the Small-cap index underperformed by 2ppt, even as broad market indices remain above the 2017 peak (relative to large cap).

Consumer Discretionary outperforms: Seven sectors delivered positive returns on an absolute basis, while only two sectors outperformed relative to their EM counterparts. Consumer discretionary was the best-performing sector on an absolute basis while Energy outperformed the most on a relative basis. Communication Services underperformed the most on a absolute and relative basis.

Volatility declined: Stocks trading above their respective 200-day moving averages remain unchanged at 78% February. The advance-decline line was down 13% MoM. The open interest was up 1% on a MoM basis. Put-call ratio and VIX were down 5% and 3% MoM, respectively.

Domestic institutions continue to bid: Total institutional inflows rose backed by strong domestic bid, with inflows of US\$2.9bn, whereas FPIs selling declined to US\$24mn. FPIs remained buyers of debt at US\$2.7bn (the highest in over six years).

Yield curve rise: The yield curve fell below May-23 lows, although it rose 18bps from the bottom.

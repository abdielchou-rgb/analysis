# Global Macro Strategy | North America

# US Rate Volatility Screener

We analyze price action in US interest rate volatility markets, particularly in vanilla, curve, and forward vol. Data are as of July 25, 2024.

## Key Takeaways

The vol surface was higher, led by upper table products

Expiry term structures flattened, led by 1m/1y switches

Tail term structures were also flatter

Curve vol was higher, led by gamma 2s30s and 2s10s

Forward vol at the 1y horizon screens cheap, with attractive roll to spot

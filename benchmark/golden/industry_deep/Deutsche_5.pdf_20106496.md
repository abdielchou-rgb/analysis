## There are arguments for and against the continued dominance of the Mag 7

## • Factors supporting the continued growth of the Mag 7

Big Tech relies on network effects. These have substantial room to grow with the Mag 7 having global reach and very high innovation power.

Some argue the US is incentivised to promote large technology firms to better counter tech-based threats at a geopolitical and military level.

• AI is just in its infancy.

Macroeconomic fluctuations often account for most of the risks associated with exposures to instruments that promise to pay fixed or variable interest rates. The longer the mat certain cash flow and the higher the move in the discount factor, the higher will be the loss. Upside surprises in inflation, fiscal funding needs, and FX depreciation rates are among the most common adverse macroeconomic shocks to receivers. But counterparty exposure, issuer creditworthiness, client segmentation, regulation (including changes in assets holding limits for differen types of investors), changes in tax policies, currency convertibility (which may constrain currency conversion, repatriation of profits and/or liguidation of positions), and settlement issues related to local clearing houses are also important risk factors. The sensitivity of fixed-income instruments to macroeconomic shocks may be mitigated by indexing the contracted cash flows to inflation, to FX depreciation, or to specified interest rates – these are common in emerging markets.

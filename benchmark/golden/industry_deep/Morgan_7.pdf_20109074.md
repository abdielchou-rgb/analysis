## Must-Reads and Highlights from Equity Research

## This month in J.P. Morgan Europe Equity Research…

UK Elections – The Results. With a Labour Party win in the UK, our sector teams assessed the implications for their coverage with standalone notes on Financials, Housebuilders, Utilities, Trainline.

French Elections. Our sector teams detailed their coverage exposure to France, FX sensitivity and potential single stock implications, with detailed pieces from ESG, Building Materials & Infra and Banks.

Credit Check IV. In the fourth edition of Credit Check, our sector teams refreshed their assessment of companies that may face leverage concerns and those that are well positioned, with additional details on gross debt and leases and how second order impacts have evolved.

Global Real Estate Survey – The New Normal. Neil Green and team asked more than 3.5k people across 16 global locations about their return to the office, and sees the results as supportive for Gecina (OW).

Autos – China Feedback. Jose Asumendi deep dived into the Chinese market, assessed Chinese OEMs market share in Europe, Porsche’s (OW) product strategy in China, and BMW’s (OW, AFL) execution.

Global Energy Analyzer – E&P Capex Survey. Kate Somerville and team see continued capex growth as supportive of order intake/margins across OFS and highlight Saipem and Subsea 7 as key OW plays.

Puig – Beauty Elixir. Celine Pannuti initiated on Puig Brands with an OW and added to our Analyst Focus List, as it offers a compelling structural growth story within European staples.

Semi equipment stocks to remain top performers. Sandeep Deshpande believes that the downturn in the general semiconductor market appears to be over, but the upturn outside AI has been milder than prior cycles. His top picks are ASML, ASMI and VAT (all OW).

UK Water – Taking the plunge. Pavan Mahbubani upgraded United Utilities to OW and Severn Trent to N and placed both on Positive Catalyst Watch into the regulatory Draft Determinations in July.

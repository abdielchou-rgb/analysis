## Extensions by sovereign

Ireland will have the largest extension this month (0.73y) compared to other countries, higher than its average March extension of 0.123y. The higher extension is mainly caused by IRISH 5.4% Mar-2025 (market value €11.7bn, weight 9.6%) falling out of the index. The 1-5y index will extend by 0.6y.

France will extend by 0.171y, compared to its average March extension of 0.014y. FRTR Mar-2025 (market value €46bn, weight 2.6%) will fall out of the index. The 1-5y index will extend by 0.133y.

Italy will extend by 0.129y, compared to its average March extension of 0.049y. BTPS 5% Mar-2025 (market value €26bn, weight 1.6%) will fall out of the index. The 1-5y index will extend by 0.109y. BTPS 4.2% Mar-2034 will change indices, causing notable extensions in the 5-10y and 10-15y indices.

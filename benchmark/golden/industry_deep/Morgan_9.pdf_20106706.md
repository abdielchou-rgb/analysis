# ADRs Flows Master – Institutional Ownership & Short Interest

In 2024 YTD, we have seen significant institutional inflows into Asian ADRs. The majority of these inflows have gone into buying Japanese ADRs, with US and EU hedge funds leading the buying. For long-only, their flows were relatively muted, with selective buys on ADRs from China, Japan, and Taiwan.

## Key Takeaways

一 Trading activities of US listed Asian ADRs are up by +25% in 2024 YTD. Significant institutional inflows were recorded at US\$20.4bn.

These inflows were led by hedge funds, with bias toward Large cap issuers, and mostly toward the broader Japanese ADRs, and selective Chinese ADRs.

一 On the selling side, long-only managers led on that front, with Indian and Singaporean ADRs suffering the most.

Hedge funds have dominated sales of Chinese ADRs, with Trip.com, PDD and BABA recording the highest outflows.

On sector level, Japan Banks & Automobiles, and Pinduoduo from China were net bought the most, but other China consumer names continued to lead outflows.

1Q24 is set to see historical high quarterly net inflows to Asian ADRs: The quarter is only two-thirds gone, but US listed Asian ADRs in aggregate have recorded net inflows of US\$20.4bn from global institutional investors QTD. Accordingly, trading liquidity increased further to US\$11.4bn on 30-days averagedaily volume basis.

Per our estimates, US\$19.6bn out of the US\$20.4bn inflows were contributed by hedge funds, with US and EU hedge funds dominating with net buying of US\$9.9bn and US\$8.3bn, respectively ( Exhibit 1 ). The majority of these inflows were toward Japanese ADRs - the top five issuers being net bought QTD were MUFJ Financial, Sumitomo Mitsui Financials, Honda Motor, Takeda Pharmaceutical, and Nomura Holdings ( Exhibit 8 ). Meanwhile, Chinese ADRs also recorded net inflows from hedge funds, but most of that was seen on Pinduoduo, while other Chinese ADRs, and in particular those from Consumer sectors, continued to record net outflows. Trip.com and Vipshop were net sold the most.

For fund flows from long-only mutual funds and ETFs, their flows in ADRs segment have been rotating from India (e.g. ICICI Bank) and Singapore (e.g. Sea Ltd) to China (e.g. Pinduoduo), Taiwan (e.g. TSMC) and Japan (e.g. ARM Holdings). ( Exhibit 6 )

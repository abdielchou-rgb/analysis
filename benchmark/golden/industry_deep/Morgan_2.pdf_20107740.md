## Highlights of the week

• A busy and diverse week saw €1.4bln price in primary. Pricing at +95bp was TOGET’s second RMBS of the year and more sterling supply came from Silver Arrow’s latest auto ABS. Principality BS is in the market with a UK prime deal and a few consumer/auto ABS deals are also in the imminent pipeline. Meanwhile in the CLO space, Neuberger (NB) priced its first CLO since last 2022, adding to the list of managers that did not price a CLO last year but have done so in 2024. Including NB, that list stands at seven managers so far by our calculations. Overall CLO new issue supply YTD stands at €12bln from 29 CLOs while 12 CLOs have reset so far.

• Secondary ABS markets were generally immune to broader market volatility with helpful technicals bolstering levels at the senior parts of the stacks. The non-IG CLO stack saw decompression with weaker profiles widening by \~10bp over the week. Meanwhile, the April payment cycle for CLOs is underway and payouts so far are pointing to a rather healthy outcome with average payments at over 5% - one of the strongest readings in the past several years.

• European CLOs have stood the test of time well despite major macroeconomic shocks, as outlined in a recent S&P report. CLO 1.0 has seen only 1.5% of tranches suffering losses since 2000 (a low 22 defaults out of 1,500-rated tranches), while CLO 2.0 has been even stronger with zero defaults and lower downgrades, with no tranche having a ‘CCC’ rating, owing to better structural protections.

• UK headline inflation declined more than expected to 3.2%Y in March. However, with recent global developments like the upside in US CPI – where our US economists shifted their first Fed rate cut call to July-24 – geopolitical tensions and UK economic data yet to get softer, our UK economists now expect the first BoE rate cut in June-24 (instead of May) with 75bp of cuts this year.

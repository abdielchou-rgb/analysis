Careful stock selection based on idiosyncratic factors is especially important in this type of environment.

## Our Current Top Picks - North America

The rally off the August 5th lows has coincided with better than expected economic data —ISM Services, initial jobless claims and retail sales, in particular but this summer has largely been characterized by macro uncertainty. We believe the market remains in a precarious position and continue to recommend large cap quality and defensive equities as a whole. Despite the elevated degree of macro uncertainty, opportunity remains for careful stock picking.

Stock specific risk, or the proportion of return not explained by macro factors has largely been on the rise since late 2022. Equity risk can be decomposed into macro risk - that associated with common factors – and stock-specific risk. We use a 6-factor model to estimate macro risk; the factors are the equity market; two size residuals; a style residual; an equity quality factor; and momentum. Over rolling 63-day periods, we estimate this model for each stock and find the fraction of risk explained by the model (referred to as its R-squared). One minus this R squared gives us what we call stock specific risk.

Stock specific risk remains elevated for the S&P 500 and is currently in the 80th percentile with \~63% of returns being explained by idiosyncratic factors. it is elevated across most sector - for all sectors but Utilities, stock specific risk is above the 66th percentile and is especially high relative to history in Financials, Energy, and Communication Services ( Exhibit 1 and Exhibit 2 ). Elevated stock specific risk creates a stronger environment for stock picking.

## Methodology & FAQs on Top Picks

## 1. What is a "Top Pick"?

"Top Picks" are our analysts' highest conviction ideas (Overweight or Underweight) within their coverage universe, consistent with their investment thesis and time horizon.

## 2. Why is it relevant?

The Top Pick framework aims to pinpoint high conviction research ideas to identify actionable opportunities for investors to capture alpha. Combining the analyst Top Picks framework with our Bull, Base and Bear case analysis allows investors to better assess risks and opportunities at the stock level.

## 3. What is the selection process and criteria?

The selection of a Top Pick is at the analyst's and Research Management's discretion. Once the idea is selected, analysts must explain their rationale to members of Morgan Stanley's Research Product team, who are closely involved in the process and provide additional insight to the analyst's thought process. Top Picks are updated on an ongoing basis as the underlying stock thesis plays out or analysts' stock preferences change.

Auto drop rules were introduced in April 2023 to Morgan Stanley's Top Pick methodology. These stipulate that a stock is automatically removed as a Top Pick if (1) the Top Pick is outstanding for 6 months with some exceptions pending discussions with our Stock Selection Committee, or (2) the stock price for the Top Pick drops 25% or more. An analyst would thus need to actively re-instate a Top Pick in either of these instances if the stock remains their highest conviction Overweight or Underweight.

## 4. Why do some analysts have more than one Top Pick?

If an analyst covers multiple industries they are allowed to have a top pick for each industry.

## 5. Is having a Top Pick mandatory for each team?

Choosing a Top Pick is not mandatory and is at the analysts' and Research Management's discretion.

# Visible and Attractive Dividend Growth

Reaction to earnings

Unchanged

In-line

Impact to our investment thesis

Largely unchanged

Financial results versus consensus

2023 results and briefing had no major surprise, and the messages were similar to peers'. We forecast a 2023-26 dividend CAGR of 10%, given the company's commitment to increase payout to >75% by 2026 and solid earnings, making its \~7% 2024e yield attractive. Stay OW on CT-H.

Overall healthy growth in 2024: We forecast service revenue to grow 6.3% and net profit to grow 7.5% in 2024, slightly moderating from 2023. Management did not provide any quantitative growth guidance. Traditional services should be largely steady; the company expects stable growth in mobile and broadband ARPU. For Industrial Digitalization, we forecast revenue to grow 14% (vs. +18% in 2023), as cloud revenue moderates from a high base, partially offset by recovering IDC and still-robust big data, cybersecurity, etc..

Cloud: Revenue of Rmb97.2bn (+68% yoy) in 2023 slightly missed its Rmb100bn guidance, although growth of 72% yoy in 2H re-accelerated from +63% in 1H. Management emphasized that cloud business priority will be on quality, i.e. developing more "intelligent" cloud capabilities – CT's intelligent computing power will increase to 21EFLOPS in 2024 from 11EFLOPS currently. In terms of competition, management believes that each of the major cloud players possesses diversified/unique competitive advantages, such as cloud-network integration for CT. CT will not engage in any price competition.

Capex saving in 2024, but less significant than CM's and CU's: 2024 capex guidance implies a 2.9% yoy decline, lower than 4.4% and 12% implied by CM's and CU's guidance, respectively. Similar to its peers, the saving is mainly attributable to 5G capex decline, partially offset by increased investments in Industrial Digitalization areas.

Committed to raise dividend payout to >75% over 2024-26, from 70% in 2023: We think this is not a complete surprise. CM announced a similar plan previously and it comes amid the ongoing SOE reform (e.g., listing market capitalization management as a KPI). We forecast dividend payout ratio to inch up 2ppts p.a. in 2024 and onwards, driving a 10% DPS CAGR over 2023-26 (in RMB terms).

Stay OW on CT-H with price target of HK\$5.0 unchanged: Current \~7% 2024e dividend yield looks attractive given its visible earnings and dividend growth outlook. Stay EW on CT-A – valuation is insufficiently attractive.

## Global Equity Strategy Continue to be careful of cyclicals

There would seem to be three catalysts now for cyclicals to underperform.1) it looks like US macro momentum is peaking (based on soft data macro surprise, recent PMIs and UBS forecasts) -nearly two thirds of the time cyclicals underperform if PMIs fall; 2) the time of year (May to October sees cyclicals underperform c60% of the time) and 3) high macro complacency (as highlighted by low intra-index correlation being in the bottom 1% of its range).

The dilemma: Normally cyclicals move in the same direction as the market. We still see consolidation continuing near term (tactical indicators are still extended at 1std, though less so than 3 weeks ago). But we do see equities rising to 800 Global MSCI AC World by year-end. We think that growth defensives can outperform into a rising market, see this screen. ## What are the catalysts?

We see three potential catalysts:

## 1) Economic momentum peaking in the US.

Clearly, if PMIs rise, then cyclicals tend to rise. They have moved in the same direction as PMI (if we look at 3 month percent change) 63% time in the US and 65% time in Europe.

We are not going to discuss US GDP growth at length (that was done in our recent equity note and in much more detail by our excellent US economics team's notes) but we would stress that: i) overall credit conditions are still very tight whether we look at yield curve, senior loan officers survey or real money supply (though obviously less tight than before); ii) the reasons that tight credit conditions did not impact on growth are now diminishing (excess savings which peaked at 10% of GDP in the US and is now c1.5% of GDP are used up except in the top income quintile), fiscal stimulus (which the IMF estimate boosted GDP by c1.7% in 23 but is a small drag this year), immigration (which has peaked at 6 fold the level of 2019) and the very long maturity of debt (which means that the lags of monetary policy are much greater than normal).

# Positions of Active Long-only Managers in China/HK

December marked the worst monthly net outflows of China/HK equities in 2023 and the third-largest monthly outflows in history. Both US and EU active managers have accelerated selling in order to meet redemptions and deepen the market underweight.

## Key Takeaways

■ Monthly net outflows of China/HK equities in December were estimated at US \$3.8bn, surpassed only by two previous instances (August 2015, March 2020).

Both investors' redemptions from equity funds and portfolio managers rebalancing to deepen underweight on China contributed to the outflows.

EU-domiciled active fund managers are aligning their underweight on China/HK equities with that of US-domiciled active fund managers, in particular on GEM funds.

Throughout 2023, the top industries with weights being added were Media and Capital Goods. Weights in Banks and Tech Hardware were cut the most.

At the stock level, Tencent, Alibaba, Moutai and Netease led the weight additions, but JD.com, Yum China and AIA were cut the most YTD.

December is known to be a redemption season of active funds, but 2023 was much tougher than usual, according to EPFR – US\$2.0bn of monthly outflows from China in December were due to redemptions, vs. the 13-year average of US\$1.0bn ( Exhibit 11 ), while US\$1.8bn of selling was due to managers' rebalancing out of the country. EU managers are catching up to align their underweight on China with their peers in US ( Exhibit 1 ). In terms of positioning changes within the country, active managers were rotating from Value to Growth, and from Consumer to Internet throughout 2023. Please see Exhibit 4 & Exhibit 5 for details.

## Methodology of Our Fund Analysis

Coverage: Our SICAV fund universe in this analysis is formed by taking the 40 largest active funds in "International Funds: MorningStar China Equity" and the 40 largest active funds in "International Funds: MorningStar Emerging Markets Equity", covering total AUM of US\$129.5bn.

Our US fund universe in this analysis is formed by taking the 20 largest active funds in "US Funds: MorningStar China Equity" and the 40 largest active funds in "US Funds: MorningStar Emerging Markets Equity", covering total AUM of US\$241.4bn.

Rationale: We track only the funds with the largest AUM because of their better data quality. Changes in individual securities positions across different funds are aggregated based on market value. Please note that not every individual fund discloses month-end positions, so our estimations are for best-effort market indication purposes rather than factual reported data.

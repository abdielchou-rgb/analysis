## Venture Vision: Longshots

We look to the VC landscape to help investors understand what themes are around the corner for public investors. In our view, Diabesity, AI drug discovery and AI assisted fertility treatments are showing healthy signs of innovation and early-stage funding to sustain a long-lasting Thematic impact.

• See our Longshots, Earthshots and Moonshots report series for emerging investible technologies

• Listen to podcast version here

Last week we published our Longshots note on emerging healthcare technology. We assess their maturity and investibility across a range of innovative applications of healthcare tech. Obesity Medications, Smart Chemotherapy and AI-assisted drug discovery are better understood and look more investible. AI-assisted fertility technology, DNA synthesis, Biosecurity technologies and psilocybin are seeing material outcome- and cost-improvements but remain earlier-stage and more speculative.

More widely than simply the Longshot technology funding discussed above, the early-stage venture/growth funding for biotech start-ups has risen to c6% of all capital deployed in 2023 despite only being c3% of all deals. As the deals and capital deployed in the vertical have fallen back from peak by 58% and 50% respectively, the funding per deal has recovered to its highest level in history at c \$13m per deal (from c\$6m over the last 10 years on average). This focus on fewer, more focused bets is not exclusive to the biotech vertical but appears more extreme in this pocket of the market. ## The Fintech Radar (28 Apr 2024)

• #1 Pomelo raises \$35m in Series A funding: This week (24th April), US-based payments start-up Pomelo raised \$35m in Series A funding, led by Vy Capital. The funding round brings the total raised amount to \$55m in equity capital and \$125m from their warehouse facility. The raised funds will be allocated towards product and market expansion as the company plans to enter the Mexican market, one of the "largest corridors" for the US. Founded in 2022 in the Philippines, Pomelo allows users to transfer money internationally while simultaneously building their credit score.

• #2 Midas secures \$45m in Series A funding: This week (23rd April), Turkey-based retail investing start-up Midas raised \$45m in a Series A funding round, reflecting the largest Series A investment raised by a Turkish fintech company. The capital raised will be put towards the company's expansion and product launches, particularly cryptocurrency trading, savings accounts, and mutual funds. The funding round was led by Portage, with participation from International Finance Corporation, Spark Capital, Earlybird Digital East Fund, and Revo Capital.

• #3 Payflows raises \$26m for all-in-one finance platform: This week (22nd April), Payflows secured \$26m for all-in-one finance platform. Payflows is an all-in-one finance platform that provides a suite of payments, procurement, cash management and cash collection modules. The platform works as an ERP overlayer to any accounting system.

• #4 Betterment announces agreement to acquire Goldman Sachs' Marcus Invest accounts: This week (23rd April), the US-based independent investment digital advisor, Betterment, reached an agreement to acquire Goldman Sach's digital investing accounts. The accounts, subject to customary closing conditions, will be transferred to Betterment around end of June (c. 29th of June). The transaction will only involve the acquisition of Marcus Invest accounts and AUM - any additional accounts, technology, operations or employees are not included.

• #5 Thunes announces acquisition of Tilia: This week (23rd April), Singaporeanbased cross-border payments company Thunes announced their agreement to acquire Tilia, an "all-in-one" payments platform. The proposed acquisition would allow Thunes to expand their services by offering their network the ability to accept and pay in real-time via 4bn bank accounts and 3bn digital wallets. Additionally, 550 Alternative Payment Methods (APMs) would become available to merchants in the U.S. Tilia's technology is expected to also allow Thunes to offer American Express, MasterCard, Visa, JCB and UnionPay acceptance to their customers. The proposed transaction is subject to regulatory approvals.

Unless otherwise indicated, the news reports referenced above have not been confirmed.

## Real-time Valuation Monitor:

A simple top-down metric to look at is the Refinitiv Venture Capital Index, which provides an aggregated and rebased index of US venture capital-funded firms. The index:

• Leverages Refinitiv private company data of over 22,000 US firms;

• Estimates the value of each company over time by compiling known firm values, estimating missing values and interpolating values between events; and

• Aggregates month-to-month returns to compute a market cap-weighted index tracking the broad US venture capital universe since 1995.

While the log-scale sees a more modest pull-back in valuations of US VC-backed companies, the index as a whole is nonetheless nearly 18% down YoY and 40% off its highs.

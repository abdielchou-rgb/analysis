# Asia EM Equity Mid-Year Outlook | Asia Pacific

# Japan remains preferred versus EM / China

We have reworked our bull / base / bear case targets for our coverage universe as part of the overall MS macro team's midyear outlook process. Japan remains the top pick across our coverage with a Jun 2025 Topix Target of 3,200 (+17%).

Our mid-year outlook process rolls forward our target price horizons to June 2025. We lift our TOPIX target from 2,800 to 3,200 (+17% versus spot). Earnings revisions and EPS growth are likely to remain best in class with our new base case forecast for Topix EPS of ¥185 (+15% in calendar year 2024) followed by two further years of double-digit percentage growth. This is likely to drive ROE to our target of 12% by end 2025. The factors supporting this bullish view include: 1) Higher trend growth in the US (over 20% of revenues for Japan equities); 2) sustained domestic nominal GDP growth; and 3) a weaker profile for the USDJPY than we previously expected (now looking for 140 in 2Q25) and positive leverage to the Multipolar World and AI Thematics. Our base case target P/E multiple remains 15x, driven by further upweighting from foreign investors as well as inflows from Japan's revised Nippon Individual Savings Account (NISA) scheme, which got off to a strong start earlier this year.

We see no upside to our new EM target of 1090 (-1% versus spot) given our below-consensus earnings forecast, valuations, which are now high compared to history, and a forecast profile of a stronger US dollar. Therefore, within the remainder of our coverage we continue to focus on strength in earnings growth over the forecast horizon in India, and on semiconductors and technology hardware in Korea and Taiwan. For India, this is driven by sustained nominal GDP growth in the low teens, and a substantially more stable currency outlook than in the past. While for Korea/ Taiwan Tech, AI integration and a CPU / Memory intensive upgrade cycle are the drivers. This is in contrast to China, where we think consensus' mid-teens EPS growth expectations for this year and next are likely to be disappointed, given our sub-5% nominal GDP growth forecast, and where the forward P/E multiple has recently re-rated back to 10.4x, which is close to the 10-year average. Finally, we also highlight that Mexico, Brazil, ASEAN, and UAE markets look cheap compared to history on a forward P/E basis and also offer some diversification benefits.

## Our new bull / base / bear targets

We have reworked our bull / base / bear case targets for our coverage universe as part of the overall MS macro team's mid-year outlook process. For details, see our report, Global Strategy Mid-Year Outlook, which covers our overall macro assumptions (economics, rates, FX) as well as asset allocation recommendations.

Our base case target price upside is highest for Topix at around 17% versus -1% for EM and -3% for MSCI China. Bull / bear skew also heavily favours Japan versus EM and China, driven by the earnings and valuation components in these risk scenarios. For these reasons we continue to strongly prefer Japan to the rest of our coverage.

In the base case we are around 5% above consensus for Topix EPS over the forecast horizon with December 2025 EPS of ¥205 and ¥225 for December 2026. We maintain our target forward P/E multiple assumption at 15x, which is close to where the market is trading.

While hedging Yen weakness (USDJPY strength) has been a winning strategy year to date, we recommend caution going forward on this front for USD-based investors. This is given the balance of risk towards a lower USD/JPY from recent overbought levels, and in the context of recent intervention by the Japanese authorities. Within Japan, we favour a diversified approach between exporters and domestic stocks with a thematic orientation to productivity and innovation leaders and bottom-up corporate reform beneficiaries

In contrast, for EM in the base case we are below consensus by around 5% at US\$90 for December 2025 and US\$100 for December 2026. Our team forecasts a strong profile for the US dollar going forward. MSCI EM is now trading somewhat above its long-run average multiple at 12.6x forward P/E and we assume that fades to 11.5x to June 2025 (our prior multiple assumption forecast was 11.0x). For MSCI China, we are around 14% below consensus over the forecast horizon, mainly due to our expectation of continued weak nominal GDP growth (and hence revenue growth) and forecast CNY depreciation versus the US dollar.

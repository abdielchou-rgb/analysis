## Valuation Methodology and Risks

## Amazon.com Inc (AMZN.O)

Our \$215 PT implies paying \~23x 2025 FCF (implying a 0.8X multiple on FCF vs. growth, a \~21% discount to tech peers) or '28x EV/EBIT

## Risks to Upside

n Faster than expected AWS rev growth and margin expansion

n 1-day shipping cost transition goes faster than expected

n Amazon succeeds in expanding its TAM (logistics, advertising, grocery, etc)

## Risks to Downside

n Investments step up and continue for longer than expected

n Unit growth slows

n AWS revenue decelerates and /or margins decline

## Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL.O)

Our price target is determined using a discounted cash flow/discount to long-term EBITDA multiple. It implies a \~11X 2025e adjusted EBITDA. Our DCF uses a \~10% WACC and a \~3% terminal growth rate (in line with other growth-oriented internet companies). Our multiple based valuation uses a \~12x EV/EBITDA multiple, \~4% below the 3-year median.

## Risks to Upside

n New products generate higher than expected top line contribution.

n Capital returns through greater share buybacks.

n Hiring and/or spend per headcount is lower than expected.

## Risks to Downside

n High exposure to SMB and travel could pressure ad revenue in a recession

n Improved disclosure around the Google and Other Alphabet segments may not decrease the overall investment activity of the business.

## Meta Platforms Inc (META.O)

Our price target is determined using a discounted cash flow/discount to long-term EBITDA multiple. It implies a \~12.1X 2025e adjusted EBITDA. Our DCF uses an \~11% WACC and a \~2% terminal growth rate.

## Risks to Upside

n Faster than expected Reels monetization & higher engagement growth

n Increased benefit from AI investment

n Revenue driven by subscriptions and/or click-to-message ads

n Further efficiency improvements drive outsized FCF growth

## Risks to Downside

n Macro pressures/weaker consumer spend

n Regulation impacts ability to target ads

n Reality labs losses widen further

n Mis-execution risk could result in higher LT capital intensity

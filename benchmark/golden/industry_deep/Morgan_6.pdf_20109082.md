HarmonyOS NEXT user experience could help Huawei sustain share in China's smartphone market

■ Despite progress in large language models, we think China's foundry capacity is unlikely to be able to fullfil its AI GPU demand given equipment restrictions.

Huawei unveils new Harmony OS, AI model in race for tech self-reliance. Key highlights:

• Huawei launched Harmony OS Next, saying the operating system can be seamlessly adopted in all of its devices including smartphones, PCs, tablets, cars, wearables and enterprise uses.

• Huawei continues to build its AI intelligence foundation, including the computing power of its Ascend chips for AI accelerators, Kunpeng server central processing units, and Kirin chips for device CPUs, as well as its Pangu large language model (LLM).

• The Pangu 5.0 LLM, has four different sizes of training models: the smallest, which can be embedded on smartphones, a midsize model with up to 90 billion parameters, an "ultra" model with up to 230 billion parameters to handle complicated tasks for enterprises, and a "super" model that has a trillion of parameters. Open AI's latest GPT-4 has a parameter count of 1.76 trillion. (Nikkei Asia, 21 June, 2024)

## China Localization Progress

We expect China's semiconductor self-sufficiency ratio to reach 25% by 2026e, up 5ppt from 20% in 2023. China semiconductor self-sufficiency ratio only increased to 20% in 2023, which fell short of our 23% expectation. We attribute this to three reasons:

• Weak consumer and industrial demand, as Chinese semiconductor vendors' exposure to the consumer market is relatively higher, thus the weak consumer and industrial demand as well as the end customer inventory digestion affected revenue growth in 2023.

• Limited breakthrough in logic chips using leading node foundry. China's selfsufficiency remains low in CPU and GPU. Even though Huawei's 5G smartphone is using locally produced chips the capacity is limited.

• Chinese memory vendors face tough restrictions from the US, and they had no presence in high growth areas like high bandwidth memory (HBM) in 2023, thus despite capacity additions, these companies were affected more during the industry down cycle in 2023.

## By segment:

• We are still positive on upstream vendors like equipment suppliers and EDA, they are likely to achieve a higher self-sufficiency ratio, as these are the chokepoints in China's semi localization and local development of these industries has become a strategic imperative for China.

• We are positive on the self-sufficiency ratio of semiconductor components increasing using mature nodes, such as image sensors and power semis, given local foundry capacity expansion

• While we are relatively cautious on logic semis using advanced nodes, like CPUs and GPUs, given US export restrictions.

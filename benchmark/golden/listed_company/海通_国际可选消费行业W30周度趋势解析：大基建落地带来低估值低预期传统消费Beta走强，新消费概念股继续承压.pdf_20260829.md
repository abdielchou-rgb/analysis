## 各板块涨跌幅原因剖析

雅鲁藏浦江下游水电站项目本周开工，由大基建带动经济复苏预期提升，风格继续切换，低估值低预期传统消费 Beta 走强，高估值高预期的新消费概念股承压。本周生活用品板块上涨 7.6%，其中百亚股份上涨 15.5%。国内化妆品板块上涨 2.8%，由伊蚊传播的基孔肯雅热在华南区域蔓延，确诊病例增多，拥有防蚊虫产品的润本和上海家化分别上涨16.9%和5.6%，丸美生物本周上行3.4%，锦波生物本周股价下挫 3.5%，主要因为二季度表现可能低于此前市场预期，巨子生物本周股价下挫 5.7%。国内运动板块上涨 2.3%，强Beat 表现，李宁/特步/晶苑/滔搏分别上涨 2.5%/3.1%/ 5.5%/5.7%，安踏由于传闻收购品牌客群收窄，本周表现较弱。奢侈品板块上涨2.2%，LVMH 本周上涨 4.2%，主要业务二季度业绩公布显示出复苏迹象。本周零食/博彩/海外化妆品板块分别上涨 0.8%/0.7%/0.4%，表现温和。海外化妆品上涨 0.4%，其中逸仙电商下跌 14.9%，618高增长后资金退场有所影响。海外运动板块本周下跌 1.3%，除NIKE 外股价均有下挫，露露柠檬股价新低。宠物板块本周下跌1.4%，其中乖宝宠物下跌 2.5%。黄金珠宝板块本周下跌 1.5%，老铺黄金股价下挫 ，一方面由于上半年毛利率或不及预期带来市场对其净利润预期的下调，另一方面下半年进入店效高基数增速收窄，资金或陆续退场。潮宏基本周上涨 5.7%，7 月店均 GMV 双位数高增，创今年店效增速新高，带动股价强劲上涨。

## 各板块涨跌幅原因剖析

雅鲁藏浦江下游水电站项目本周开工，由大基建带动经济复苏预期提升，风格继续切换，低估值低预期传统消费Beta走强，高估值高预期的新消费概念股承压。本周生活用品板块上涨 7.6%，其中百亚股份上涨 15.5%。国内化妆品板块上涨 2.8%，由伊蚊传播的基孔肯雅热在华南区域蔓延，确诊病例增多，拥有防蚊虫产品的润本和上海家化分别上涨16.9%和5.6%，丸美生物本周上行3.4%，锦波生物本周股价下挫3.5%，主要因为二季度表现可能低于此前市场预期，巨子生物本周股价下挫 5.7%。国内运动板块上涨 2.3%，强 Beat 表现，李宁/特步/晶苑/滔搏分别上涨 2.5%/3.1%/ 5.5%/5.7%，安踏由于传闻收购品牌客群收窄，本周表现较弱。奢侈品板块上涨 2.2%，LVMH 本周上涨4.2%，主要业务二季度业绩公布显示出复苏迹象。本周零食/博彩/海外化妆品板块分别上涨 0.8%/0.7%/0.4%，表现温和。海外化妆品上涨 0.4%，其中逸仙电商下跌14.9%，618 高增长后资金退场有所影响。海外运动板块本周下跌 1.3%，除 NIKE 外股价均有下挫，露露柠檬股价新低。宠物板块本周下跌1.4%，其中乖宝宠物下跌2.5%。黄金珠宝板块本周下跌1.5%，老铺黄金股价下挫9.7%，一方面由于上半年毛利率或不及预期带来市场对其净利润预期的下调，另一方面下半年进入店效高基数增速收窄，资金或陆续退场。潮宏基本周上涨 5.7%，7 月店均 GMV 双位数高增，创今年店效增速新高，带动股价强劲上涨。

Among them, the Gold Jewelry, Domestic Cosmetics, Pet, Daily Necessities, and Snacks sectors outperformed the MSCI China index, while the Overseas Sportswear and Luxury Goods sectors showed negative growth.

## Reasons for Sector Performance

This week, the Daily Necessities sector surged by 7.6%, driven by the start of the Brahmaputra River downstream hydropower station project. The project, part of the broader infrastructure push, has enhanced economic recovery expectations and triggered a market style shift. This has bolstered traditional consumption sectors with low valuation and low expectations, while highvaluation, high-expectation new consumption concept stocks faced pressure. Among the gainers, Baiya Shares rose 15.5%.

The Domestic Cosmetics sector increased by 2.8%. The spread of Chikungunya Fever, transmitted by Aedes mosquitoes, in South China has led to a rise in confirmed cases. Companies with mosquito-repellent products, Runben and Shanghai Jahwa, saw their shares rise by 16.9% and 5.6%, respectively. Marubi Biotech gained 3.4% this week, while Jinbo Biotech fell 3.5%, mainly due to concerns that its second-quarter performance may fall short of previous market expectations. Giant Biogene also saw its stock price drop by 5.7%. The Domestic Sportswear sector rose by 2.3%, with strong performances from Li Ning, Xtep, Crystal International, and Topsports, which increased by 2.5%, 3.1%, 5.5%, and 5.7%, respectively. Anta underperformed this week due to rumors that its brand acquisition may narrow its customer base. The Luxury Goods sector climbed by 2.2%, with LVMH rising 4.2% this week, as its second-quarter business results showed signs of recovery. The Snacks, Gambling, and Overseas Cosmetics sectors saw mild increases of 0.8%, 0.7%, and 0.4%, respectively. Within the Overseas Cosmetics sector, Yatsen fell 14.9%, partly due to profit-taking after its strong performance during the 618 shopping festival. The Overseas Sportswear sector declined by 1.3% this week, with most stocks falling except for NIKE. Lululemon hit a new low in share price. The Pet sector dropped by 1.4%, with Gambol Pet Group falling 2.5%. The Gold Jewelry sector fell by 1.5%. Laopu Gold saw its share price plunge by 9.7%, primarily due to concerns that its first-half gross margin may not meet expectations, leading to a downward revision in market estimates of its net profit. Additionally, the sector faces a high base effect in the second half of the year, which may lead to a slowdown in store efficiency growth and subsequent profit-taking by investors. In contrast, CHJJEWELLERY rose by 5.7% this week, as its July store average GMV increased by double digits, setting a new high for the year and driving its stock price higher.

# Venture Vision: IPO Follow Through

Listings of >\$750m have had the best 1-day returns YTD. However the \$500-750m grouping tends to have the best performance follow-through after day 1.

It has been noted by our strategists in their Mid-Year Outlooks that the current backdrop of M&A and IPO activity recovering from cyclical lows has further to run. According to Marina Zavolock: "Equities markets feel far more mid than late cycle. An M&A recovery from cycle lows for example does not marry with a 'late cycle' market dynamic, neither does a fairly early stage recovery in capital markets activity – both suggest European equities are in the early innings of a rebound". We've looked into this in some depth previously.

The pipeline of companies ready - or needing - to come to market is large (1200 unicorns are competing for private capital -65% from peak, according to Pitchbook). However, those that have come to market in 1H24 are already setting a positive tone for those to follow. The 1-day to 1-week to 1-month follow-through has a correlation approaching 0.8. This is the strongest correlation since the bull market of 2021.

We also note that there is broadly equal likelihood of price action not continuing in the same direction after the first day. If stocks trade up on day 1 there is roughly a 24% chance that one week or one month later the stock in question will be down from its pricing date. Similarly, if a stock is down on day 1 then there is typically a 25% chance of it being up a week later. That may, however, be a misleading rally after a weak issuance because after being down on day 1 there is still only an c18% chance of that stock being higher by the end of month 1, even if it has an intervening positive reversal.

When laying all these issuances out for the US market and just taking the positive day 1 performance stocks, it becomes easier to see that the vast majority of the reversals to negative performance by the end of the first month tend to come from stocks with positive day 1 moves of less than 5%. Any stocks delivering more than a 5% pop on day one are highly unlikely to be lower than issuance a year later (3 out of 38 year to date).

Exhibit 5: Stock-by-stock performance after day one and month one
![](images/86294d27679872720c806e0f4bb30ce1dd5554081ced21a21857bbca2a399724.jpg)

The final question is the degree to which size has an impact on the likelihood of a stock being either (1) positive or negative on day 1; or (2) having a better or worse likelihood of following through to similar performance by the end of week 1 or month 1. If we look at the size of issuance - both on a median or average basis - the stocks most likely to perform positively on day 1 are >\$400m in size and tend to be around double the size of the stocks which tend to perform negatively on day 1.

Breaking down the follow-through of performance into market cap baskets, it tells a slightly different story. While larger companies, predominantly in the \$750m-1bn category tend to have the best 1 day returns, any stock over \$750m tends to have a lower chance of reliable follow-through. By contrast, those stocks in the \$500- 750m grouping tend to have amongst the best 1 day performance and then typically a more than doubling thereafter by the end of the first month. With capital markets still gradually gathering steam, these figures could change rapidly. However, for now, the positive follow-through momentum is as strong as it has been in the last 24-36 months and should bode well for future issuances.

## The Fintech Radar (19 May 2024)

• #1 Mexico-based BNPL provider Aplazo raised \$70m in funding: According to press reports, this week (13th May), Mexico-based Aplazo announced the closing of a \$70 million investment round, of which \$45m in Series B led by QED Investors. There was participation from existing investors Picus Capital, Kaszek, and Oak HC/ FT in addition to Volpe Capital. Aplazo has secured over \$100 million in capital and \$75 million in debt financing since its launch in 2020. Ángel Peña, CEO at Aplazo, said that this new round of investment will help the company further advance its mission to provide financial solutions that are fair, simple and transparent to consumers as well as help merchant partners to boost growth in online and instore sales. Our Latam Financials team is quite bullish on BNPL growth in the region (particularly in Mexico) given the rapid digitalization of payments; strong credit demand from a large, underserved, and young population; and growing ecommerce penetration. For more details on Aplazo and the BNPL landscape in Latam, please see the following reports from our Latam Financials team: The Rise of Latam BNPL, The Upsurge of Latam BNPL & Revisiting Latam BNPL.

• #2 Fintech Farm raises \$32m in Series B: This week (13th May), press reports indicate that UK-based startup Fintech Farm raised \$32m in a Series B funding round led by Nordstar and an extension Series B round led by Bank of Georgia. Fintech Farm is a technology solutions provider that helps medium-sized banks in emerging markets build neobank apps with a focus on providing operational support rather than regulatory / infrastructure services. The additional capital is to support the company's efforts to expand its operations into India given its robust digital payment ecosystem, especially the Unified Payments Interface.

• #3 Corpay signs agreements to purchase Paymerang from Aldrich Capital for \$475m: Last week (8th May) Corpay signed an agreement to acquire Paymerang, and expects it to close in 2Q24 subject to regulatory approval and customary conditions. According to a recent SEC filing, the transaction values the target company at \$475m. Paymerang is an invoice and accounts payable automation platform based in the US. The company previously stated their ability to grow revenue at an annualised rate exceeding 40% since 2018. The acquisition is aimed at allowing Corpay to expand and sell into new vertical markets where Paymerang is strongly positioned, including: healthcare, manufacturing, education and hospitality. The acquisition will also add 250k merchants to its own network. The combined businesses are estimated to process \$120bn in annual spend.

Unless otherwise indicated, the news reports referenced above have not been confirmed.

## Real-time Valuation Monitor:

A simple top-down metric to look at is the Refinitiv Venture Capital Index, which provides an aggregated and rebased index of US venture capital-funded firms. The index:

• Leverages Refinitiv private company data of over 22,000 US firms;

• Estimates the value of each company over time by compiling known firm values, estimating missing values and interpolating values between events; and

• Aggregates month-to-month returns to compute a market cap-weighted index tracking the broad US venture capital universe since 1995.

While the log-scale sees a more modest pull-back in valuations of US VC-backed companies, the index as a whole is nonetheless nearly 18% down YoY and 40% off its highs.
